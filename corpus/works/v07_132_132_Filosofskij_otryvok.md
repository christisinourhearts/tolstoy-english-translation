## **UNPUBLISHED, UNFINISHED, AND INCOMPLETE**

# **[PHILOSOPHICAL FRAGMENT.]**

*6 December 1868.*

Not *cogito ergo sum*, [^1] not that space and time are innate, but *everything moves.* A word expressing something outside movement = 0.—

Geometry is a science because it admits infinite movement without asking what it is or where its end is, but studies only the relations of the movements of points—lines.

Mathematics does not ask about cause, what comes from what, and for that reason reaches results [?].

The aim of philosophy is to discover general laws; for this one must detach oneself from personality. Personality is a point of lines, and their relations only in the movement of personality.

From this point of view: what do people do, what is their aim. The application of one's abilities—movement in the direction given by X. This movement gives a line and their combination and laws.

Wundt's—Hegel's philosophy—the concept. His refutation of the concept—*thought*. What is his refutation of materialism.

## Notes

[^1]: I think, therefore I am,
