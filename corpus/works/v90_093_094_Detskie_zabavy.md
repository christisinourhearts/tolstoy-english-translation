## CHILDREN'S AMUSEMENTS

Written by Count Nikolai Nikolaech Tolstoy, Sergei Nik: Tolstoy, Dmitry Nik: Tolstoy, Lev Nik: Tolstoy.

### First Section

### NATURAL HISTORY

Written by C: L: Ni: To: 1835

**1. The Eagle**

The eagle, king of birds. They say about it: one boy began to tease it, it got angry at him and pecked him to death.

**2. The Falcon**

The falcon is a very useful bird it catches gazelles. A gazelle is an animal that runs very fast, so that dogs cannot catch it; then the falcon comes down and kills it.

**3. The Owl**

The owl is a very strong bird, in sunlight it cannot see. The eagle-owl and the owl are all the same. The eagle-owl differs only by its little horns.

**4. The Parrot**

The parrot is a very beautiful bird it has a crooked or hooked nose they teach it to speak.

<!-- vol. 90, p. 94 -->

**5. The Peacock**

The peacock is also beautiful on it are blue spots the tail is bigger than itself.

**6. The Hummingbird**

The hummingbird is a very small bird it has a golden crest it can be white.

**7. The Cock**

The cock is a beautiful bird its motley tail is bent downward its throat is red blue and all colors and its beard is red. When the turkey cock sings it spreads its tail and its throat swells red black and all colors it has a red beard just like the cock. The turkey cock has a different tail from the cock the turkey cock's tail is spread out.
