## PREFACE TO THE BOOK *LIFE AND SAYINGS OF KRISHNA*

The Hindus believe that Krishna, a god, a manifestation of the highest love, comes to earth once every three hundred million years. He comes in order to teach people to live by love.

The description of Krishna's life and his sayings is for the Hindus a book like the Gospel. It describes Krishna's life on earth. All Krishna's teaching and all his life are only love. Krishna knows nothing but love, gives and accepts nothing but love, acts only by love, breathes only it, and speaks only it. It is this appearance of Krishna on earth and his deeds during his appearance that are described in the accompanying book.

The writer of this book, addressing the reader, says: “Let everyone read these lines with an open soul, and the divine drink of love will flow into his soul through his reason.”
