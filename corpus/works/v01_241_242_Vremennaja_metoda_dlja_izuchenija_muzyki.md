## [\*FRAGMENT I.] A TEMPORARY METHOD FOR THE STUDY OF MUSIC.

Music is the expression of the relation of sounds to one another in space and time and force; consequently, knowledge of music consists in knowledge of the way of expressing sounds in space and time, i.e. knowledge of the expression of intervals between sounds in space and in time and in force. — For this one must read more music by good composers, with the greatest attention, trying at once to remember and reproduce it, and listen, trying to write it down. — But in order to reproduce certain sounds on any instrument, one must have a certain habit in the fingers, which is acquired by nothing other than frequent repetition of the passage that seems difficult. And in order to express it, one must have certain knowledge in relation to what in music is called composition, which consists in the succession of sounds in relation to time, space, and force. — Music is expressed for us in three ways, namely: on paper, on an instrument, and for our ear; consequently, in order to know music, one must know all these three ways of expression and be able to convey what is expressed in one way by another, without losing sight of space, time, and force, namely.

*1) To transfer from writing to an instrument.* For this a special habit and dexterity in the hands is needed, acquired by frequent repetition of this kind of exercise and especially of difficulties. *2) To hear from writing.* This knowledge is acquired by reading music without an instrument and then checking. — *3) To write from playing.* This knowledge is acquired by the habit of distinguishing intervals and quickly writing notes by ear. — *4) To hear from playing.* Anyone can do this. *5) To play by ear.* This is acquired <!-- vol. 1, p. 242 --> by patiently finding known melodies on an instrument: after playing[^1] unfamiliar notes a couple of times, try to play them from memory. *6) To write by ear.* For this one must do the same thing as for 5), only write instead of play. —

## Notes

[^1]: Written: *to play*.
