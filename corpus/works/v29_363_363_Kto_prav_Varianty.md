## WHO IS RIGHT?

### No. 1

As always happens after the departure of constraining guests, and the prince was such a guest, [there came] an unusual animation. It was as though a person had been kept locked up and suddenly released.

Especially tasty appetizers had been prepared for dinner... On the table with its cleanest tablecloth stood bottles of several kinds of wine; there were even bouquets on the table. Flowers still remained—mignonette, sweet peas, asters. The dinner was especially good, and Vladimir Ivanovich laughed that he would invite the prince every day so that they could eat so well every day. Among the children, however, the animation, already great, reached its furthest limits.

Before dinner Vera had run around and laughed so much with all the children and even with the Swiss governess, whom she had drawn into it, that her mother had to quiet her, remarking that the noise was unpleasant for her sister.

### No. 2

On the very first day after the hunt, Vera with Sasha and all the boys, under Semyon Ivanovich's direction, set off after tea on their rounds.

They went in a carriage in which they were to ride as far as the village of Lyskovo and from there divide up: Semyon Ivanovich with the older boy and Natasha were to go to Durnovka and Chekalino, while Vera with the two younger ones was to go round all the huts, omitting those known to be prosperous, examine their situation, and record under columns prepared in advance: 1) number of mouths to feed, 2) number of allotments, 3) horses, cows, sheep, pigs, 4) what grain there was, 5) special notes, in which special circumstances, whether disastrous or advantageous, were to be indicated.
