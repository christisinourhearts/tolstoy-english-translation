## V. \* FRAGMENT FROM THE DIARY OF STAFF CAPTAIN A. OF THE L. L. INFANTRY REGIMENT.

As long as a month and a half ago Fyodor Karlych told me that the Grand Duke would come here in Gorchakov's place and that the Sovereign was in Nikolaev; then they said it was nonsense, then again they assured us that his horses were already in Simferopol, and again it turned out to be nonsense. So I involuntarily assigned this news to the category of our army-hospital news—like the reports that Napoleon had been killed, that Victoria had personally assumed command of the army, that Grand Duke Konstantin was coming with the American fleet to rescue us—and stopped thinking about it. — But yesterday the quartermaster, having arrived from Simferopol, said for certain that the Sovereign would be here. — He said that in Simferopol...
