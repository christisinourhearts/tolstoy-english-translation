## **UNPUBLISHED, UNFINISHED** **AND INCOMPLETE**

## I. * FIRST PLAN OF THE NOVEL *FOUR EPOCHS OF DEVELOPMENT*.

I fall asleep.

2nd day 2 weeks after arrival, grandmother’s name day, (Valakhin[s], passionate love, din[ner]) outing, (with children) confectioner’s], dinner, Valakhin[s], dancing, theater.—Conversation with brother about the day spent.—Drunken Karl Ivanych. Childhood.—(Mother’s death.)—

## Boyhood.

First day. We are again in Moscow with sister (we have a new tutor. Grandmother is very distressed.) I am 15, (classes) brother 16. Morning. He goes to take an examination, conversation with him. My classes. Returns with D[mitry], my love for him, strength.—Dinner, his conversations with K[atenka] and (he) leaves with D[mitry], my dépit,[^1] symmetries, I buy gingerbread. My escapade with the maid, the next day it becomes known, I am punished. Another day—Sunday. (Walk with father and brother, meeting with K[arl] I[vanych]. I eavesdrop. K[arl] I[vanych] comes I persuade father, grandmother dies.)

*2nd day. In the country.* My walk to the neighbors, meeting with father, I learn that father is getting married.—Brother’s story.—Conf[ession] 2.—

## Youth.

1st day. I am 18. I ask father for money, stepmother annoys [me?], I go to the examination meet brother on horseback, dinner,—despair after dinner, walks past the palace, tavern, story. Father calls me to him in the evening, scolding. I ask to go to the country (Moscow) morning in the tavern alone, go around the shops, <!-- vol. 2, p. 242 --> go to the Valakhins, dine, get embarrassed, fall in love, no money.—After dinner the old tutor and old comrades. The old governess drinks tea at my place, evening visit to the prince. D[mitry] arrives, rescues me, conversation with him and story about sister. He is a bridegroom.

Enthusiastic plans. I decide (to be a scholar, writer) to go with D[mitry].

## Young Manhood.

I am 23. I am an official, go on leave, stop by D[mitry] before father, tell him about father, brother and myself, love his wife.—Estate management, I go alone [?] and reason.

Main thought: The feeling of love for God and one’s neighbors is strong in childhood; in boyhood these feelings are stifled by sensuality, self-confidence, and vanity; in youth by pride and an inclination to reasoning; in young manhood the experience of life revives these feelings.

Boyhood.—1. St. Jérôme. 2nd I cast a glance at my life and my situation, 1) about classes, 2) about Lyubochka, Sonechka and [1 *illegible*], 3) about father and money, comparison with mother.

~~3rd Russian teacher, strength.~~

In Young Manhood I become attached to estate management, and papa after many discussions gives me maman’s estate [?] to manage. The manager’s gossip.—
