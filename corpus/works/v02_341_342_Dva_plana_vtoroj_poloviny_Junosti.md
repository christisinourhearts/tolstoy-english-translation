## **UNPUBLISHED, UNFINISHED** **AND INCOMPLETE**

## IX. \*\* TWO PLANS FOR THE “SECOND HALF” OF *YOUTH*.

*1st page:*

1st vacation. Father marries. I read novels and study music (idyllic dreams). Volodya hunts and is bored and judges Father. Mimi and Katenka intrigue. Lyubochka submissive. Stepmother urges going to Moscow. — 1st winter. I read, study music (aristocrat), frequent relations with the Nekhlyudov family, friendship with his sister. Volodya goes out into society. Stepmother too. Father ~~loses a great deal~~ too. Mimi wants to leave; they reconcile her. — I am proud and I fail. 2nd vacation. Volodya chases after Katenka and wants to marry. I chase after the neighboring girls. *Coq du village*.[^1] Stepmother is jealous, Father—his character deteriorates. Lyubochka—meekness. Nekhlyudov arrives; she begins to please him.

*2nd winter.* Lyubochka marries Obolensky, Ivin, Father has children, Father loses at cards, we separate. I keep company with musicians. I am a liberal and study decently, advance. Volodya with Katenka ~~frie~~ love ~~Mimi quarrels and leaves for Sister's. —~~

*3rd Vacation.* Volodya has cooled, goes to Khabarovka, liberal. I after a girl, and philosophy seriously. Petrovskoye is sold to Sister. My summer poetic, with musicians. — Papa goes to his wife's estate.

*3rd Winter.* We are alone. Volodya is already sensible, practical, disillusioned. I am a philosopher. Papa in the country. Mimi with her, and Katenka dies. Sister in Petersburg. I am ill and leave the university, unite philosophy with practice. I go to Khabarovka to manage the estate. —

*3rd page:*

General plan. I make a thousand different plans in youth, nothing succeeds, at age 24 I go to the Caucasus to serve. This is the beginning of young manhood. —

<!-- vol. 2, p. 342 --> 1st year of university vacation. Father marries, Volodya ~~chases~~ hunts, I read books and philosophize. In Moscow Father goes out into society, Volodya too, I ~~keep company and~~ begin to study music and idle, fail the examination. 2nd year of vacation. Father is bored. Volodya chases after Katenka, and Mimi leaves us. Aunt appears. I ~~hunt~~ make music and hunt. — In Moscow Father loses an awful lot. Volodya goes out into society, Lyubochka ~~too~~ marries, I work ~~and decide~~, but begin to think that everything is wrong. — 3rd year. There are no vacations for me because I am ill; after recovering, I plunge into revelry and gambling and fail the examination. Volodya, on the contrary, works, occasionally goes out. In the middle of winter Father, having gambled away his money, goes to the country. His property is separated from ours. All the while the beneficent influence of the Nekhlyudov family on me. 4th year. Prince Ivan Ivanovich buys Petrovskoye in Lyubochka's name. I live with Sister. Father lives in his wife's little village. I go to Khabarovka to manage the estate, obtain a rank, do stupid things, live there through the winter in gloomy despair. Volodya lives through the winter in Petersburg, in the Guards. 5th year. In summer I live with Sister, in winter with her in Moscow in a long frock coat. Buy a machine and become a member of the Agricultural Society. 6th year. In spring in Petersburg take the examination, Volodya—society, in winter in Moscow with success in society, this is the *coup de grace*,[^2] I flee to the Caucasus. In youth 4 phases: 1) romantic dreams, plans, reading novels, bad music, 2) musical, artistic and liberal [*2 illegible*] in Rousseau, 3) scholarly, philosophical (especially during illness), 4) Revelry, gambling, oblivion of everything, 5) Pennilessness and passionate estate management. ~~6)~~ Ends in calm and a desire to live out one's days, 6) social.

*4th page:*

*Youth* with respect to the 2nd half.[^3]
