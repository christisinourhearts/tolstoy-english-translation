## \*II. [CONGRATULATORY VERSES.]

January 12, 1840

To Dear Auntie

The wished-for happy day has come,  
And I can prove to you  
That I am not the silent child  
I was when Mother caressed me. —  
Now I understand clearly,  
I know all that you have done;  
For us you sacrificed yourself,  
Your kind heart and your soul. —  
Now I understand the happiness  
This day has given me.  
With all my heart I wish  
That God bless you for all your labors. —  
Perhaps now, once again,  
Fortune will look in on us again,  
The merriment of former days will come,  
And we shall live happily.  
As a pledge of happy days  
I welcome this day with delight;  
I wish the stream of your life  
To be clearer and brighter. —

Leo Tolstoy.
