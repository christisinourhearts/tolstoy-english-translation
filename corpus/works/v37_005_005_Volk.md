## THE WOLF

There was once a boy. And he loved eating chickens very much and was very afraid of wolves.

And once this boy lay down to sleep and fell asleep. And in his sleep he saw that he was walking alone through the woods to gather mushrooms, and suddenly a wolf jumped out of the bushes and rushed at the boy.

The boy was frightened and cried: “Ah, ah! He's going to eat me!”

The wolf says: “Wait, I won't eat you; I'll talk with you.”

And the wolf began speaking in a human voice.

And the wolf says: “You're afraid I'll eat you. But what do you do yourself? Do you like chickens?”

“I do.”

“Then why do you eat them? Those chickens are just as alive as you are. Every morning—go and look at how they catch them, how the cook carries them to the kitchen, how their throats are cut, how their mother clucks because her chickens are being taken away from her. Have you seen that?” says the wolf.

The boy says: “I haven't seen it.”

“If you haven't seen it, then go and look. And now I'll eat you.

“You're just such a little chicken—so I'll eat you.”

And the wolf rushed at the boy, and the boy was frightened and cried: “Ah, ah, ah!” He cried out and woke up.

And from then on the boy stopped eating meat—he ate neither beef, nor veal, nor mutton, nor chicken.

Leo Tolstoy.
