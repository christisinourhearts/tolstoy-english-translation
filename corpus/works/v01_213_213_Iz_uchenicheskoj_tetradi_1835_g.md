## I. [FROM A SCHOOL EXERCISE BOOK, 1835]

C L: Ni: To.

## 1. The Eagle

The eagle, king of birds. They say about it that one boy began to tease it, but it got angry at him and pecked him to death.

## 2. The Falcon

The falcon is a very useful bird it catches gazelles. A gazelle is an animal that runs very fast, so that dogs cannot catch it; then the falcon comes down and kills it.
