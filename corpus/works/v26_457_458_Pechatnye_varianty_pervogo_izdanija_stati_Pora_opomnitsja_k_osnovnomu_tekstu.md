### PRINTED VARIANTS OF THE FIRST EDITION OF THE ARTICLE “IT IS TIME TO UNDERSTAND!” AGAINST THE MAIN TEXT

Page 443, line 5. *Instead of:* and, what is most terrible of all, ruins the soul — *in the first edition:* ruins the soul.

Line 11. *Instead of:* encourage them — encourage him.

Line 28. *Instead of:* when many doctors — when all doctors.

Page 444, line 2. *Instead of:* harmful — always harmful.

Line 5. *Instead of:* will do much less work — will do much less work; therefore she also has less strength.

Line 9. *Instead of:* only for a short time, but in the long run — only in the short run, and in the long run.

Line 15. *Instead of:* discuss the matter — discuss the matter; and therefore, if one is going to drink at all, it is better at an ordinary time.

Line 21. *Instead of:* for a short time — in the short run.

Line 31. *Instead of:* so many people — everyone.

Line 32. *Instead of:* “Everyone drinks....” — There is one answer to this: “Everyone drinks....”

Line 33. *Instead of:* to this many — to this people.

Line 34. *Instead of:* these people actually imagine that everyone around them drinks — they actually think that everyone drinks.

<!-- vol. 26, p. 458 -->

Page 445, line 3. *Instead of:* If they came to blows — But what if they came to blows.
