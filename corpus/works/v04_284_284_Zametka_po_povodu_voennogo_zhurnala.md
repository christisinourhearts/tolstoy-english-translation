## II. \* [NOTE CONCERNING THE MILITARY JOURNAL.]

Draw up a more detailed program — and a form letter for inviting contributors. —

Draw up 2 lists of contributors.

1) Contributors whose articles can be ready by the new year, and

2) contributors who will assist in the future. —

Send programs and letters *to all contributors.* —

Write letters to Petersburg to persons who may have influence on the success of the Journal project, and ask persons taking part in it to do the same.—

If certain articles in the sample sheet are changed, turn to Kovalevsky for advice and change Tolstov's article [to] Rostovtsev's.

At the same time try to have the 2nd sample sheet printed. —

The funds are with Fride. In case they are insufficient, by common effort. —

Contributors of the 1st class.

Cantacuzene

Kovalevsky

Korzhanovsky

To the commander of the 3rd Sapper battalion, Nik. Konst.

Zatsepin

Redinger.

Ushakov.

And all those to whom courtesy requires it.

Contributors of the 2nd class. —

Regimental and battery commanders of the Southern Army.

To the commander of the 4th Battery Battery of the 20th Artillery Brigade, Colonel Alekseev. In Kizlyar. —
