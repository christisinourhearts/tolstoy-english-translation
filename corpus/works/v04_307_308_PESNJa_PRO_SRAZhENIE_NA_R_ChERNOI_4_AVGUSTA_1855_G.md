## VIII. SONG ABOUT THE BATTLE ON THE CHERNAYA RIVER, 4 AUGUST 1855

[Composite text]

On the fourth day of the month  
The devil drove us off  
To take the heights (bis).

General Baron Vrevsky  
Kept after Gorchakov  
When he was tipsy (bis).

“Prince, you take those heights;  
Don't get into a quarrel with me,  
Or I'll report you” (bis).

All the great epaulettes  
Gathered for their councils,  
Even Platz-bek-Kok (bis).

Police-master Platz-bek-Kok  
Could not for the life of him think up  
What he ought to say (bis).

Long they thought and guessed,  
The topographers kept writing  
On a great big sheet (bis).

It all went smoothly on the paper,  
But they forgot about the ravines,  
And we had to walk through them... (bis).

Princes and counts rode out,  
And behind them the topographers,  
To the Great Redoubt (bis).

<!-- vol. 4, p. 308 -->

The Prince said: “Go, Liprandi.”  
And Liprandi: “No, sir, *attendez*,  
No, I won't go” (bis).

“No clever man is needed there;  
You send Read there instead,  
And I'll have a look”... (bis).

Then Read, just like that,  
Led us straight toward the bridge:  
“Come on, to the hurrah!” (bis).

Weymarn wept and begged him  
To wait a little while.  
“No, let them go now” (bis).

But General Ushakov,  
He was not like that at all:  
He kept waiting for something (bis).

He waited and kept waiting  
Until he gathered up his courage  
To cross the river (bis).

We raised a shout of hurrah,  
But the reserves did not arrive;  
Someone got it wrong (bis).

And General Belevtsov  
Did nothing but shake the standard,  
Quite unbecomingly (bis).

To the Fedyukhin Heights  
Only three companies of us arrived,  
Though regiments had set out!... (bis).

Our army was not large,  
The French were three times as many,  
And *secours* without end (bis).

We waited for a column  
To come out from the garrison to help us;  
The signal was given (bis).

But there General Saken  
Kept reading akathists  
To the Mother of God (bis).

And so we had to retreat,  
Ras... their mother,  
Whoever led us there (bis).
