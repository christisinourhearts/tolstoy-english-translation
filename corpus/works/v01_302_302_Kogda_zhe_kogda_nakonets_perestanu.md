<!-- vol. 1, p. 302 -->

V.

When, when at last shall I cease  
To spend my life without purpose or passion,  
And feel a deep wound in my heart  
And know no means of healing it?

Who made that wound, God alone knows,  
But from birth I have been tormented  
By the bitter pledge of coming insignificance,  
By tormenting sadness and doubts.
