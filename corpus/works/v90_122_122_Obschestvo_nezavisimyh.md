## SOCIETY OF INDEPENDENT PEOPLE

The aims of the society are: 1) to bring independent people closer together; 2) to help one another with the aim of preserving independence; and 3) to assist all Russian people in freeing themselves from dependence.

Every Russian person who receives nothing from the government—neither ranks, nor crosses, nor money—is a member of the society if he wishes to be.

Every member of the society who receives crosses, ranks, or money from the government is excluded from the society.

The activity of a member:

1. Unless there is a special reason not to, to live in the place of one's birth, or where one has property.
2. To engage in work not paid for by the government (except front-line military service).
3. To oppose luxury and set an example of simplicity in life.
4. Not to refuse whatever help is possible to a member of the society.
5. To maintain purity of conduct in life (debauched people and drunkards cannot be members).
6. To try to increase the number of members.
7. Not to donate one's money to anyone or anywhere except for the purposes of the society.
8. Never to gather more than 10 members, whether for deliberations, demonstrations, or dinners.
9. Not to introduce into the society any aims other than the support and spread of independent people.
10. The introduction of any political aim into the society's activity is forbidden, and any effort toward any action opposed to the government thereby excludes the member or members concerned from the society.
11. All members of the society know one another from the list of members, which each member has.
12. All members treat one another as equals and address one another with the familiar *ty*.
