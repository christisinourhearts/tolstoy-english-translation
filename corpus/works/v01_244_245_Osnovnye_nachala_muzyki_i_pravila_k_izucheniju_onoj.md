## [**FRAGMENT III.] FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLES OF MUSIC AND RULES FOR ITS STUDY.

1850 [year], 14 June.

Definition of music.

Music is an aggregate of sounds in a twofold relation: 1) in relation [to] space, 2) in relation [to] time. By the space between two or several sounds I mean the degree of speed of vibration of the air from some cause of its division. The cause of the concept of space is movement, and movement necessarily requires the concept of some points or moments. In musical space these two moments are the upper and lower notes, octaves, etc.—from *do* to *do* and from *re* to *re*. Every person can imagine space unlimited; therefore a person can imagine an unlimited number of octaves. In actuality, however, this space is limited in different ways: partly by the very construction of the human ear, partly by the construction of the instrument; thus, for example, a flute has five octaves; a violin and cello from 6 to 9; a piano, depending on the time of its construction, from 4 to 7.

By the time between two or several sounds I mean the measure of their duration....... Just as the concept of space requires the concept of movement from one moment to another, so the concept of time requires the concept of these two moments.

In musical relation these two moments will be the beginning and end of some musical thought. As with space, a person can imagine time unlimited. In actuality, however, it is limited partly by the property of fantasy and by the construction of the instrument. Thus, for example: on instruments from which sound is drawn by means of a bow, and on wind instruments, the duration of the sound depends on the player. On instruments from which sound is drawn through touching the strings with a finger or some other implement, such as the piano, gusli, cimbalom, etc., the sound depends not on the player but on the quality of the instrument.

Definition of music.

Music is an aggregate of sounds that strike our faculty of hearing in a threefold relation: 1) in relation to space, 2) in relation to time, 3) in relation to force.

17 December. The word music has a threefold meaning: 1) it denotes a fact, and in this case its definition is the preceding one, 2) music is a science, and in this case it is the knowledge of the laws by which sounds are combined in a threefold relation, 3) music is an art, by means of which sounds are combined in these three relations. There is a fourth meaning of music—the poetic meaning. Music in this sense is a means of arousing certain feelings through sound or transmitting them.

Musical Analysis.

For the true study of music I find it necessary for the student, after learning the first rules of music, that is, notes and intervals, tempo and accentuation, to analyze music, that is, according to what part of music one has gone through, to examine a musical composition in relation to the rules of the science; for one who has gone through the full course of the science of music, to analyze music as follows:[^1]

The highest degree of analysis: 1) in relation to space, the course of the combination of sounds, that is, of chords, is examined, observing the rules and divisions adopted for the chord.

In relation to time, the relation of sounds to one another in time is examined. No rules exist.

In relation to force, the relation of sounds to one another in force is examined. No rules exist.

3) The highest degree of analysis is that in which all transitions from one tonic to another, from one tempo to another, from one accentuation to another are examined. For each kind of Analysis I invented an expression not in words but in signs, which for those practicing will be a translation, so that in order to translate, one will need to understand what to translate.—

## Notes

[^1]: In this analysis one need only examine the motif, and if several motifs are joined, each separately.
