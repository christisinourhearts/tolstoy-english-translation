## **THE CALF ON THE ICE.**

A calf jumped around the pen and learned to make circles and turns. When winter came, the calf was let out with the other livestock onto the ice to the watering place. All the cows carefully approached the trough, but the calf took a run onto the ice, curled his tail, laid back his ears, and began circling. On the very first circle his leg slid out from under him, and he struck his head against the trough.

He bellowed:

“Poor me! I jumped knee-deep in straw—I didn't fall, and here on the smooth surface I slipped.”

The old cow said.

“If you weren't a calf, you would know that where it is easier to jump, it is harder to keep your footing”
