## **THE WOLF AND THE LAMB.**

A wolf saw—a lamb was drinking at the river.

The wolf wanted to eat the lamb and began finding fault with him. “You,” he says, “are muddying my water and not letting me drink.”

<!-- vol. 22, p. 563 -->

— 3 —

The lamb says: “Ah, wolf, how can I muddy your water? I'm standing downstream from you, and even then I drink only with the tips of my lips.” And the wolf says: “Well then, why did you insult my father last summer?” The lamb says: “But, wolf, I wasn't even born last summer.” The wolf grew angry and says: “There's no outarguing you. Well, I'm on an empty stomach; so I'll eat you for that.”
