<!-- vol. 21, p. 31 -->

Grandfather grew old. Once he was climbing onto the stove and could not. His grandson was in the hut. He found it funny. Shame on you, grandson. What is bad is not that grandfather is old and weak, but that the grandson is young and foolish.
