## \*\* &lt;STORY

Yesterday I was in town. I traveled by railway. The engine went fast. ~~The jackdaws could not keep up flying behind us.~~ In the middle of the journey the engine driver whistled loudly. I leaned out of the carriage and began <!-- vol. 21, p. 343 --> to look. On the track[^1] sat two boys.[^2] They heard the whistle, jumped up from the track, and ran away. Then we passed. And the engine driver shook his fist at them and said: I almost ran you over ~~along with your mushrooms.~~

## Notes

[^1]: : workmen had loaded a cart full of sand and could not move it.
[^2]: they had gone into the woods, gathered mushrooms, and fallen asleep.
