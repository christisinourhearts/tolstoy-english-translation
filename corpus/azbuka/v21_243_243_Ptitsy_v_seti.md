## THE BIRDS AND THE NETS

*(A Fable)*

A hunter set nets by a lake and caught many birds. The birds were large; they lifted the net and flew away with it. The hunter ran after the birds. A peasant saw the hunter running and says: “Where are you running? Can you catch a bird on foot?” The hunter said: “If it were one bird, I wouldn't catch it, but now I will.”

And so it happened. When evening came, the birds began pulling toward their roosts, each in its own direction: one toward the forest, another toward the swamp, a third into the field; and all of them fell to the ground with the net, and the hunter took them.
