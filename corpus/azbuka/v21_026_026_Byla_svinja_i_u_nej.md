<!-- vol. 21, p. 26 -->

There was a sow, and she had fifteen little white piglets. All were obedient; only one spotted piglet disobeyed his mother. His mother told him not to go away from her, but he kept straying off. Once the disobedient piglet ran far away. The sow got angry with him and says: “I will go farther away; he will get frightened and from now on will not disobey.” As soon as she had gone away, she heard the piglet squealing behind her, looking for her. She felt sorry for the piglet; she turned back and wanted to take him with her. Suddenly she heard him squeal. The sow ran to the place where the piglet had been, but he was already gone; only the ground was splashed with blood, and wolf tracks could be seen as far as the dust.
