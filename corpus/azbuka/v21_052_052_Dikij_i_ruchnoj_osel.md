## THE WILD AND TAME DONKEY

*(A Fable)*

A wild donkey saw a tame donkey, went up to him, and began praising his life: how sleek his body was and how sweet his food. Then the tame donkey was loaded up, and the driver began urging him on from behind with a club. The wild donkey said: “No, brother, I don't envy you now—I see that you pay dearly for your good life.”
