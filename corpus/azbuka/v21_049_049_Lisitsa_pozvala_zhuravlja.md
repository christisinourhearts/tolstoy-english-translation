<!-- vol. 21, p. 49 -->

The fox invited the crane to dinner and served soup on a plate. The crane could not take any with his long beak, and the fox ate it all herself. The next day the crane invited the fox to his place and served dinner in a jug with a narrow neck. The fox could not get her muzzle into the jug, while the crane put in his long neck and drank it all by himself.
