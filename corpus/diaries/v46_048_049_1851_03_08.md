8 March.

[^1] Again I took a long time to come to myself; nevertheless, I overcame it. — Wrote a letter to Nikolenka (thoughtl[essly] and hastily). — To the office — in the same stupid form I have adopted (self-deception). Did gymnastics without proper judgment, i.e. with too little regard for my own strength; this weakness I shall call in general: presumption, a departure from reality. — Looked at myself often in the mirror. This is stupid physical self-love, from which nothing but what is bad and ridiculous can come. With Poiret I was embarrassed again (self-deception). At the horse-breeding office I acted weakly, bowed first to Golitsyn[^2] and did not go straight where I needed to go. — Distraction. — At gymnastics I boasted (self-praise). I wanted Kobylin to have a true opinion of me (petty vanity). Ate far too much at dinner (gluttony). Went to Volkonsky without finishing the business (lack of consistency). Ate my fill of sweets, stayed too long. — Lied. — Occupations for the 9th. From 8—10 an account of debts. Letters: Auntie and Fersen. 10 to 11 — gymnastics. Music from 11 to 12. From 12 to 3 to Panin, the Perfilievs, Beer, Anikeeva, Begichev, and the businessman. About money. — <!-- vol. 46, p. 49 --> Dine at the Gorchakovs’, about the position. — At home read and write whatever comes into my head, an outline of what I have read, or even make excerpts. — Compile a journal for weaknesses. (Franklin-style).
