28 February.

I have let much time slip. At first I was carried away by social pleasures, then once again there was emptiness in my soul; and I fell behind in my studies, that is, studies whose object is my own personality. — For a long time I was tormented by the fact that I have not a single heartfelt thought or feeling that would <!-- vol. 46, p. 46 --> determine the whole direction of life — everything is just as it happens; but now it seems to me that I have found a heartfelt idea and a permanent aim: the development of the will, an aim toward which I have long been striving, but which I have only now recognized not simply as an idea, but as an idea that has become akin to my soul. —

Program for tomorrow. Get up at 9 o’clock. — Study the Encyclopedia and write an outline. Go to the funeral, then to gymnastics, dine, and from 6 to 12 study alone or with Koloshin. Do not smoke. — Remember that carrying out what has been planned constitutes all the happiness of my life, and vice versa. —
