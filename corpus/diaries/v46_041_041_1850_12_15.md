<!-- vol. 46, p. 41 -->

15 December.

I am very dissatisfied with yesterday. First, that I did nothing concerning the Guardianship Council; second, that I wrote nothing; and third, that I began to weaken in my convictions and began to yield to the influence of people.—Get up very early, read a little in the morning, then occupy myself with the diary, writing, and letters; at 12 o'clock go to the Council, to Evreinov, to Kryukov, to Anikeeva, and to Lvov; dine at home and write some more; then to the Theater and again work at home.—

Rules for S[ociety]. Do not change the form of address, but always address people in one and the same manner.—

Let not the slightest unpleasantness or barb from anyone pass without repaying it twofold.
