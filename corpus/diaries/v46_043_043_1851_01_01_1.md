<ins>1851. 1 January. Ya. P.</ins>

On 1 January 1851 I was at Pokrovskoye, saw Nikolenka; he has not changed, while I have changed very much and could have had an influence on him if he were not so strange; either he notices nothing and does not love me, or he tries to act as though he notices nothing and does not love me. —
