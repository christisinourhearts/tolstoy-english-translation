<ins>8 September.</ins>

Missed one day. 8 Sept. 94. Y. P. evening. Yesterday wrote very little, but thought a great deal. Started everything from the beginning. After breakfast chopped with Davydov, read in the evening. Masha arrived. Everything is good, but she is boring. This morning did nothing but read and did not sit down at the table; rode horseback with the girls to Oveyannikovo; in the morning wrote an agreement with the peasants—somehow. I slept little and therefore wrote nothing. In the evening thought well and clearly that one can live badly only through lusts, and well only through one thing: kindness, the desire, the effort to be kinder and kinder.

Thought also: that only people with strong, ideal aspirations can fall morally low. Only a bird with wings can plunge headlong down from a tree or roof. Consciousness of the power of spiritual ascent—wings.
