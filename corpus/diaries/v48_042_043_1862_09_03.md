<!-- vol. 48, p. 42 -->

3 September.

At their place, at first nothing, then a walk. “He is ugly, you are healthy,” a lorgnette, “please come.” I am calm! As I rode, I thought: either everything is accidental, or she feels with extraordinary subtlety, or it is the most vulgar coquetry—one man today, another tomorrow and, above all, what use is a man who is leaving—or it is accidental, subtle, and coquettish all at once. But in general, nothing, nothing, silence. — Never had the future with a wife presented itself to me so clearly, joyfully, and calmly. Evening at the Perfilievs’. Boring old people. I know, Vasyuk, I know your sins. How vulgar is the quiet deceiving of one another—keeping accounts. And perhaps that is my fate too. — Memento,[^1]

<!-- vol. 48, p. 43 --> Dublitskoy, old devil, Uncle Lyavon. But you feel: “mein schönes Herz.”[^2] — The main thing, it seems, is that it would be so simple, just right, no passion, no fear, not a second of remorse. —

## Notes

[^1]: *Remember,*
[^2]: *my beautiful heart.*
