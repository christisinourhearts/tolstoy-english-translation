10 March.

Did not get up for a long time. — Spoke badly with Ozerov and pressed the horse on him. Base[ness]. Poiret. Deception and haste. Lied to Begichev that I know the Siberian Gorchakovs. Did not take a fur coat, haste and groundl[essness]. At the Council cowardice. At gymnastics vanity. At Lvov’s self-confidence and affectation. Made no excerpts, lazy. I write the journal hastily and indistinctly. — Occupations for 11 March. Arrangements concerning the warm shirt and horses, diary, Franklin journal, and write until 10; from 10 to 11 gymnastics, from 11 to 12 music. — Walk and dine until 6. From 6 excerpts and read. — —
