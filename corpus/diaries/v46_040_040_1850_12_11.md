Tasks for today. 11. Stay home, read, in the evening write rules for society and an outline of the tale. Tasks for 8 December. In the morning read, then before dinner the diary and a schedule of Sunday errands and visits. After dinner read and bathe, in the evening, if I am not very tired, the tale. — In the morning, immediately after coffee, letters to the office, Auntie, and Perfiliev.
