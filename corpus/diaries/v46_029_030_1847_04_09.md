<!-- vol. 46, p. 29 -->

9 April

(6 in the morning). I am completely satisfied with myself for yesterday. I am beginning to acquire bodily will; but <!-- vol. 46, p. 30 --> the mental is still very weak.—Patience and diligence, and I am sure that I will achieve everything I want.—
