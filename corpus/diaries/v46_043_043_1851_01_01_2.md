2 January. The christening: stay with my family, go to the Dyakovs’, and at night[^1] to Tula. Conclude the agreement there with Shcherbatov, return on the night of the 3rd to Yasnaya, stay until evening, and on the night of the 4th go to Moscow. In Tula leave the power of attorney and petition, be at the Chairman’s.
