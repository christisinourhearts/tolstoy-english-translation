22. 89. M.

<!-- vol. 50, p. 40 --> Got up later. Worked a great deal. Lay down, then wrote a letter to Semenov and to Rod, and made corrections to the preface, then[^1] [?] Zheltov came, and I went around the bookshops with him. Took Robertson from Gautier's, and *The Teaching of the Twelve Apostles* from Marakuev's. What a pure man Zheltov is! At home read, talked with Lyova. No one was there. Thought: Wisdom, knowledge, among us has split into two: theology, in which nothing can be changed and which settles all questions about life, everything that needs to be known. What is this world? What am I? What for? What will be? What is to be done? All this has been settled by theology, and it must not be touched. Philosophy, on the other hand, can speak about everything, only not about this, only not about what is necessary and important. Received a letter from Chertkov.

## Notes

[^1]: May be read: *lit the stove*.
