<!-- vol. 46, p. 41 -->

<ins>14 December</ins>.

I am dissatisfied with myself for yesterday; first, because I listened to all the Countess's abuse of Vasinka, whom I love, and second, because out of stupid delicacy [^1] yesterday evening was wasted for me in vain.—Today I must have a petition written, go to Vasinka, dine at Gorchakov's, and in the evening do something from among the things I have begun; and above all, write letters.
