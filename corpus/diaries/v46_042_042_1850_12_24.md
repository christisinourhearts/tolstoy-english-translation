<!-- vol. 46, p. 42 -->

24 December

Get up at 12; write letters to the office. Order the account to be sent. — Dine at home with Laptev; before dinner go to the relics; in the evening study thoroughbass and the sonata; if in the mood, write the first letter. Rules. Play cards only in extreme cases. — Tell as little as possible about myself. Speak loudly and distinctly. — Rules. Take exercise every day. — In accordance with the law of religion, have no women. —
