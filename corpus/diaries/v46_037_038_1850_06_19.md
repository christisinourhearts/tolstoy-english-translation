<!-- vol. 46, p. 37 -->

19 June.

Yesterday went fairly well; I carried out almost everything. I am dissatisfied with only one thing: I cannot overcome sensuality, all the more because this passion has merged in me with habit. — Now, having carried out two days, I make a schedule for two days: 19 June. 5—8 estate management and thoughts about music, 8—10 reading, — 10—12 write thoughts about music, 12—6 rest — 6—8 music — 8—10 estate management. — 20 June. 5—10[^1] estate management and diary, 10—12 music, 12—6 rest, 6—10[^2] estate management. —

From the general rules. It happens that[^3] you remember something unpleasant and do not think this unpleasant thing over[^4] properly, and spoil your mood for a long time.

Every unpleasant thought should be considered:[^5] — first, whether it may have consequences;[^6] if it may, then <!-- vol. 46, p. 38 --> how to avert them. — If they cannot be averted, and such a circumstance has already passed, then, after thinking it over properly, try to forget it or become accustomed to it.
