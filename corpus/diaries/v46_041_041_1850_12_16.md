<!-- vol. 46, p. 41 -->

16 December.

Did everything except the writing.—Always get up early. In the morning write letters and the story, go to the Kolymazhny Dvor and to the bathhouse, send to the Council and to Lvov, dine at home, and in the evening at Prince Andrei Ivanovich's play and pay court to the princess. Buy broadcloth and sheet music after [^1] dinner.

[^1]: Deleted: *before*.
