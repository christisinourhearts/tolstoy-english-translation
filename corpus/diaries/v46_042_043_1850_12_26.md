<!-- vol. 46, p. 42 -->

26 December.

— To Prince Sergey Dmitrievich, to Yakovleva, <!-- vol. 46, p. 43 --> to the Zakrevskys, to Prince Andrei Ivanovich, to the Countess, to Perfilieva, to Volkonsky, and to the Dyakovs. Send cards to Gorchakov, Lvov, Ozerov, Koloshin, Volkonsky. —

Spent the 26th badly; was at the Gypsies.
