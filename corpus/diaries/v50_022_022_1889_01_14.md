<ins>14 January</ins> 12 Jan. M. 89.

Late. Letters of sympathy and visits. Ershov with a book. Yesterday and today read a book about American socialism: about two parties: the International and the Socialist. The anarchists are entirely right, except about violence. Astonishing blindness. Still, on this subject I think as I used to think about questions of religion, i.e. it seems necessary and possible to resolve it, but there is no solution yet. — Went walking to Sytin's, did not get there, but stopped in at Voeikov's. Who knows what kind of man he is. A good aspiration, but he is entirely disfigured by narcotics. At home Sonya was worried about both Masha and Fet. Everything worked out; I brought Fet. Dull. In the evening finished reading about the socialists. A letter from Posha. In the evening the police appeared to protect me. — Yes, Stakhovich was here. Thank God, he was not unpleasant.
