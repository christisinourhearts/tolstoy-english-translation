16 June.

Yesterday I carried out what I had planned poorly; I will explain why later. For 16 June. From 5 [^1] <sup>1</sup> / <sub>2</sub> to 7 bathe and be in the fields — 7—10 — diary — 10—12 play — 12—6 breakfast, rest, and dinner — 6—8 write about music. 8—10 — estate management.— —

## Notes

[^1]: 6
