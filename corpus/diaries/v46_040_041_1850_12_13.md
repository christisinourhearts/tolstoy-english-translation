<!-- vol. 46, p. 40 -->

<ins>13 December.</ins>

Although I did not enter 12 December in the diary, <!-- vol. 46, p. 41 --> I spent it well, that is, not in idleness. I went around to the authorities and to the clubs, as a result of which I became convinced: first, that with society taking its present direction I shall succeed; and that I seem to have stopped gambling altogether. — It seems I no longer have a passion for gambling, though I will not vouch for it: it must be tested in practice. I will not look for an occasion, but I will not pass up an advantageous one. — Tasks for 13 Dec. Talk to Pyotr about the petition addressed to His Imperial Majesty and whether I can transfer to service in Moscow? Write letters to Auntie and the Perfilievs, go to Prince Sergey Dmitrievich and to Kryukov, read, make purchases (camellias) and buy books about music, dine, read, and work on composing music or a story.
