[14 June. Y. P.] 14 June. Almost two months later I take up the pen to continue my diary.—Ah, it is hard for a person to develop the good out of himself under the influence of nothing but bad. If only there were no good influence, but no bad either, then in every being spirit would gain the upper hand over matter; but spirit develops differently. Either its development in each individual being forms part of universal development. Or its decline in individual beings strengthens its development in the universal whole.
