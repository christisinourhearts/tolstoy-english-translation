<ins>7 April 1847</ins>

(April. 8 o'clock in the morning.) I have never kept a diary, because I saw no use in it. — Now, when I am engaged in developing my abilities, through the diary I shall be able to judge the course of this development. — The diary should contain a table of rules, and my future actions should also be determined in the diary. — In exactly a week I leave for the country. — What, then, should I do this week? Study English and Latin, Roman law and the rules. Specifically: read *The Vicar of Wakefield*, [^1] learning all [^2] unfamiliar words, and go through

the first part of the Grammar; read, [^3] both for the benefit of the language and of Roman law, the first part of the Institutes; finish the rules of inner education; and win back from Lilya at chess what I lost. —

## Notes

[^1]: *The Vicar of Wakefield*.
[^2]: Deleted: new
[^3]: Deleted: well
