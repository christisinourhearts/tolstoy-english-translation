*18. To Count S. N. Tolstoy. 20 April 1849. St Petersburg.*

20 April.

Please go to Yasnaya and even to Tula if necessary, and somehow settle matters with these merchants. I feel that I myself am to blame for the confusion that has arisen, but what can be done? Ksenofontov [^1] offers 325, while Kopylov offers 280.— It <!-- vol. 59, p. 43 --> seems to me there is only one way [^2] to finish this business: give it to Kopylov for 280 on condition that he enter all the money already taken on the former contract without interest; if he does not agree, then there is nothing to be done—give it to Ksenofontov. In any case, I ask you only one thing: do all this as quickly as possible, however you think best. Whatever you do, I will be grateful to you for everything and will neither argue nor contradict you, because I have the Council hanging over me!

Goodbye. <!-- figure:./img/i000004570002.png -->

### Editorial notes

1. Printed from the autograph held in the State Tolstoy Museum. Published here for the first time. The year is determined by the content: the sale of timber to Kopylov.

## Notes

[^1]: Ksenofontov—evidently a Tula merchant.
[^2]: Deleted: *do this properly*
