*26. To T. A. Yergolskaya. February? 1850. Tula.*

Dear Aunt!

I am sending you cards and hair oil; as for money, I still cannot send you any. Since my return [^1] I have spent ten days in Tula fairly successfully, that is to say, I have lost nothing except my time. The sale of Vorotynka [^2] still cannot be carried out, but I am expecting from day to day the powers of attorney I need for this matter.—

Serge has returned from Petersburg very sensible; for the moment he is at Pirogovo, [^3] where he plans to spend only a few days before [^4] paying you a visit.—

How is your health, and Marie's, and my goddaughter's?—

Kiss them very tenderly for me. Goodbye.—

On page 3:

To Her High Born Tatyana Alexandrovna Yergolskaya.

Dear auntie!

I am sending you cards and hair oil; I still cannot send any money. Since my return [^5] I have spent ten days in Tula fairly successfully, since <!-- vol. 59, p. 56 --> I lost nothing except time. The sale of Vorotynka [^6] still could not take place; I am waiting from day to day for the powers of attorney that I need for this matter.—Seryozha returned from Petersburg a sensible fellow; now he is in Pirogovo [^7] for a few days, and from there he is planning to come to you.—How is your health, Mashenka's, and my goddaughter's?—

Kiss them tenderly for me. Goodbye.

### Editorial notes

1. Printed from the autograph held in the ATB. Published here for the first time. Judging from the contents, the letter was written from Tula. We date it approximately to February 1850 because the letter asks about the health of Tolstoy's goddaughter, Countess Varvara Valerianovna Tolstaya, who was born on 8 January 1850.

## Notes

[^1]: Tolstoy evidently traveled from Tula to Pokrovskoye for the christening.
[^2]: Malaya Vorotynka.
[^3]: Pirogovo was an estate in Krapivna district of Tula province. In the division among the Tolstoy children in 1847, Pirogovo was divided between Sergei Nikolaevich and Maria Nikolaevna; Sergei Nikolaevich received the manor house and the stud farm.
[^4]: Deleted: *in order to go*.
[^5]: Tolstoy evidently traveled from Tula to Pokrovskoye for the christening.
[^6]: Malaya Vorotynka.
[^7]: Pirogovo was an estate in Krapivna district of Tula province. In the division among the Tolstoy children in 1847, Pirogovo was divided between Sergei Nikolaevich and Maria Nikolaevna; Sergei Nikolaevich received the manor house and the stud farm.
