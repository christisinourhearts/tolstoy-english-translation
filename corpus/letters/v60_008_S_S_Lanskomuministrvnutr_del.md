8. To Minister of Internal Affairs S. S. Lanskoy. Draft. 1856. April 25. Petersburg.

To Mr Minister Lanskoy.

Wishing to set free the peasants on my estate, which is mortgaged to the Board of Guardians, I have chosen the method of redemption for the land which they possess, a redemption that will continue for 30 years, the period necessary for repayment of the debt to the Board of Guardians.[^1] —

For this purpose I have written a draft agreement between myself and the peasants, or an obligation on my part, which would immediately place the peasants in the position of a free estate while at the same time not destroying the connection between them and the land.[^2] —

Venturing to submit this project for Your High Excellency's consideration, I have the honor most humbly to request, in view of the novelty of this case, permission for the drawing up and confirmation before the deed authorities[^3] of such an agreement. —

Questions. For the Board of Guardians: Is it possible to free them without land, leaving the debt on the land? Is it possible for several to redeem themselves <!-- vol. 60, p. 58 --> with land, paying their share to the Board of Guardians? Is it possible to set all of them free with all the land, transferring the debt to them? And how much land per soul must there be for this purpose?

For the Ministry of Internal Affairs: Is it possible to make an agreement with the peasants? Is it possible to bequeath freedom and to bequeath it to one person?[^4] —

Questions to the Ministry of Internal Affairs:

Is it possible to conclude a contract with peasants and household serfs on an estate under mortgage, consisting in this: that from the day the contract is concluded I release the peasants and household serfs from all obligations to the landowner, give into the complete disposal of the commune 4 1 / <sub>2</sub> desyatinas of land per tyaglo, and bind them to pay me for 30 years 5 rubles per desyatina? During these 30 years the estate comes out of mortgage, and I undertake not to mortgage it again. At the end of 30 years the peasants are free and pass into the state-peasant category with the land. — Is it possible, when concluding the contract, to write a deed of freedom that becomes effective at the end of 30 years — or merely to stipulate in the contract that at the end of the term the peasants are free? —

In the event of arrears, the estate may be sold only into the state domains.

If it is not possible to conclude a contract before the deed authorities, can the same kind of private agreement be made and confirmed by a will?

If this is a new question, may a memorandum about it be submitted to the Minister of Internal Affairs? —

Questions to the Board of Guardians.

Can the debt be divided: part onto the peasants, part onto the land that remains with the landowner?

Can peasants redeem themselves separately with land, paying their portion of the debt to the Board separately? —

Can all be set free with the debt transferred to them, and what is the minimum amount of land required for this? —

### Editorial notes

1. First published in vol. 5 of the present edition, pp. 245–247. The date is determined from the contents. See the Diary, vol. 47, entries from 22 to 25 April, p. 69.

2. Count Sergei Stepanovich Lanskoy (1787–1862) — first governor in Vladimir and Kostroma, in 1850 a member of the State Council, and in 1855 Minister of Internal Affairs; he held this post until April 1861.

## Notes

[^1]: The Yasnaya Polyana estate was mortgaged to the Board of Guardians for a sum of about 20,000 rubles.
[^2]: See vol. 47, p. 50, and vol. 5, pp. 243–245.
[^3]: “Before the deed authorities” — that is, in the deed section of the civil chamber.
[^4]: On the following page Tolstoy made an extract from volume IX of the *Digest of Laws* relating to the “Regulation on Obligated Peasants” (articles 761 and 764 of the laws on estates); this extract is followed by a continuation of the questions to the Ministry of Internal Affairs and the Board of Guardians.
