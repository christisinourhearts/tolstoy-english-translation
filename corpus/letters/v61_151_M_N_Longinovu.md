*151. To M. N. Longinov. 1865? November 18...20? Yasnaya Polyana.*

I am sending you the archive, [^1] esteemed Mikhail Nikolayevich. If you have not forgotten to ask Alexandrov [^2] about my land consolidation, please pass the answer on to me. In the next few days I will make use of your offer to read Dolgorukov at your place, [^3] <!-- vol. 61, p. 120 --> as everything has begun to go well for me again now. Goodbye.

Yours, Count L. Tolstoy.

### Editorial notes

1. Dated provisionally on the basis of the letter to A. A. Tolstaya of 26...27 November, in which Tolstoy writes that “the day before yesterday” he was in Tula and saw Longinov (see no. 152).

## Notes

[^1]: Probably the journal *Russian Archive*, which Tolstoy borrowed from Longinov to read in connection with his work on *1805*.
[^2]: Alexandrov — a peace arbitrator, who later served in the Tula zemstvo.
[^3]: *The Russian Genealogical Book, published by Prince Pyotr Dolgorukov*, St Petersburg, 1854–1857. In 1874 P. I. Bartenev acquired this book for Tolstoy.
