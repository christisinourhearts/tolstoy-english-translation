*146. To T. F. Gotoitsev. 1896. October 19. Yasnaya Polyana.*

Trofim Fyodorovich,

Vladimir Grigoryevich Chertkov is a close friend of mine, and Ivan Mikhailovich Tregubov is just as much a friend of mine, and what they are asking you for <!-- vol. 69, p. 181 --> they need for a good cause, and therefore, if you can and wish to, then fulfill their request. [^1]

I wish you all the best.

Leo Tolstoy.

19 October 1896.

### Editorial notes

1. Printed from a leaf (the number is cut off) of the copying book.

2. Trofim Fyodorovich Gotoitsev (b. 1874) — a peasant of Novozybkov district, Chernigov province, and a friend of S. V. Shidlovsky (see letter no. 117). He asked Tolstoy to confirm that V. G. Chertkov and I. M. Tregubov really were his friends and that materials about religious persecutions could be entrusted to them.

## Notes

[^1]: V. G. Chertkov, in connection with the religious movement among the Caucasian Doukhobors that began in 1895, began collecting materials about the government's persecution of sectarians in Russia, with the aim of making them public.
