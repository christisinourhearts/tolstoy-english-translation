*116. To S. V. Danilevich. 1907. May 17. Yasnaya Polyana.*

To Danilevich.

I was glad to receive your letter. May God support you in those intentions toward purity of life that you write about. At your age this is one of the strongest temptations. For a successful struggle with it one must believe in the possibility of success and, besides that, remember that there is always a way out—marriage.

Do you need any of my books? I can send them.

With all my heart I wish you constancy and, as a result of constancy, progress on the one true path of strengthening spiritual life that you have chosen.

Leo Tolstoy.

### Editorial notes

1. Printed from copying book no. 7, fol. 337. Basis for the dating: in the copying book the letter is printed among letters of 17 May.

2. In a letter of 13 May 1907 from Ekaterinoslav, the tailor Samuil Vulfovich Danilevich (b. 1881) reported that Tolstoy's religious and moral views had helped him in resolving the question of the meaning of life and the sexual question.
