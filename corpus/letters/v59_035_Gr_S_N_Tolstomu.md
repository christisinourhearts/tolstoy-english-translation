*35. To Count S. N. Tolstoy. 1850. December 22. Moscow.*

you spen...

service — in Mos...

to leave would be unpl... for you

in that case; if

you got caught up in some affair.

That is what I was told. [^1] — Please answer my previous letter, and also do me a favor: find out at the T[ula] Assembly of Dep[uties] whether I have been awarded a rank? — I have prepared a petition addressed to His [Imperial Majesty]. [^2]

reverse of the page:

and orde...

soon there will be...

...ostoy, see that money is sent to me as soon as possible. —

Goodbye.

22 December

1850

### Editorial notes

1. Printed from the autograph held in the ATB. Published here for the first time. The original is a torn sheet of paper.

2. <!-- vol. 59, p. 81 --> 1 This concerns Count S. N. Tolstoy's rank; see the preceding letters.

## Notes

[^1]: This concerns Count S. N. Tolstoy's rank; see the preceding letters.
[^2]: Tolstoy's diary contains entries concerning this petition: on 13 December: “Tasks for 13 December. Talk to Pyotr about the petition addressed to His Imperial Majesty and whether I can transfer to service in Moscow”; and on 14 December: “Today I must have the petition written.” We cannot say what the petition concerned.
