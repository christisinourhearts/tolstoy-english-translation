*85. To Count V. P. Tolstoy. 1854? 30 April? Bucharest.*

How can one not adore Aunt Tatyana Alexandrovna after what she has done? Her greatest happiness is to devote herself to others, as now in her journey and stay at K. I am sure she has written to me, and yet I have not a single letter from her, not even an answer to mine.

### Editorial notes

1. How can one not worship Aunt Tatyana Alexandrovna after what she has done? Her greatest happiness is to sacrifice herself for others; such is now her journey and stay in Kursk. I am sure she has written to me, yet I have not received a single letter from her, not even an answer to mine.

2. <!-- vol. 59, p. 262 --> Printed from a copy made by T. A. Yergolskaya and held in the ATB. Published here for the first time. The text is in T. A. Yergolskaya's hand on a small sheet kept among her letters to Tolstoy. Above the text are two notes in the same hand: “30 Avril [30 April]” and “t. d. l.l.d. V. (E. p.L.),” meaning *tiré de la lettre de Valerien (Ecrit par Léon)* [taken from the letter to Valerian (written by Lev)]. Whether “30 Avril” is the date of Tolstoy's letter to Count V. P. Tolstoy, the date on which Tolstoy's letter was received, or the date on which T. A. Yergolskaya copied the passage from Tolstoy's letter remains unknown. We provisionally take it as the date of Tolstoy's letter to Count V. P. Tolstoy. We provisionally date it to 1854 on the ground that the sheet was enclosed in an envelope on which T. A. Yergolskaya had written Tolstoy's Bucharest address.
