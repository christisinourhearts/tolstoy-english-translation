6. To N. A. Nekrasov. 29–30 March 1856. St Petersburg.

Could you give me the money for *The Snowstorm* now, and let the debt of 400 rubles remain until my next piece of writing.—You would oblige me greatly. Goodbye. Yours, Tolstoy.

### Editorial notes

1. First published in *Sovremennik*, 1913, no. 8, p. 267. Dated on the basis of a notation made by an unknown hand on Tolstoy's letter: “Paid for *The Snowstorm*. On 30 March the account was settled—Tolstoy still owed: 400 rubles. [illegible] 500: Total by 1 April owed by Tolstoy: 900.”
