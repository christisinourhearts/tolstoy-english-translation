*2. To Countess A. I. Tolstaya. 7 February 1856. St Petersburg.*

Unfortunately for me, tomorrow I must be on duty all day; I hope to get away for dinner; but if I do not manage it, I hope you will excuse me, most respected Countess, and believe that only absolute impossibility could deprive me of the pleasure of being with you, and that the feelings of respect and devotion with which I have the honor to be your most humble servant are entirely sincere—

Count L. Tolstoy.

7 February.

On the fourth page:

To Her Excellency Countess Tolstaya. At the Academy of Arts.

### Editorial notes

1. The year is determined from the content. In 1856 Tolstoy was serving at the St Petersburg Rocket Establishment.

2. Countess Anastasia Ivanovna Tolstaya, née Ivanova (1817–1889), was the second wife of Tolstoy's first cousin once removed, Count Fyodor Petrovich Tolstoy (1783–1873), an artist and medallist who was vice-president of the Academy of Arts in 1828–1859. The evenings at the Tolstoys' in the Academy of Arts, attended by artists, actors, writers, musicians, and scholars, are described in the *Recollections* of their daughter E. F. Junge, Sphinx edition, Moscow, no year given, p. 68.
