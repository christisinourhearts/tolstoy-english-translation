*165. To G. A. Ermolaev. 1892. February 6. Begichevka.*

Dear Grigory Alexeyevich!

Fyodor Alexeyevich Strakhov, [^1] at my request, is going to Klekotki in order to clarify the quantity of firewood and its distribution, as well as all the other matters. — Please tell him everything and explain everything to him, and carry out all his instructions.

I wrote down for him [^2] what needs to be done, and he himself will decide the rest as he thinks best, with your advice.

I wish you all the best.

L. Tolstoy.

### Editorial notes

1. Written on the same day as the letter to S. A. Tolstaya of 6 February (see vol. 84, pp. 115–116).

## Notes

[^1]: Fyodor Alexeyevich Strakhov (1861–1923), a lawyer by training and the author of a number of articles of religious-philosophical content; an acquaintance of Tolstoy's since 1889. In February 1892 Strakhov helped Tolstoy in his famine-relief work, organizing soup kitchens in the Skopin district and living in the village of Muravlyanka.
[^2]: This note by Tolstoy is unknown.
