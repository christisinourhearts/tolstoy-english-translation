1855. 31 December. St Petersburg.

I was carried away a little by the wish to please you, dear sir Andrei Alexandrovich, when, the last time I was at your place, I said that I promised your journal *The Novel of a Russian Landowner* for this year as well. That novel is still far from finished and polished; therefore, if you mention me among your contributors, please do not specify exactly what I have promised. You may be certain, however, <!-- vol. 59, p. 337 --> that by February or March I will without fail send you a piece.

Truly respecting and devoted to you,

Count L. Tolstoy.

I am writing to you because tomorrow I am going to Moscow for 28 days.

### Editorial notes

1. Printed from the autograph held in the Public Library of the RSFSR in Leningrad. A photograph of the autograph is held in the State Tolstoy Museum. First published by M. A. Tsyavlovsky in *L. N. Tolstoy. Jubilee Collection of the Moscow Tolstoy Museum*, 1929, p. 57. Dated from the content: “tomorrow I am going to Moscow for 28 days.” On 31 December 1855 Tolstoy wrote to N. A. Nekrasov: “I will write to Kraevsky.” The present letter is the fulfillment of that promise.

2. Andrei Alexandrovich Kraevsky (1810–1889), a well-known journalist and editor-publisher of *Otechestvennye Zapiski* in 1838–1868. Tolstoy met him in November–December 1855, after arriving *in* St Petersburg from Sevastopol. Their dealings were purely businesslike, as the letter printed here also shows.
