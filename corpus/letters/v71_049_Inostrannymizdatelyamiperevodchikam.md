*49. To Foreign Publishers and Translators. 1898. February 25. Moscow.*

Now that my friend Vladimir Tchertkoff is living in England, it is into his hands that I desire to transmit all arrangements in connection with the first publication of my writings in foreign countries and therefore to him that I would refer all translators and publishers interested in the matter.

Leo Tolstoy.

Moscou.

8 March 1898.

### Editorial notes

1. Now that my friend Vladimir Chertkov lives in England, I wish to place in his hands all agreements and connections concerning the first publication of my writings abroad; and therefore I would like all translators and publishers interested in this matter to apply to him.

2. Leo Tolstoy.

3. Moscow.

4. 8 March 1898.

5. Printed from a photocopy. The date is New Style (see Tolstoy's letter to V. G. Chertkov of 25 February, vol. 88).
