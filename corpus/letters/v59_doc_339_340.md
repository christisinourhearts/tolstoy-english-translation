1853. June 6. Vozdvizhenskaya stanitsa.

15. In the year 1853, on the 6th day of June, I, the undersigned, Count Lev Nikolaevich Tolstoy, artillery fireworker 4th class, having set free the unmarried serf peasant Alexander Mikhailov, who came to me by inheritance after the death of my parents, Lieutenant Colonel Nikolai Ilyich and Maria Nikolaevna Counts Tolstoy, and under the deed of division with my brothers Nikolai, Sergei, Dmitry and my sister Maria, the Nikolaev children, Counts Tolstoy, confirmed by the Tula Chamber of Civil Court on 8 February 1851 <!-- vol. 59, p. 340 --> as part of our estate, portion *[4 illegible]*, according to the returns of the 9th revision for Tula province, Krapivna district, at the village of Yasnaya Polyana, family no. 5, for the purpose of his, Alexander Mikhailov's, entering the monastic estate, in whatever monastery he may wish to live; neither I nor my heirs have any claim upon him or any right to interfere with him, and I undertake to pay the poll-tax money for him, Mikhailov, until his death. His identifying features are: 23 years of age, height 2 arshins 3 vershoks, dark-blond hair on head and eyebrows, gray eyes, nose and mouth of medium size, round chin, clear face, no special marks. To this deed of emancipation Count Lev Nikolaevich Tolstoy, artillery fireworker 4th class, has set his hand.

### Editorial notes

1. Witness to this deed of emancipation was Lieutenant Colonel Nikita Petrov son of Alexeyev,<sup>1</sup> commander of Battery No. 4 of the Artillery Brigade, who set his hand and certifies it by affixing the seal of the battery brigade.

2. Witness to this deed of emancipation was Lieutenant Alexander Pavlov son of Ogolin,<sup>2</sup> of Battery No. 4 of the 20th Artillery Brigade. On 14 August 1853 the peasant Alexander Mikhailov, thus freed by Count Lev Nikolaevich Tolstoy, fireworker 4th class, presented this deed of emancipation with a petition to the Tula Chamber of Civil Court and asked that it be confirmed and issued to him.

3. Consequently, after the information necessary for the case had been collected, on 15 March of the present year 1854 the matter was reported and it was resolved to confirm the deed of emancipation presented by the peasant Nikolai, to make upon it a notation that it had been exhibited, and, after recording it in the register of deeds, to forward it for issue to the established council of the Holy Trinity–St Sergius Lavra, asking that the receipt obtained from him be sent to the Chamber; notice of confirmation of the deed was to be sent for information to the Moscow Emancipation Council and the Tula Treasury Chamber. On 29 March 1854 it was signed by Assessor Milyaev and Secretary A. Afanasyev.

4. Dmitrievsky for the clerk, and the seal of the Chamber. The deed was written on a sheet bearing a ninety-kopeck silver stamp duty and sent to the established council of the Holy Trinity–St Sergius Lavra for issue.

5. 1 April 1854, no. 4342.

6. Printed from the *Register of Deeds of the Tula Chamber of Civil Court for Recording Deeds of Emancipation in 1854* (folios 148 and 19), held in the Tula Branch of the Moscow Regional Archival Administration.

7. The first paragraph of the serf deed published here is a copy, certified in the legal manner for deeds, of the original deed of emancipation signed by Tolstoy in the Caucasus on 6 June 1853 in the name of the serf Alexander Mikhailov, about whom no information is available.
