*22. To A. I. Sobolev. 1849? April... May? St Petersburg?*

Andrey Ilyich!

Will you finally tell me how much is overdue to the Guardianship Council? If the money received for the land cannot be sent to the Council, then try to sell something and send at least a little money to the Council, so that we are not subjected to an inventory, which would stop the sales I have planned.

Count L. Tolstoy.

### Editorial notes

1. Printed from the autograph held in the ATB. Published here for the first time. It is dated approximately from its content: Tolstoy was preoccupied in April–May 849 with paying money to the Guardianship Council and selling land. See letters nos. 18 and 19.
