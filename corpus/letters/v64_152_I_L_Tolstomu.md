*152. To I. L. Tolstoy. 1887. October. Yasnaya Polyana.*

.... [^1] Two things bind her [^2] to you: convictions—faith—and love. In my opinion, even one is enough. The real, true bond is human, Christian love; if that is there, and on it there also grows love—being in love—then it is beautiful and firm. If there is only love—being in love—then it is not that it is bad, but there is nothing good in it either, though it is still possible. And with honest natures, with great struggles, one can live even with that. But if there is neither one nor the other, but only the appearance [^3] of both, then that is surely bad. I advise you to be as strict with yourself as possible and to know what you are acting in the name of.

### Editorial notes

1. Fragment of a letter, printed from a copy. First published in *Russkoye Slovo*, 1913, no. 273, 27 November. Dated from the content.
