*24. To T. A. Yergolskaya. 1849. June... December? Tula.*

What are you doing, dear Aunt? I am enjoying myself. Goodbye, I kiss your hands. — This note is for André.

On the reverse: To Her High Born Tatyana Alexandrovna Yergolskaya. At Yasnaya Polyana.

### Editorial notes

1. What are you doing, dear auntie? I am enjoying myself. Goodbye, I kiss your hands. — This note is for Andrei.

2. Printed from the autograph held in the ATB. Published here for the first time. It is dated approximately to the period when Tolstoy was often in Tula.
