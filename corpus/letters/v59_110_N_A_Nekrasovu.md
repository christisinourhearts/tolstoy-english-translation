*110. To N. A. Nekrasov. 1855. December 31. St Petersburg.*

My leave came through not for 28 days but for 14, so, without losing a single day, I am leaving for Moscow tomorrow, that is, the 1st. I will finish the proofs tomorrow, [^1] write to Krayevsky, and ask you, if you can, to send me 100 rubles as an advance on future work. Get well. If I have time, I will call on you myself.

Yours, Count L. Tolstoy.

### Editorial notes

1. Printed from the autograph held in the IRLI. First published in *Sovremennik*, 1913, no. 8, p. 266.

2. Dated from the contents: Tolstoy is going on leave “tomorrow, that is, the 1st.” Since Tolstoy returned to St Petersburg around 2 February, and according to his service record he received 28 days' leave in January 1856, the “1st” is 1 January 1856.
