<!-- vol. 41, p. 42 -->

17 January

By fulfilling his inner purpose, living for the soul, a person involuntarily and in the most effective way serves the improvement of social life.

1

> People cannot be freed in outward life more than they are freed within.

> *Herzen.*

2

> The dreamer often correctly determines the future, but he does not want to wait for it. He wants by his own efforts to bring it nearer. What nature needs thousands of years for, he wants to see accomplished during his lifetime.

> *Lessing.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 43 -->

3

> Why do you torment yourselves in vain in your calamitous condition? Your desire is good, but you do not know how to attain it. Know that only he who gives up his life can receive it. You will achieve nothing without God. You toss on your bed of suffering, and what have you found? You destroyed several oppressors—others appeared, worse than the former. You destroyed the laws of slavery, and they gave you new laws of blood and still new laws of slavery. Do not believe people who stand between you and God so that their shadow may hide him from you. These people have evil intentions. For only from God comes the power that liberates, for only from God comes the love that unites. What can a person do for you who is guided only by his own thought and the law of his own will? If he has good intentions and desires only good, he will nevertheless give you his own will instead of law and his own thought instead of rule. All oppressors do this. It is not worth destroying in order to replace one violence with another. Freedom consists not in this one ruling rather than that one, but in no one ruling. Where God does not rule, man rules. The kingdom of God is the kingdom of justice in minds and mercy in hearts; its foundation is faith in the law proclaimed by Christ, the law of mercy, the law of justice. The law of justice teaches that all are equal before God the Father and the one teacher, Christ. The law of mercy teaches love and help to one another, as sons of one father and disciples of one teacher.
>
> If people say to you: “Before us no one knew what justice was; justice comes from us, believe us, and we will arrange such justice for you as will satisfy you,” then such people deceive you or, if they sincerely promise you freedom, deceive themselves—because they want you to recognize them as masters, and then your freedom will be only obedience to these new masters. Answer them that your master is God alone, and you want no other, and God will free you.

> *Lamennais.*

4

It would be good if wisdom were of such a nature that it could pour from the person who is full of it into the one in whom there is none, as water passes through wool <!-- vol. 41, p. 44 --> from one vessel into another until both are equal. But the trouble is that to receive another's wisdom, independent work is needed first of all.

5

> *If you can teach a person good and do not do so—you lose a brother.*

> *Chinese wisdom.*

Do your life's work, perfecting and improving your soul, and be sure that only by this path will you contribute in the most fruitful way to the improvement of common life.
