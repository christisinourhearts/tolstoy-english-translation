## PREFACE

The thoughts collected here were taken by me from a very large number of works and collections of thoughts.

Thoughts without a signature were either taken by me from collections in which their authors are not named, or belong to me.

The remaining thoughts are signed with the names of their authors, but unfortunately, when I copied them out, I did not note exactly from which work they were taken.

Often I translated authors' thoughts not from the originals but from translations into other languages, and therefore my translations may prove not to be entirely faithful to the originals.

Another reason these thoughts may not entirely correspond to the originals is that, often selecting individual thoughts from a long argument, I had, for clarity and completeness of impression, to omit certain words and sentences and sometimes not only replace some words with others but express the thought entirely in my own words, since the purpose of my book is not to give exact verbal translations of writers, but, by making use of the great, fruitful thoughts of various writers, to give a large number of readers an accessible daily circle of reading that awakens the best thoughts and feelings.

I would like readers, in reading this book each day, to experience the same beneficial, elevating feeling that I experienced while compiling it and continue to experience now, both in reading it each day and in working to improve its second edition.

Leo Tolstoy.

March 1908. Yasnaya Polyana.
