<!-- vol. 41, p. 16 -->

4 January

Even if we did not want it, we cannot help feeling our connection with the whole world of people: we are connected by industry, trade, art, knowledge, and, above all, by the unity of our condition, the unity of our relation to the world.

1

> *Good people help one another,* *even without realizing it,* *while bad people deliberately work against one another.*

> *Chinese proverb.*

2

> Everyone has his own burden, his own faults: no one can do without the help of others; therefore we must help one another with consolation, advice, and mutual warnings.

> *From “Pious Thoughts.”*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 17 -->

3

> The organization of our world is such that a thousand people working together can produce many times more than the same thousand people could produce working separately. Yet this does not prove the necessity of nine hundred and ninety-nine people becoming the slaves of one.

> *Henry George.*

4

> A good person is the teacher of a bad person; a bad person is the very thing a good person must work on. The one who does not respect his teacher and does not love what he must work on, however intelligent he may be, is mistaken.

> *Lao-Tzu.*

5

> All the children of Adam are members of one body. When one member suffers, all the others suffer. If you are indifferent to the suffering of others, you do not deserve to be called human.

> *Saadi.*

6

> The life of an individual must grow completely and tightly into the common life of humanity, for all creation is permeated by harmony and unity. In outward nature as well as in the spiritual realm, all the phenomena of life are closely connected with one another.

> *Marcus Aurelius.*

The whole history of humanity, for as long as we know it, is humanity's movement toward ever greater unity. This unity is brought about by the most varied means, and it is served not only by those who work for it, but even by those who oppose it.
