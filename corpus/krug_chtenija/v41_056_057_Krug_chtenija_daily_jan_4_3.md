<!-- vol. 41, p. 56 -->

24 January

Where humanity will arrive is not given to people to know. The highest wisdom consists in knowing where you are to go. And this you know: to go toward the highest perfection.

1

> Narrow is the path leading to life, and few find it. Few find it because the majority go by the broad path, the one everyone goes by. But the true path is narrow, only for one person. And in order to find it, one must follow not the crowd but those solitary people — Buddha, Confucius, Socrates, Christ — who, each for himself and for all of us, one after another, laid the same narrow road.
>
> *According to Lucy Mallory.*

2

> There are only three classes of people: some have found God and serve him; these people are reasonable and happy. Others have not found him and do not seek him; these are foolish and unhappy. The third have not found him but seek him; these people are reasonable but still unhappy.
>
> *Blaise Pascal.*

3

> Where the search for truth begins, life always begins; as soon as the search for truth ceases, life ceases too.
>
> *John Ruskin.*

4

> To see all things in divine perfection, to make one’s life a movement toward that perfection — this is the marvelous point of view of the sages of antiquity: Socrates, Epictetus, Marcus Aurelius. There is a kind of Christianity that reviles wisdom and refuses to recognize it. And yet how much higher is a wisdom content with the kingdom of God on earth than the teaching which teaches that it is possible only beyond the grave.
>
> A sign of false teaching is that it postpones life to another time and values a person who believes in its teaching more highly than a virtuous person.
>
> *According to Amiel.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 57 -->

5

> If a person seeks wisdom, he is intelligent, but if he thinks he has found it, he is foolish.
>
> *Persian Wisdom (from Albitis).*

6

> *What matters is not the place we occupy, but the direction in which we are moving.*
>
> *Holmes.*

The goals you share with the people around you should not determine your activity, but the purpose of your life, which is the same as the purpose of all people in the world.
