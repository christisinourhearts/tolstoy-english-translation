## No. 5, 1874

1874. 27 February. Yasnaya Polyana.

*Novum Organum* with translation.

There is a language of philosophy; I will not speak it. I will speak in simple language. The interest of philosophy is common to everyone, and everyone is a judge.—

Philosophical language was invented to counter objections. I am not afraid of objections. I am seeking. I belong to no camp. And I ask the readers not to belong to one.—This is the first condition for philosophy. I must answer the materialists in the preface. They say that apart from earthly life there is nothing. I must answer, because if that were so, then I would have nothing to write about.—Having lived nearly 50 years, I have become convinced that earthly life gives nothing, and the intelligent person who looks seriously into earthly life—labors, fear, reproaches, struggle—for what?—for the sake of madness, will shoot himself at once, and Hartmann and Schopenhauer are right. But Schopenhauer made one feel that there is something, which is why he did not shoot himself. This something is the task of my book. What do we live by?—Religion.—
