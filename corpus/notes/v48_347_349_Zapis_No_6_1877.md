## No. 6, 1877

15 December 1877.

The laws of good, expressing the consciousness of masses of people—of all—and not subject to reason and therefore to dispute, are manifestations of divinity.

For the first conversation with a schismatic.

On the scales—to condemn, not to love, or to wait, to judge—not to look.

Objection and difficulty.

<!-- vol. 48, p. 348 --> —My fathers were schismatics. [^1]

Refutation. I will rise above the fathers—by reconciling.

Why are ethical laws manifestations of divinity?

Because to know what is good and bad, what one must love, is equivalent = to knowing why I exist, where I am going, what I am striving toward.

The ethical significance of religion (Christ's), indeed, is not recognized by science, the worshipper of progress. And what is progress? In the best definition—movement forward for the attainment of the aim of moral good. Who indicated this aim?

The difference between philosophy and religion—philosophy pertains to a certain degree of development of the mind. And it is rejected from another point of view and admits different, diametrically opposite points of view.

Religion in its ethical significance includes, like a glass gathering rays, all views and does not admit disagreement—there is none in reality.

St. Augustin: *il n’y a pas de fausses religions qui ne contiennent quelques éléments de vérité.* Max Müller 35.

Max Müller 58. *La conscience du péché qu’on a commis est un des traits saillants de la religion de Véda.*

M. Müller 60. *Une autre idée c’est celle de la foi Sradha.*

65/66. *Nulle part il n’est question de Métempsicose mais de l’immortalité de l’âme.* [^2]

66. Henri Martin on the immortality of the soul in the Old Testament.

<!-- vol. 48, p. 349 --> Strauss's conclusion.—Christ is not a complete ideal because he does not include the family, the state, art, craft.—And the complete ideal of religion is progress. This has already been decided.

17 December.

Life is nothing other than a manifestation of freedom.

This manifestation is only in the sense of good and evil. Knowledge of good and evil is faith—religion.

The mysterious phenomenon of conscience is nothing other than consciousness of conformity or nonconformity to the revealed law of good—revelation. (The character of revelation—accessibility to all.)—

Max Müller 90. Papou [^3] (where) sit before the idol Karvar in order to learn whether what they have conceived is good or bad.

Max Müller 72. Three conclusions about ancient religions:

1) In their youth religions are pure.

2) All have an answer to the question.

3) Ours better eval...

This is false.

Max Müller. Preface VII. In all religions: 1) concept of God, 2) feeling of weakness and dependence, 3) faith in providence, 4) distinction between good and evil, 5) future life.

We distinguish science and religion, but perhaps we have no right to, and there is one rational knowledge.

Proof that science cannot even touch it and that religion is superstition.

1. But 1) is there life without this knowledge?

2) Is life possible without this knowledge?

3) Is knowledge of faith possible?

Max Müller. True Christianity. What is it? A phrase.
