## [NOTEBOOK NO. 2, 1909]

[1 June.] For *Nobody Is to Blame*.

Chertkov comes to Stolypin and tells him about the 1,000 people hanged.

Neustroev writes literature for the people and a program of activity.

Professor, priest, governor. Military judges.

Chertkov in a monastery. Goes out into the world and reproaches Stolypin. —

Neustroev is in love with Lina.

The statistician does not have a minute free.

Megalomania—in the future.

So wanted to believe in self-sacrifice for the people that he believes.

Afanasyevna's thoughts. Peasant with a cow. Secretaries of the Police Administration.
