[1863, 3... 16 August.] &lt;Sonya, forgive me, only now do I know that I am guilty and how guilty I am! There are days when one lives as though not by one's own will, but submits to some external irresistible law. That is how I was these days toward you. — And who then [illegible: 1 word]

I. And I always thought that I had many faults and had one-tenth part feeling and generosity. I was proud and cruel—and to whom? — To the one being who gave me the greatest happiness of my life and who alone loves me. Sonya, I know that this is not forgotten and not forgiven; but I know more than you do and understand all my own baseness. Sonya, darling, I am guilty, but I am vile [illegible: 1 word]; there is an excellent man in me who sometimes sleeps. Love him and do not reproach him, Sonya.&gt;
