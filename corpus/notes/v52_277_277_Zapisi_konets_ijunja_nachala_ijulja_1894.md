## 4 [Late June–Early July 1894]

1) Ask about the study.

2) Give the letter to Chertkov.

3) Khelchitsky.

4) One of life’s strange delusions: whoever thinks that external conditions can increase or diminish my good. Everything is done by me alone. My whole life is made by me alone. Whether it is good, bad, happy, unhappy — I.

Send to Chertkov, write to Salomon.

Write to Turner about the translation of the Gospels.

10. Cause of the fight between the French and Italian workers.

Take his manuscript to Tishchenko and send the books — Storozhenko, Rumyantsev Museum.
