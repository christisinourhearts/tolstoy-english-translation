## [NOTES ON SHEETS, 1881]

[12 May 1881. Tula.] Podyakat needed.

Conversation behind the clerk’s wall: swindlers, the people have grown spoiled.

A soldier escorted a party to Kyiv. — 27 people — howling.

Drunkenness.

Gavrilo, 50 years old. I only have to strip off — I’ll earn 10 rubles a week.

Spent 3 rubles going out for a drink to cure the hangover — that’s nothing to me.

Why did they take away what the Tsar gave? —

I have two homes — a daughter, 3 allotments. My son-in-law gave it up because there is no one; if there were an old man, they would not have given it away.

Chicks. — Warden, first prisoner.

In the jail he has no home. — Guards with sabers. Two women. One on the floor.

Stand up! One, a beggar woman, for having no papers; the other — simple-minded, kind, because she left the booth. —

Prisoners under investigation. Grigory in a coarse coat with a diamond patch, rags of a shirt, trousers, bast shoes. — Tears, Alexander — tears. To the fierce evildoer. Walked a week.

Was he a dog? Ha, ha, ha.

The peasant’s cheerfulness at the investigator’s gate.

A woman laughs at Vasily — what a coachman!

You into jail. — Well?

The investigator’s ineptness and slovenliness, 2 weeks — they did not find out about the wheels.

Ivan Rodivonov. Nikolai promised 50 rubles, only don’t forgive.

<!-- vol. 49, p. 136 --> District police chief, a mild smile. I laughed from the heart. Was at a dead body; they brought him barely alive.

The warden, a noncommissioned officer — good.

Two nights at the volost office, in Kolpna, no escort. In Khmelevets, 5 huts answer for it.

In Retinka, God grant them health, they fed us; otherwise we had not eaten.

Tears. Wheels, cart, horse collar taken. —

People from Sinyavino — arson.

Upstairs: dark horse thief, swollen. A man from Shchyokino has been sitting a year for a fight. Knout handle into the mouth, died. Sentenced (to imprisonment).

Stand up! Soldier Pirogovsky. Meek, quiet, swollen, does not complain — sheep were dying. Don’t speak! —

Conversation with the warden — Innocent? He does not believe it, it rarely happens. Mockingly sarcastic smile. About the woman at the crossing. The same smile. She left — a death occurred.

— Yid — gleam of black-reddish hair: oily eyes and lips, a Holland-cloth shirt showing from under the uniform. They are guilty because they are not like me. —

Temporary cell. Good-natured fine fellow, old moustache, young beard. Printed-calico shirt worn outside.

Children read in the kitchen. An old beggar woman being transferred, ran away, was caught, — lies without a mattress, about 60. Remembers only that her companion left. Upstairs plain red beds, without mattresses, a round cast-iron stove. 6 people. Two from Panino for oats — say they are innocent. The dark one reads. He falsely accused [me] about the horse too. They stole. Boy. I stole.

Another boy climbed into a woman’s shed, laughs, covers his mouth.

Pirogovsky boy.

16 years old — casket.

Fishermen, pale, does not look in the eyes.

In the jail it is hungry until being brought in, and afterward there is enough.

In the temporary cell there is cheerfulness; they eat only bread without salt, 2¼ pounds, it used to be 1¾.

They share with those to whom food is brought. Two have been sitting 2 months, nothing is brought from home. —

Gavrilo sat 2 months, warden a strict convert from Judaism.

Wove bast shoes.

<!-- vol. 49, p. 137 --> In summer it is dull. He knows the wheels, — which ones — the investigator did not ask.

Tsar’s pay, 75 each. One was himself 3. They gave 145, counted 20 people as 15.

Silence. —

I go out into the empty yard — one colt neighs.

And she made a bolt too, and the dogs are vicious — a bitch with a pup.

A tear will break through.

I’ll pull out all my sinews.

In the next world he will be before me. —

Will dry [me] out entirely.

It tickles there. —

Got the grain in. —

20 kopecks, she’ll lick her own paws.

Poor sense. —

Wouldn’t start groaning.

Laziness in the a..e and back — drive it out.

One rib will go behind another.

On the stove, kalachi; on the step, porridge.

And we’ll drink, and we’ll make merry.

Death will come, we’ll die.

Sufferings are so real, so inherent, that it seems to me I am almost incapable of thinking of future bliss.

Get out of sin.

How will I die? Lovely.

Such a zavoroten will hit harder than a man.

Otherwise she just keeps at it. —

Glad to strain herself at work —

A woman of gold.

They’ll bury the body, but they did not escape God.

Lays the blame on her.

Went to chew, went to gnaw.

What will I lean on.

Fill yourself with another’s bread, drink your fill of blood.

The parents entreated God.

The enemy latched on.
