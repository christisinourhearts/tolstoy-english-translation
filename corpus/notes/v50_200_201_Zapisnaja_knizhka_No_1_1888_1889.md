## [NOTEBOOK NO. 1, 1888—1889]

[November 1888. Y. P.]

1 To Chertkov about family life

2 Answer Biryukov

3 Answer Khilkov

4 Lisitsyn

5 Blake

6 Popov

7 Did not write to Bronevsky

8 Orlov.

9 Antony

10 Ge

11 Semenov

[10—11? April 1889. M.]

Gauthier

Herzen

[November 1888 — March 1889]

Beyond Razgulyai, Demidovsky Lane, Kuzmicheva's house, Popova's apartment.

[8? March 1889]

Arkhangelsky Nik. Nik., Rozhdestvenka, corner of Trubnaya Square, Yakovlev's house, Rodionov's apartment.[^1]

Bolshoi Sergievsky Lane, Smirnov's house, formerly Zhivago's. Sretenskaya district.

[9—10 April.]

Write to Popov: seek the Father's perfection, so that the light may shine, and wait. Fulfillment of the will is to drink and eat and wait.

For the Comedy. They're already stuffing themselves. A bottomless pit—where does it all fit.

Semenov.

<!-- vol. 50, p. 201 -->

[30? March — April.]

Rev. Thomas Van Ness. Unity Church. Denver. Colorado. U. S. A.[^2]

Skvortsov — Pyotr Konst. 1st Zachatyevsky Lane, Lyasnev's house.

[14 April.]

Not passing through, but a worker.[^3]

[13—16? April.]

Bolshaya Serpukhovskaya St. Novoproektirovanny Lane, Strashnov's house, to I. Ivin.[^4]

[19? April.]

Children's hospital, to the Chertkovs about the wet nurse and about the boy Fedosya.

[20? April.]

Articles by Dmokhovskaya.

[22? April.]

Communes are not needed.[^5]

Nonresisting Christians—they are the leaders. If only the self-appointed leaders did not rush about.[^6]

By the path by which he learned the feeling when *beibringen*.[^7]

[23? April.]

The learned pious will leave the world, rulers will become monks. Who then will remain as an example? Nobody.

[27 April.]

The slops from rinsing the cup in which there had been a Christian—that is a gentleman.[^8]

27 A. 89.

[30 April.]

Not allowed. A great phrase. Everything is abolished—there is no other law.

1) Church, 2) army, 3) punishments, 4) drunkenness, 5) landownership, 6) factories, ~~7~~ drunkenness>, 8) prostitution.[^9]
