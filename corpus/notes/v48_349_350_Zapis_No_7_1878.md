## NO. 7, 1878

1 February 1878.

Evil is an expression of freedom, a departure from law. Freedom[^1] is life itself.

<!-- vol. 48, p. 350 --> The answer to the person who asks what evil is is this. Evil is the (form) appearance of life. If you ask: why does evil exist? I answer with a question: why life? Evil exists so that there may be life. Life manifests itself in freedom. Freedom has one field of action—the greater or lesser observance of law. Law and good are one and the same. Departure from law is evil.

In temporal form one cannot express evil otherwise than as a beginning of evil—a devil who desired evil. —

Man's departure from good—from law—is hereditary (Darwin is right).

On the book *Of Errors and Truth*: pp. 78, 79, 80 are astonishing.

P. 99. Woman is below man, coming from man, while man is from God.

[^2] belly—Mercury

— sulfur, head—salt.

A madman is not responsible, for he is already punished. On punishing children for “the sins of the father?” p. 172. Two lines of an angle cannot make a figure without a third. —

Fourth progression.

1) God man creat. mater., p. 211. Astonishing. I would express it this way: Every being, and man like every lower being, cannot help feeling that it is only when he submits to the laws of his being (eat, sleep) that he does not suffer. Now there is another sphere of man's activity, the one to which he has been raised by reason—the sphere of freedom of will, of choice. He needs to know the law so as not to suffer from failing to obey it. The moral law,[^3] written in my heart and confirmed by tradition.

P. 210, extraordinary! By labor, by torments, I arrived at the very same thing.
