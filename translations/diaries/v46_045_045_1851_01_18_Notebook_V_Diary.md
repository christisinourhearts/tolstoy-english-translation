18.

Behaved neither well nor badly. Little firmness. — For the 19th — position. Occupations: be at the riding school, at Chertova’s, at the Gorchakovs’, at Prince Nikolai Mikhailovich’s. Toward evening, bank. Write the history of my childhood.
