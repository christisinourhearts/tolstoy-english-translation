29.

I live completely like a beast; though not entirely dissolutely, I have abandoned almost all my occupations and have fallen very low in spirit. — Get up early; until 2 o’clock receive no one and do not go out; at 2 go to Chulkov’s, to the Dyakovs’ and dine, to the Prince, ask for a position.[^1] Think over future actions at leisure in every new place. — In the morning write the story, read and play, or write about music; in the evening the rules or *The Gypsies*.

## Notes

[^1]: Deleted: *Rule* [Tolstoy’s italics.]
