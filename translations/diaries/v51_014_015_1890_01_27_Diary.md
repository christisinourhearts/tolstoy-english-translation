<!-- vol. 51, p. 14 -->

<ins>27 January.</ins>

Today, 27 Jan. Yesterday, 26 Jan. Leskov was leaving, and I, feeling that I would not be able to work, saw him off—I went to Tula myself as coachman. Carried out an errand with Chertkov, and then we had dinner at Davydov's. Talked a great deal. The girls met us. The day before yesterday, the 25th. In the morning talked with Chertkov and Leskov while walking. Went into the school. Then, as far as I remember, I corrected the comedy, the fourth act. In the evening we talked, and I read the comedy. All vanity. Chertkov is the same, even closer to me. Three days ago, the 24th. In the morning corrected the whole comedy from the beginning.—Did not finish by dinner. Went to Tula for Chertkov and Leskov and missed them on the road. In the evening saw Sonya off <!-- vol. 51, p. 15 --> to Moscow. On the 23rd—I don't remember what I did in the morning. I think I tried the Koni story, but wrote nothing.—
