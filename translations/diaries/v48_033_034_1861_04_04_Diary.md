<ins>4/16 April.</ins> 16 April. (Weimar.)

Schullehrerseminar. [^1] Excellent. Rechnen [^2] with sticks and conversion into numbers — geography with measurement assignments. (Language—not good, with the useless labor of defining the defined.) Zwätzen. A most stupid school, showing what institutions imposed from above lead to. — Theory without practice. Grignon—a model. Went on foot. On a hill in the woods, drank in nature simply and happily. Contentment. Near Weimar. Prussian rider and little flowers. A woman, freely bent backward, in a plowed field, against the sky—half. Bare arms. Kähler, it seems, needlessly. Maltitz—boredom. Old woman delighted that I do not like civilization. Evening—again the anxious thoughts about education, just as on the road, and it becomes clear only by limiting oneself: first limit—away with upbringing, only <!-- vol. 48, p. 34 --> [^3] instruction; second (on account of reading kitchen chemistry). Practical teaching of science is the first and last stage—the task of the school is not *die Wissenschaft beibringen*, but *die Achtung und die Idee der Wissenschaft beibringen*. [^4] With this I fell asleep calmly. On the road, throwing little stones, I thought about art too. Can one have situations alone as one's aim, rather than characters? It seems one can; that is what I have done, and where I have had success. Only this is not a universal task, but mine.

## Notes

[^1]: *Teachers' seminary.*
[^2]: Arithmetic.
[^3]: Deleted: education.
[^4]: *not to impart science, but to impart respect for science and the idea of science.*
