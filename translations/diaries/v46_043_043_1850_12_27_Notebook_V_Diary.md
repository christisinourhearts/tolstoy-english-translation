<!-- vol. 46, p. 43 -->

27 December.

Get up at 9; until 12 to the Kolymazhny Dvor, to Chortov and to Anna Petrovna. At home and, if there is money, invite the two Volkonskys, Ozerov, and Sologub to dinner. To the Gypsies. Before dinner, after getting ready, to Gautier and to Chulkov. —
