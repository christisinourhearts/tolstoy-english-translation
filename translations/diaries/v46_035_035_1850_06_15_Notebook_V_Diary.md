15 June.

—Yesterday I carried out exactly everything I had planned.—For 15 June. From 4 <sup>1</sup> / <sub>2</sub> to 6 in the fields, estate management, bathe.—From 6 to 8 continue the diary. From 8 to 10 write the music method.—From 10 to 12 play the piano. From 12 to 6 breakfast, rest, and dinner. From 6 to 8 reading and rules, from 8 to 10 bathe and estate management.—
