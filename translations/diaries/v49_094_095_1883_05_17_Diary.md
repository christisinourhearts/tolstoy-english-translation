<!-- vol. 49, p. 94 -->

<ins>17/29 May.</ins>

Late. Peasant girls on *pas de géants*.[^1] The pagan element, in them as in our own, struggles with the Christian and still has the upper hand. Beautiful, enticing, lustful. Wrote badly. Went on horseback to Bibikov’s. Two petitioners. A soldier asking for assistance, and a man from Telyatinki—the district police officer had taken a stray horse from him.

Bibikov threw me off by saying that Seryozha would come. Went after dinner. At home dozed off, then tried to talk with my wife. At least without malice. Yesterday I lay and prayed that God would turn her. And I thought, what an absurdity this is. I lie beside <!-- vol. 49, p. 95 --> her and say nothing, and God is supposed to talk with her for me. If I do not know how to turn her where I need her to go, then who will know how?

Wrote a letter to Krasnova.

## Notes

[^1]: *giant steps.*
