18 December.

Get up at 9 <sup>1</sup> / <sub>2</sub>, read until 10 <sup>1</sup> / <sub>2</sub>, write until 12 and receive Volkonsky; from 12 to 2 loaf about; write and write until evening. Dine at home. —
