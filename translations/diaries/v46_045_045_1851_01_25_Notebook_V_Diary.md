25 January.

Fell in love, or imagined that I had fallen in love; was at an evening party and lost my head. Bought a horse that I do not need at all. Rules. — Do not offer any price for a thing you do not need. When you enter a ball, immediately ask someone to dance and make a round of the waltz or polka. — This evening think over means of putting my affairs right. — Stay home. —
