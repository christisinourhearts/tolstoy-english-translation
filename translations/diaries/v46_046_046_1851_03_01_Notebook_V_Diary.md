1 March.

R[ule]: In difficult cases always act according to the first impress[ion]. Get up at 8 <sup>1</sup> / <sub>2</sub>, study until 12.[^1] From 12[^2] to 1 music, from 1 to 2[^3] studies, from 2 <sup>1</sup> / <sub>2</sub> to 6 rest. — Do not seek out acquaintances; evening at home, studies. —
