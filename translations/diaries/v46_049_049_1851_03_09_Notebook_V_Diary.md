9 March.

[^1] Did not get up for a long time — (lack of energy). Letter to Islavin (inattention). Went out in bad gloves and without a fur coat (thoughtlessness and haste). Told Panin about my building (desire to show off). At Olivier’s and Beer’s — (indecision and cowardice). — At the Gorchakovs’ — (false shame and desire to show off). Went to Kireevskaya (groundlessness) so (cowardice). Occupations for the 10th. Get up at 8. From 8 to 9 letters, from 9 to 11 music, 11 to 1 fencing. 1 to 2 <sup>1</sup> / <sub>2</sub> the Anikeevs and walking, gymnastics, dine at Lvov’s. — Evening read and diary. —

## Notes
