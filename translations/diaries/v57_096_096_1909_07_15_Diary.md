15 July.

Woke early and kept thinking about a prayer for Sonichka. Wrote it and sent it, but it is not good. A letter from Chertkov. Worked on the letter about Science. I am nearing the end.

Rode horseback. And again several times, both at home and while riding, when something seemed unpleasant, I remembered that I live not before people but before God, and it passed at once. How deeply, though, concern about people's judgment has lodged in me. It now stands more than anything else in my way to God. — (Yes, wrote a little letter about the organization of communities.) I have reached, thank God, the point where in my thoughts, alone with myself, I can overcome concern about people's judgment through the awareness of living before God, but I cannot get used to remembering this in every interaction with people. I will learn and train myself. —

Going to dinner.
