11 March.

— Wrote a letter well, a little hastily; did gymnastics hastily. — At Islenyev’s little dignité[^1] and at the riding school too (distraction). — Waited for dinner, cowardice. With Begichev was frank, wanted to show off. With Mikhalkov cowardice, desire to show off, and failure to carry out the rules. — [Occupations for] the 12th. From 10 to 11 arrangements concerning the horses[^2] and Poiret. From 11[^3] to 3 concert. Volkonsky. Dinner. — In the evening excerpts and read. — Concert and read, go to bed early.

## Notes

[^1]: *dignity*.
[^2]: Deleted: *11 to 3 concert*.
[^3]: 12
