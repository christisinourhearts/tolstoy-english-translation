<!-- vol. 46, p. 28 -->

(8 (April. 6 o'clock in the morning).

Hope is an evil for the happy and a good for the unhappy. —

Although I have already gained [^1] much since I began working on myself, I am still very dissatisfied with myself. — The farther one advances in self-improvement, the more faults one sees in oneself; Socrates spoke truly when he said that the highest degree of human perfection is to know that one knows nothing. —

## Notes

[^1]: gained
