21 December

From 8 to 10 write; from 10 to 2 get money and fence. From 2 to 6 dine somewhere; from 6 until night write at home and receive no one. At Lvov’s find out about Seryozha’s service. To Countess Avdotya Maksimovna. — —

Do not read novels. —
