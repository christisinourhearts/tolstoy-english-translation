<!-- vol. 46, p. 41 -->

17 December.[^1]

<!-- vol. 46, p. 42 --> Get up early and work on the letter to Dyakov and the story; at 10 o’clock go to liturgy at the Zachatievsky Monastery and to Anna[^2] Petrovna, to Yakovleva. From there stop at Koloshin’s, send for sheet music, prepare a letter to the office, dine at home, work on music and the rules; in the evening to the girls and to the club. On the 18th be at the Council, at Lvov’s, at Evreinov’s, at Prince Andrei Ivanovich’s, and ask about a position. — General rules. Go to bed at 12 when nothing necessary keeps me up, and get up at 8; every day spend 4 hours seriously on music. —

## Notes

[^1]: Deleted: *in the morning*.
