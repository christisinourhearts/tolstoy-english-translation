<!-- vol. 46, p. 42 -->

20 December

At 10 to Volkonsky’s; from 11 until 2, letters and the story.[^1] Until 3 <sup>1</sup> / <sub>4</sub>, music; until 9 at the Dyakovs’; at home write about music. It is already 11 o’clock in the evening, and I have written nothing and am displeased with myself for becoming embarrassed at the Dyakovs’. —

## Notes

[^1]: Deleted: *At 3*.
