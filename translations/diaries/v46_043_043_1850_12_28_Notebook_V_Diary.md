<!-- vol. 46, p. 43 -->

28 December.

Very dissatisfied with myself; chiefly because I was unwell;[^1] today follow the following rule: if I am ill, I may fail to do what was planned, but I must not do anything else either. Go to the Gorchakovs, reread the diary, and do what was left undone. Go to Neskuchnoye and pay court to the princess. Toward evening go with Nikolai Gorchakov to the Gypsies and pack. —
