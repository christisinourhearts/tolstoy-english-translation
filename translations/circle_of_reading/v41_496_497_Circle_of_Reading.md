<!-- vol. 41, p. 496 -->

14 July

The kingdom of God is the realization among people of the law of God in the measure in which it has been revealed to them.

1

> Where will poverty go if each person seeks first of all the kingdom of God and his justice? That is, if each, voluntarily submitting to the law of God, strives for the conscientious fulfillment of the duties imposed by this law?

> Poverty is the daughter of injustice, of covetousness, of criminal contempt for the sacred duties of mankind—so general and constant a violation of them that we, through a terrible darkening of our conscience, have even grown used to regarding poverty as a necessary condition of the order of life. And so, may your kingdom come, Lord; may your law be the law of the renewal of the world; may nakedness not be the lot of three quarters of the human race; may the world be not the dwelling place of embittered enemies who harm one another, but the dwelling place of brothers straining to help one another. Multiplying daily, may the sons of God unite for the destruction of evil, for the overthrow of the temple of Satan, and for the building of your temple from its ruins.

> *Lamennais.*

2

> Only then can it be said with full grounds that the kingdom of God has come to us, when the necessity of the transition of church faith into a universal rational religion is openly acknowledged. Let the full realization of this kingdom be infinitely far from us; but in this establishment of a universal rational religion in place of church faiths, as in a germ that develops and then multiplies, there is already contained everything that is to enlighten the world and take possession of it.

> In the life of the world a thousand years are as one day. We must patiently work at this realization and wait for it.

> *Kant.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 497 -->

3

> The kingdom of God on earth—this is the final goal and desire of mankind (“Thy kingdom come”). Christ brought this kingdom near to us, but people did not understand him and set up among us the kingdom of priests and not the kingdom of God.

> *Kant.*

4

> The time is coming when ritual, verbal divine service, which draws people to itself by its poetry and splendor, and the violent social order, considered an inevitable condition of life, will be displaced by the understanding of the life of man. The time is coming of the kingdom of heaven, the kingdom of God on earth, when our very life in our deeds will be wholly filled with the conscious fulfillment of the law of God.

> One main thing is required: it is required to understand religion in its true meaning: not in the sense of sorcery and fooling people, but in the sense of true science, the understanding of the life of man—to understand it so that by divine service one means not something mysterious, supernatural, which cannot be done without a priest, without grace, but understands by divine service love for God and one's neighbor, service to one's neighbor, activity for the good of one's neighbor, for the common good—so that one understands by divine service the doing of good.

> *Buka.*

*The kingdom of God is within you. And therefore seek the kingdom of God in yourselves, and all the rest will come about just as we could only wish.*
