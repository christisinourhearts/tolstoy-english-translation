12 March

A person's deeds are his life. And his deeds become his fate, good or bad. This is the law of our life, and therefore what is most important of all in the world for a person is what he is doing now.

*The Indian Agni Purana.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 164 -->

1

The Persians have this story:

After death a soul was flying to heaven, and then a terrible woman appeared, coming to meet it, hideous, filthy, all covered with festering sores. “Why are you here, so repulsive and vile, worse than any devil?” asked the soul. “Who are you?” The terrible woman answered:

— I am your deeds.

2

> To do good deeds, to be merciful, meek, humble, to speak good words, to wish good to others, to have a pure heart, always to learn, always to speak the truth, to restrain anger, to be patient and content, to be friendly, modest, respectful to the old, to honor parents and teachers—all these are friends of the good and enemies of the evil.

> To speak untruth, to steal, to look at women with an impure gaze, to deceive, to curse, to wish evil to one's neighbor, to be proud, idle, a slanderer, stingy, disrespectful, shameless, hot-tempered, to take what is another's, to be vengeful, stubborn, envious, to do evil to one's neighbor, to be superstitious—all these are friends of evil and enemies of good.

> *The Persian Catechism.*

3

> *What matters is not reasoning about a good life, but good deeds.*

> *The Talmud.*

4

> Never put off a good deed if you can do it today, because death does not sort out whether a person has done or not done what he ought. Death waits for no one and for nothing. It has neither enemies nor friends.

> *The Indian Agni Purana.*

5

> When you came into the world, you cried, and all around you rejoiced; so act that when you are leaving the world, all will weep and you alone will smile.

> *Indian saying.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 165 -->

6

Only when you fulfill the truth that you know will a new truth be revealed to you.

*Lucy Mallory.*

However strong the influence of past deeds on the direction of life, a person can still always change it by an effort of the spirit.
