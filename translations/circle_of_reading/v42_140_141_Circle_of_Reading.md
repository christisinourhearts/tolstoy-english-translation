<!-- vol. 42, p. 140 -->

13 October

State organization—of whatever kind—is so contrary to the most basic tenets of Christianity that it is enough to say that people live the life of a state in order to say by this that people live a life wholly contrary to the teaching of Christ.

1

> Where great sages have power, the subjects do not notice their existence. Where sages who are not great rule, the people are attached to them and praise them. Where still lesser sages rule, the people fear them, and where still lesser, the people despise them.

> *Laozi.*

<!-- vol. 42, p. 141 -->

2

For an unawakened man, state power is certain sacred institutions that make up the organs of a living body, a necessary condition of people's life. For an awakened man, it is people who have gone very much astray, who ascribe to themselves some fantastic importance that has no reasonable justification whatever, and who carry their desires into effect by means of violence. All this, for the awakened man, is people who have gone astray and for the most part have been bribed, who do violence to other people, exactly like those robbers who seize people on the roads and do violence to them. The antiquity of this violence, the scale of the violence, its organization—cannot change the essence of the matter. For the awakened man there is no such thing as what is called the state; and therefore there is no justification for all the acts of violence committed in the name of the state; and therefore participation in them is impossible for him. State violence will be destroyed not by external means, but only by the consciousness of people awakened to the truth.

3

> Strength is in mutual love, weakness in mutual rivalry. Uniting in love—we stand firm; dividing in discord—we fall.

> *Lucy Mallory.*

4

It may be that for the former condition of people state violence was needed; it may be that it is needed even now; but people cannot help seeing, cannot help foreseeing, that condition in which violence can only hinder the peaceful life of people. And seeing and foreseeing this, people cannot help striving to bring about such an order. The means of bringing about this order is inner perfecting and nonparticipation in violence.

*Try to live so that violence is unnecessary to you.*
