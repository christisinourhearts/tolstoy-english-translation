<!-- vol. 41, p. 393 -->

11 June

All the external changes of our life are insignificant in comparison with the changes that take place in our thoughts.

<!-- vol. 41, p. 394 -->

1

In order that a change may take place in a person's feelings and actions, a change must first of all take place in his thoughts. And in order that a change may take place in thoughts, a person must concentrate himself in the consciousness of his spiritual nature and its demands.

2

> The epochs of our life are determined not by the actions noticeable to us and performed by our will: marriage, getting a position, and the like, but by the thoughts that come during a walk, in the middle of the night, at a meal, especially by those thoughts that, embracing all our past, tell us: you acted thus, but it would have been better to act otherwise. And all our further actions, like slaves, serve these thoughts: they carry out their will.

> *Thoreau.*

3

> Every thought on which a person dwells, whether he expresses it or not, without fail either harms or helps his life.

> *Lucy Mallory.*

4

> *In order to avoid sins and overcome them, one must first of all recognize that the root of every sin is in bad thoughts.*

> *We are all only the consequences of what we think.*

> *The Buddha.*

5

> What determines a person's fate is how he understands himself.

> *Thoreau.*

6

> A disorderly thought does to our mind the same thing that a disorderly person whom we had invited to stay with us would do to our house.

> *Lucy Mallory.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 395 -->

7

We clearly see the changes taking place in the realm of material life: people traveled and hauled by horse—now by steam; they burned splinters, tallow—now gas, electricity; but we do not see the same changes in the spiritual world of people. And these changes are the most important.

We regret a lost purse with money in it, but a good thought that has come to us or that we have heard or read—a thought that, if we had remembered it and applied it to life, could have done much good—we lose by forgetting it, and we do not regret what is more precious than millions.
