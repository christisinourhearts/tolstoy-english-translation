<!-- vol. 42, p. 291 -->

29 November

A word is a deed.

1

> Never say what you do not feel. May your soul never be darkened by a lie.

> *From “Pious Thoughts.”*

2

Our enemies can be more useful to us than our friends, for friends often forgive us our weaknesses, whereas enemies usually note them and draw our attention to them.

Do not disregard the judgments of enemies.

3

> Even the most scrupulous vanity, if only it understands itself well, must notice that there is no less merit in letting oneself be convinced than in becoming convinced oneself, and that the first perhaps does a person even more true honor, because it requires more self-denial and self-education than the second.

> *Kant.*

4

> *A wise man does not ascribe importance to a man for his words, and does not disregard words merely because they are spoken by an insignificant man.*

> *Chinese wisdom.*

<!-- vol. 42, p. 292 -->

5

> Human language is a sufficient instrument for conveying the ideas that arise in the human mind; but in the realm of true and deep feeling it is only a weak translator.

> *Kossuth.*

6

> Every word has only the meaning in which the listener can take it. You will not make clear the meaning of honor to a dishonorable person, or of love to one who is a stranger to it. In trying to bring the meaning of these words down to their understanding, you will only arrive at having no more words to express either honor or love.

> *John Ruskin.*

No aims can ever justify a lie.
