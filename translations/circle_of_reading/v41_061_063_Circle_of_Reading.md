27 January

Love for people gives a true, inalienable inward good, uniting a person with other people and with God.

1

> No one can hinder a person's spiritual development except himself. Neither physical weakness nor mental incapacity can be an obstacle to the development of the spiritual nature, for the development of the spiritual nature lies only in the increase of love, and nothing can hinder this increase.

> *Lucy Mallory.*

2

*A reasonable person loves not because it is profitable to him, but because he finds good in love itself.*

3

> Do not regret the past; what use is regret? Falsehood says: grieve; truth says: only love. Put away from yourself all memories. Do not speak of what is past; live in the light of love, and let all the rest pass away.

> *Persian wisdom.*

4

A Chinese sage was asked: “What is learning?” He said: “To know people.” He was asked: “What is virtue?” He said: “To love people.”

<!-- vol. 41, p. 62 -->

5

> A person cannot attain happiness, because the higher his aspirations to worldly happiness, the less they can be realized. The fulfillment of duty cannot give happiness either. Its fulfillment gives peace, not happiness.

> Only divine, holy love and union with God give true good, because if sacrifice has become joy—constant, growing, unbroken joy—then the soul is assured of unceasing good.

> *Amiel.*

6

Try to love the one you did not love, whom you condemned, who insulted you. And if you succeed in doing this, you will feel an entirely new and wonderful feeling of joy. You will at once see in this person the same God who lives in you. And as light shines brighter after darkness, so in you too, when you free yourself from lack of love, the light of divine love will blaze up more strongly and joyfully.

7

> I am conscious in myself of a force that will in time transform the world. It does not push and does not press, but I feel how little by little and irresistibly it draws me. And I see that something attracts me, just as I unconsciously attract others. I draw them along, and they draw me along, and we are conscious of a striving toward a new union.

> And I asked that force which was in me: who are you?

> And it answered: I am love, the sovereign of heaven, and I want to be the sovereign of the earth.

> I am the mightiest of all the powers of heaven, and I have come to form the society of the future.

> *Crosby.*

8

> As a mother, at the risk of her own life, raises and guards her child, her only child, so let every person raise and guard in himself a friendly feeling toward all that lives.

> *Metta Sutta.*

The fearlessness, calm, and joy that love gives are so great that the worldly goods given by love (people's love) go unnoticed by a person who has known the inward good of love.
