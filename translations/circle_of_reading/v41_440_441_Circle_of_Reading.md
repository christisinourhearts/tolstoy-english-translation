<!-- vol. 41, p. 440 -->

26 June

Love shows a person the goal of his life; reason shows the means of fulfilling it.

1

> The sun pours out its light unceasingly over the whole world, but its light is not exhausted by this; in just the same way your reason must shine, spreading out in all directions. It flows everywhere without being exhausted, and when it meets an obstacle, it must show neither irritability nor anger, but calmly light up all that thirsts to receive it, without falling, without tiring, covering all that is turned toward the light and leaving in shadow only what itself turns away from its face.

> *Marcus Aurelius.*

2

> Compared with the world surrounding him, man is no more than a weak reed; but he is a reed endowed with understanding.

> All our advantage lies in our ability to understand. Understanding alone elevates us above the rest of the world. Let us then value and sustain our understanding, and it will light up our whole life for us, will show us where good lies and where evil.

> *Pascal.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 441 --> Some trifle is enough to kill a man. And yet man is higher than all creatures, higher than everything earthly, because even in dying he will be conscious with his reason that he is dying. Man can become conscious of the insignificance of his body before nature. But nature is conscious of nothing.

3

*Man differs from the animals only by his reason. Some develop it, but many neglect it: just as if they wished to renounce what distinguishes them from the beasts.*

*Eastern wisdom.*

I glorify Christianity because it widens, strengthens, and elevates my rational nature. If I could not remain rational while being a Christian, I would not hesitate in my choice. I feel myself obliged to sacrifice property, fame, and life for the sake of Christianity, but for no religion must I sacrifice that reason which elevates me above the animal and makes me a man. I know of no greater sacrilege than the renunciation of the highest faculty received from God. In acting so, we set our bodily nature against the divine principle that lives in us. Reason is the highest expression of our thinking nature. It corresponds to the unity of God and the universe and strives to make the soul a reflection, a mirror, of the highest unity.

*Channing.*

If a person had no reason, he could not distinguish good from bad, could not seek the true good and possess it.
