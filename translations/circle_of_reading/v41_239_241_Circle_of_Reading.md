<!-- vol. 41, p. 239 -->

11 April

In the moral world everything is connected still more closely than in the bodily one. Every deception draws after it a series of deceptions, every cruelty—a series of cruelties.

1

> If a person has once violated a light commandment, then in the end he will not stop before violating a weighty one. If he has transgressed the commandment: “Love your neighbor as yourself,” then afterward he will also violate the prohibitions: do not take revenge, and do not bear malice, and do not hate your brother—and will come at last to the shedding of blood.

> *The Talmud.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 240 -->

2

> Often people pride themselves on the purity of their conscience only because they have a short memory.

> *Zhonizad Rafezsky.*

3

> Let a person not think lightly of evil, saying in his heart: surely it will not touch me. A water vessel is filled by small drops; the fool is wholly filled with evil, doing evil little by little.

> Let a person not think carelessly of good, saying in his heart: there is no strength in me to receive good. As drop by drop water fills a vessel, so too, little by little, doing good, a person who strives toward the good is wholly filled with good.

> *Buddhist wisdom. Dhammapada.*

4

> There are vices in us that are held up only by our other vices and that vanish when we destroy the basic vices, as branches fall if the trunk is cut through.

> *Pascal.*

5

> Destroy one vice, and ten will vanish.

> *Rod.*

6

Conscience is the indication of the path along which one must go. People stray from this path, and then they do one of two things: either they direct their life along the path of conscience, or they hide the indications of their conscience from themselves. For the first there is only one means—the increase of the light in oneself and attention to what it illuminates; for the second—for hiding the indications of conscience from oneself—there are two means: an external one and an internal one. The external means consists in occupations that distract attention from the indications of conscience; the internal one consists in the darkening of conscience itself. Fear this. Only step off the path of good, and before you have time to come to your senses, you will be stuck fast in evil.

*Watch for the birth of evil. There is a voice of the soul that points out this birth: one feels awkward, ashamed. Trust this voice. Stop and search, and you will find the deception being born.*
