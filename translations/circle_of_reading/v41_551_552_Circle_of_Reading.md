<!-- vol. 41, p. 551 -->

5 August

Most false and harmful opinions are spread and kept up by suggestion.

1

We are inclined to adopt the views and thoughts that are shared by the people who live with us, and do not try to develop them further and deeper. From this comes the insignificance of our characters and our lives.

2

The soul yields to the influence of another soul. And a person is fully free only when alone.

<!-- vol. 41, p. 552 -->

3

> It is easy to live in the world following public opinion, and in solitude following only your own. Strong is the person who in the crowd keeps the meekness and independence of solitude.

> *Emerson.*

4

Blessed is the man who has not gone to the council of the wicked, and has not stood in the way of sinners, and has not sat in the assembly of blasphemers (Psalm 1:1). There is nothing more contagious than example. It brings into our life such acts as we would never have thought of committing without the influence of example.

5

> *Do not yield to the state of mind of the one who offends you, and do not set out on the path onto which he would like to draw you.*

> *Marcus Aurelius.*

6

A person can get used to the worst life, especially if he sees that all those around him live the same way.

7

> I am ashamed to think how often I have compliantly sacrificed my convictions and how easily I have submitted to dead institutions and customs.

> *Emerson.*

8

False notions and harmful moods that are spread by suggestion can be recognized by the brilliance and solemnity with which they are surrounded. Truth does not need outward trappings.

Suggestion is a necessary condition of social life, but one cannot be careful enough in its conscious use. The capacity for suggestion compels a moral person to be doubly strict with the words and acts that may affect others.
