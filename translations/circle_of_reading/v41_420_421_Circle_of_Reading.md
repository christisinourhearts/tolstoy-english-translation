<!-- vol. 41, p. 420 -->

20 June

There was a time when people ate human flesh and found nothing bad in it. And even now there are such savage people. People stopped eating human flesh little by little. In the same way, people are now little by little ceasing to eat the flesh of animals, *and very soon the time will come when people will feel the same revulsion toward the flesh of animals that they now feel toward human flesh.*

*After Lamartine.*

1

> Just as it is now considered vile and disgraceful to abandon children, to stage gladiatorial fights, to torture prisoners, and to commit other atrocities that formerly seemed to no one either reprehensible or contrary to the sense of justice, so the time is approaching when it will be considered immoral and impermissible to kill animals and to use their corpses as food.

> *Dr. Zimmerman.*

2

If you see children tormenting a kitten or a little bird for their amusement, you stop them and teach them pity for living creatures, while you yourselves go hunting, to pigeon shooting, to the races, and sit down to a dinner for which several living creatures have been killed, that is, you do the very thing from which you restrain the children.

Can it be that this glaring contradiction will not become evident and will not stop people?

3

> Abstinence from meat is spreading more and more. There is hardly a city of any size now in which there are not from one to a dozen or more vegetarian dining rooms, where food is prepared without meat.

> *Lucy Mallory.*

4

“We cannot claim rights over the animals that live on land, which feed on the same food, breathe the same air, drink the same water as we do; when they are being killed <!-- vol. 41, p. 421 --> they trouble us with their terrifying cries and make us ashamed of our deed.”

So thought Plutarch, for some reason excepting water animals. We, however, with respect to earth-born animals, have fallen far behind him.

5

> Do not raise your hand against your brother, and do not shed the blood of any living creatures that inhabit the earth—neither people, nor domestic animals, nor wild beasts, nor birds; in the depth of your soul a prophetic voice forbids you to shed blood, for in it is life, and life you cannot give back.

> *Lamartine.*

In our time, when the criminality of killing animals for pleasure or for taste is clear, hunting and meat-eating are no longer indifferent acts but plainly bad ones, which bring after them, like every bad act consciously committed, many acts still worse.
