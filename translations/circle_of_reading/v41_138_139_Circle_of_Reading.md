<!-- vol. 41, p. 138 -->

29 February

In order to walk, you must know where you are going. The same is needed in order to live reasonably and well. You must know where life is leading, both mine and that of all people.

1

> *Perfection is proper to God. To desire the perfect is proper to man.*

> *Goethe.*

2

> Life is not given in order to make merry without laboring. No, life is a struggle and a march. The struggle of good with evil, of justice with injustice, of freedom with oppression, of love with the lusts of personality; life is the procession of our *I* toward the realization of that ideal which shines like a glimmering dawn to our mind and our heart.

> *Giuseppe Mazzini.*

3

We all know that we do not live as we should, and not as we could live. It is this very thought, that life can and must be better, that one must never abandon. But one must remember it not only in order to condemn the present life, but in order to establish a better life. One must believe that life must be better than the present one, and live so that it can become better.

<!-- vol. 41, p. 139 -->

4

People often say: “Man is weak, you won't become a saint, there's no use even trying, so let's live like everyone.” There is a great error in these words. One must try to live well not in order to become a saint, but in order to become better than one was. In this is the main business of the life of all people. And in this is the good both of each person and of all humanity.

5

The ideal is in yourself. The obstacles to attaining it are in you too. Your situation is the material out of which you must realize this ideal.

*Carlyle.*

6

Perfection is perfection only when its realization is possible only in thought, when it appears attainable only in infinity, and when therefore the possibility of approaching it is infinite.

To hope and believe that the good of which we are conscious will be realized both in us and in the world is the chief condition of the possibility of its realization. But not to believe this, and to think that we will always be as bad as we are now, and that all people will always live as badly as they live now, is the chief obstacle to approaching its realization.
