<!-- vol. 41, p. 355 -->

30 May

Land can no more be an object of buying and selling than a man's person can. The selling and buying of land is only a concealed selling of the person.

1

> The essence of slavery consists in granting to one man the power to take the labor of another without recompensing him. Private property in land grants this power to the same degree as the right of property in a slave. The slaveholder must leave his slave, out of what is obtained by his labor, <!-- vol. 41, p. 356 --> as much as he needs for life. And does the huge mass of workers in the so-called free countries really receive more than this?

> *Henry George.*

2

> The earth is a solemn gift that nature has made to man; *the birth of each person is a right to the possession of the earth.* This right is as natural as a child's right to its mother's breast.

> *Marmontel.*

3

> Since I am born for the earth, the earth is given to me so that I may take from it what I need for tilling and planting, and I have the right to demand my share for myself.

> *Emerson.*

4

> A man in our society cannot sleep without paying for the place on which he sleeps. Air, water, and sunlight belong to him only on the highway. The only right recognized for him by law is to walk along this highway until he staggers from weariness, because he cannot stop but must walk.

> *Grant Allen.*

5

> The bodies of men and women, and still more their souls, must not be bought or sold, nor must land, water, air, because these things are necessary conditions for sustaining people's bodies and souls.

> *John Ruskin.*

6

*One cannot without great sin take part in the selling, buying, securing, managing of landed property.*

People strive in life not to do what they consider good, but to call as many things as possible their own.
