<!-- vol. 41, p. 441 -->

27 June

A good life is given only to the one who strives for it without ceasing.

<!-- vol. 41, p. 442 -->

1

In order to attain a good life, one must not disdain any good deed. No less strength is needed for small good deeds than for the very greatest and most resounding good deed.

2

> The kingdom of heaven is taken by force, and those who use effort seize it.

> *Matthew 11:12.*

3

If a person knows what virtue consists in and does not do what it requires of him, then he does the same as a traveler would if, knowing that only by continuing on his way will he find shelter and food, he stopped and waited for the shelter to come to him of itself.

4

> *In order not to spill a full vessel, one must carefully hold it straight.*

> *In order for a blade to be sharp, one must constantly whet it.*

> *So it is with your soul too, if you seek the true good.*

> *Laozi.*

5

> If there is anything great and good for you, it will not appear to you at the first or second call, it will not appear to you easily, without labor and effort.

> *Emerson.*

6

> Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and they will open to you. For everyone who asks receives, and he who seeks finds, and to him who knocks they will open.

> *Matthew 7:7–8.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 443 -->

7

Try to live the life most in keeping with virtue, — said Pythagoras. It may be the most difficult, but as one grows accustomed to it, it becomes the most joyful.

8

> To animals God gave everything they need. But to man he did not give this; man must himself obtain everything that is necessary to him. Man's highest wisdom was not born together with him; he must labor to attain it, and the greater his labors, the greater the reward. He cannot draw near to perfect wisdom unless he makes a great effort.

> *Tablets of the Babis.*

If you want the good, fulfill the law of God. And the fulfillment of the law of God is possible only through effort. Effort is not only rewarded by a joyful life, but effort itself gives the greatest good of life.
