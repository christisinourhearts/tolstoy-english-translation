<!-- vol. 41, p. 259 -->

15 April

The consequences of our actions can never be accessible to us, because the consequences of our actions in an infinite world appear to us infinite.

1

> *Our actions are ours, but their consequences are the business of heaven.*

> *Francis.*

2

> You are a day laborer; work out your day and receive your day's wage.

> *The Talmud.*

3

> Vain are the efforts of people to penetrate the mystery of God's being: their business is only to keep his law.

> *The Talmud.*

4

> Do your duty, and leave the consequences to the one who laid it on you.

> *The Talmud.*

5

> The results of your deeds will be judged by others; take care only that your heart now, at the present minute, be pure and truthful.

> *After John Ruskin.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 260 -->

6

> The holy man takes care of the inner and not of the outer; he disregards the outer and chooses the inner.

> *Laozi.*

7

> One of the definite conditions of human labor consists in this: that the more distant the goal of our strivings, the less we desire to see the fruits of our labors ourselves, the greater and wider will be the measure of our success.

> *John Ruskin.*

8

The deeds of a person that are most important and needed for himself and for others are those whose consequences he will not see.

9

> Every human act is the more honorable, better, and more magnificent, the more distant its consequences.

> *John Ruskin.*

10

An act performed without any considerations of any consequences whatever, with a view only to the fulfillment of the will of God, is the best act that can be performed by a person.

11

> In the world, like gunpowder in a mine, enormous deposits of evil and untruth are hidden. When we happen to make new contributions of the same evil and lies to this mine, then apparently we do not by this disturb the general calm and equilibrium of human common life; but when, as our contribution to the mine, we bring not evil and lies but good and truth, then good and truth, like sparks, explode the powder of evil and lies, and evil and lies are revealed, become manifest.

> To refrain from doing good to people and to continue taking part in maintaining the reigning untruth only in order to avoid <!-- vol. 41, p. 261 --> an explosion of the powder in the mine means not to understand the significance of the explosion of the powder, which only discharges the accumulated evil and thereby does not increase but decreases its quantity.

> Christ, who himself acknowledged that by his teaching he brought not peace but a sword and division on earth, was not troubled by the evil that he thus called out to the surface, but rejoiced at the open collision of good with evil and of light with darkness, which is what must bring the open triumph of light and good.

> *Fyodor Strakhov.*

12

The life of Christ is especially important as an example of the impossibility for a person to see the fruits of his labors. And all the less, the more important the work. Moses could enter the promised land with his people, but Christ could in no way have seen the fruits of his teaching if he had lived until now. But we want to do the work of God and to receive a human reward.

If you can see all the consequences of your activity, then know that this activity is insignificant.
