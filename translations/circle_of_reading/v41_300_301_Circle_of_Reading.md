<!-- vol. 41, p. 300 -->

4 May

Every thought expressed in words is a force whose action is limitless.

1

> One may be alone in one's private and temporary surroundings, but each of our thoughts and each of our feelings finds, has found, and will find its echo in humanity. For some people, whom the greater part of humanity recognizes as its leaders and enlighteners, this echo is enormous and resounds with particular force; but there is no person whose thoughts do not produce the same kind of effect on others, though many times smaller. Every sincere expression of the soul, every declaration of personal conviction serves someone or something—even if no one knows of it, and even when they clamp your mouth shut or throw a noose around your neck. A word spoken to someone keeps its indestructible effect and, like every motion, is transformed into other forms, but is not annihilated.

> *Amiel.*

2

> Good rules that come from a person's heart are as useful as good examples.

> *Seneca*

3

> The thoughts that you have and express turn in the end into a capacity to do good or evil, which in its development or growth comes back to you.

> *Lucy Mallory.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 301 -->

4

> Strong, briefly expressed thoughts contribute much to the improvement of life.

> *Cicero.*

5

> Innocence and childhood are sacred. The sower casting seeds, the father or mother who cast a fruitful word into a child's soul, are doing a sacred work and ought always to do it religiously, with reverence and prayer, for they are laboring for the kingdom of God. All sowing is a mysterious thing—whether the seed falls on the soil of the earth or into human souls. Every person is like a tiller of the soil; his whole task, if it is well understood, consists in cultivating life and sowing it everywhere; such is the calling of humanity, and this calling is holy. And the word is its chief instrument.

> We too often forget that the word is at one and the same time both a sowing and a revelation. The consequences of a word spoken at the right time are incalculable. Oh, how deep is the meaning of the word, but we are dull because we are corporeal! We see the stones and the trees along the sides of the road, the furnishings of our dwellings; we see everything that is matter. But we do not notice the throngs of invisible thoughts that fill the air and constantly beat their wings around each of us.

> *Amiel.*

6

> *Thought is a reasonable living force which, going out from a person, does either the work of a curse or the work of a blessing, according to its quality.*

> *Lucy Mallory.*

7

Truth expressed in words is the mightiest force in the life of people. We are not conscious of this force only because its consequences do not show themselves at once.

Make use of the good thoughts of people, and if you cannot repay them in the same way, then at least do not spread your own or other people's unclear, and therefore false, thoughts.
