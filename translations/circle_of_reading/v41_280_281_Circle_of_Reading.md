<!-- vol. 41, p. 280 -->

24 April

True courage in struggle is proper only to him who knows that his ally is God.

1

> In the world you will have sorrow; but take courage: I have overcome the world.

> *John 16:33.*

2

> Strive for the truth until death—and God will fight for you.

> *Jesus, son of Sirach.*

3

> Happy is he who, having rejected the motives and reasons by which humanity ordinarily acts, resolves to have trust in himself. High must be the soul, firm the will, clear the gaze of him who can replace for himself society, custom, <!-- vol. 41, p. 281 --> ordinances, and bring himself to the point where inner conviction alone has over him the same force that “iron necessity” has for others.

> *Emerson.*

4

*Whatever happens, do not lose heart. Nothing bad that is not proper to you as a man can happen.*

5

> All is uncertain, misty, and fleeting; virtue alone is both clear and cannot be crushed by any violence.

> *Cicero.*

6

A person who renounces his personality is mighty, because personality concealed God in him. As soon as he has cast off personality, it is no longer he who acts in him, but God.

7

Once a Roman empress lost her jewels. Then the following was proclaimed throughout the empire: “Whoever finds and returns the loss within thirty days will receive a rich reward; but if anyone returns them after thirty days, he will be put to death.” The Jewish rabbi Samuel soon found the lost jewels, but returned them after the thirty days had expired. “Were you abroad?” the empress asked him. “No, I was at home,” he answered. “But perhaps you did not know about what had been proclaimed?”—“No, I knew,” said Samuel. “Then why did you not return them before the thirty days expired? Why, you are liable to the death penalty.”—

“I wanted to show you,” said Samuel, “that it was not out of fear of punishment that I returned your loss to you, but out of fear of the Lord God.”

Do not wait for the accomplishment of that work of God which you serve, but know that every effort of yours will not remain fruitless and moves the work forward.
