<!-- vol. 41, p. 475 -->

7 July

To deny God means to deny oneself as a spiritual, rational being.

1

God and the soul I know not by way of definition, but by a completely different way. Definition destroys this knowledge in me. I know without doubt that God exists and that my soul exists. But this knowledge is indubitable for me only because I am inevitably led to it. To the certainty of the knowledge of God I am led by the question: where did I come from? To the knowledge of the soul I am led by the question: what am I?

Where did I come from?

I was born of my mother, and she of my grandmother, of my great-grandmother, and the very last one, of whom? And I inevitably come to God.

Who am I?

Legs are not I, arms are not I, head is not I, feelings are not I, even thoughts are not I. What, then, am I?

I am I—my soul.

From whatever side I come to God, it will be the same: the source of my thought, of my reason—God; the source of my love—he too; the source of materiality—he too.

<!-- vol. 41, p. 476 --> The same with the concept of the soul. Whether I turn to my striving for truth, I know that the striving for truth is the immaterial foundation of me—my soul; whether I turn to the feeling of my love for good, I likewise find the cause of this love in my soul.

2

The most unbelieving person, whether he wants to or not, acknowledges God. He cannot help acknowledging that there is a law of his life—a law that he can obey or from which he can turn aside. And it is just this acknowledgment of the higher law of his life, inaccessible to man and known to him, that is God, or at least a manifestation of him.

3

> God manifests himself in the best thoughts, in truthfulness of speech, in sincerity of action, and by his spirit gives well-being and eternity to the world.

> *Zend-Avesta.*

4

> God exists. We ought not to prove this, and we have no need to. Every attempt to prove his existence is already blasphemy; and every denial of him is madness. God lives in our conscience, in the consciousness of humanity, in the universe that surrounds us. Our consciousness, our conscience calls to him in all the most solemn moments of grief and joy. Only a very pitiful or a very criminal person can deny God under the vault of the starry sky of night, at the coffin of dear ones, or at the execution of a martyr.

> *Mazzini.*

5

The life of the world takes place by someone's will—someone is doing some work of his own by means of this life of the whole world and of our lives. The one who does this is what we call God.

*People do not believe in God only when they believe a lie that is passed off as God.*
