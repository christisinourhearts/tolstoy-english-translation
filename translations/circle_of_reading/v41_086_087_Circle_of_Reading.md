<!-- vol. 41, p. 86 -->

5 February

Everything that is accomplished in the life of an individual person and of human societies had its beginning in thought. And therefore the explanation of all that happens to people is not in preceding events, but in the thoughts that preceded the events.

1

It is perhaps more important to know what one should not think about than what one should think about.

2

> Our life is the consequence of our thoughts; it proceeds from our thoughts. If a person speaks or acts with an evil thought—suffering follows him unfailingly, as the wheel follows the feet of the ox that draws the cart.

> Our life is the consequence of our thoughts; it is born in our heart, it is created by our thought. If a person speaks or acts with a good thought—joy follows him like a shadow that never leaves.

> *Buddhist wisdom. Dhammapada.*

3

> A person will not change because his dwelling is whitewashed. The good of the people will not increase because they are given the possibility of greater pleasures and material comforts. The soul creates its body. Only thought arranges a dwelling worthy of itself.

> *Mazzini.*

4

> Our habitual thoughts give, in our mind, their own coloring to everything with which we come into contact. Let these thoughts be false—and they will distort the most exalted truths. The atmosphere created around us by our habitual thoughts is, for each of us, something more solid than the house in which we live. It is something like the shell of a snail, which the snail carries everywhere with it.

> *Lucy Mallory.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 87 -->

5

> Our thought, good or bad, sends us to paradise or to hell, not in heaven and not under the earth, but here, in this life.

> *Lucy Mallory.*

6

*Thought seems free, but there is something in man more powerful than it, which is able to govern it.*

In order to change the established course of life in oneself or in people, one must struggle not with events, but with the thoughts that have produced and are producing them.
