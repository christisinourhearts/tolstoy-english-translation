<!-- vol. 42, p. 107 -->

30 September

The more solitary a person is, the more clearly he hears the voice of God, which is always calling him.

1

Silentium! [^1]

Be silent, hide yourself, and conceal

Both your feelings and your dreams!

Let them in the depths of your soul

Both rise and set,

Like clear stars in the night:

Admire them and be silent!

How can the heart express itself?

How can another understand you?

Will he understand what you live by?

A thought uttered is a lie.

Digging, you will muddy the springs:

Feed on them and be silent!

Only know how to live within yourself:

There is a whole world in your soul

Of mysteriously magical thoughts;

The outside noise will drown them out,

The rays of day will blind them:

Listen to their singing and be silent.

*Tyutchev.*

*2*

By the mere fact that a good intention has been expressed, the desire to carry it out is already weakened. But how can one keep the nobly self-satisfied impulses of youth from being expressed? Only much later, remembering them, do you regret them, as over a flower you could not hold back from—you plucked it before it opened and then saw it on the ground, withered and trampled.

3

> In the important questions of life we are always alone, and our real story can almost never be understood by others. The best part of the drama that takes place in our soul is a monologue, or rather an intimate deliberation between God, our conscience, and ourselves.

> *Amiel.*

<!-- vol. 42, p. 108 -->

4

Pascal says: man must die alone. And man must live in the same way. In what is most important in life, man is always alone, that is, not with people but with God.

5

*It is good for that person who is needed by others, but who has no need of a companion.*

6

A depraved person is always bound up with people in life, but feels himself the more lonely in his consciousness the more depraved he is. A good and reasonable person, on the contrary, often feels lonely among people, but in return is conscious of his unceasing union with humanity when he is alone.

Temporary detachment from everything worldly and contemplation within oneself of one's divine essence is just as necessary a nourishment of the soul for life as food is for the body.

## Notes

[^1]: Be silent!
