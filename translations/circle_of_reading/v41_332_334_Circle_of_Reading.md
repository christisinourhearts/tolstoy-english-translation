<!-- vol. 41, p. 332 -->

19 May

The basis of all faiths is one and the same.

<!-- vol. 41, p. 333 -->

1

There is one undoubted manifestation of the deity: the laws of good, which a person feels within himself and in acknowledging which he does not so much unite as he is, willingly or unwillingly, united with other people.

2

It only seems that humanity is occupied with trade, treaties, wars, sciences, arts; one thing only is important for it, and one thing only does it do—it clarifies for itself the moral laws by which it lives. And this clarification of the moral law is not only the chief but the only work of all humanity.

3

> A sage was asked: is there a word that one could carry out for one's own good to the end of one's life?

> The sage said: there is the word *shu*; the meaning of this word is this: what we do not want done to us, we must not do to others.

> *Chinese wisdom.*

4

“This commandment, which I command you today, is not inaccessible to you and not far off. It is not in heaven, that one could say: who would go up to heaven for us, and get it for us, and proclaim it to us, and we would carry it out? And it is not beyond the sea, that one could say: who would go across the sea for us, and get it for us, and proclaim it to us, and we would carry it out? But this word is very near to you: it is in your mouth and in your heart, that you may carry it out.”

*Deuteronomy 30:11–14.*

So it was written in a Hebrew book more than 2000 years before us.

5

> *Act so that you could say to everyone: act as I do.*

> *After Kant.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 334 -->

6

> The source of our duties is in God. The definition of our duties is contained in his law. To reveal and apply this law more and more—such is the task of humanity.

> *Giuseppe Mazzini.*

7

> The reasonableness observed in nature, which moves man to do what he ought and holds him back from what is bad, becomes law not because it is written down in books, but because it is a law just as eternal and divine as human reason. And therefore the true, unalterable law, which commands and forbids, is the reason of the highest being.

> *Cicero.*

In every encounter with people, remember the law of reciprocity: to act toward others as you want others to act toward you. This can become a habit.
