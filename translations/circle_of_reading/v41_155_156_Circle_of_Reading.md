<!-- vol. 41, p. 155 -->

8 March

Prayer is a reminder to oneself of one's relation to the infinite, to God.

1

> Life confuses us, irritates us, scatters our thoughts. That is just why prayer is so useful for the soul. Prayer is a strengthening medicine; it gives us back peace and courage. It reminds us of our sins, of our duty to forgive all; it says to us: “You are loved—love; you have received—give; you must die—do your work; overcome anger with magnanimity, evil with good. What does the false judgment of people about you matter. You are not obliged either to please them or to have success. Do what you ought; let be what will be. Your witness is your conscience, and your conscience is God, who speaks in you. Remember and refresh all this in yourself—in this is prayer.”

> *Amiel.*

2

> Know that we pray to God and express our desires before him not because his will is subject to change, but because, in turning to him, we thereby acknowledge him, and in acknowledging his power our soul is purified and exalted.

> *The Talmud.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 156 -->

3

Prayer is addressed to a personal God not because God is personal (I even know for certain that he is not personal, because personality is limitedness, and God is boundless), but because I am a personal being.

I have a little green glass over my eye, and I see everything green; I cannot help seeing the world green, although I know that it is not so.

4

> Prayer is the clarification of one's relation to the source of all; it is the clarification of one's bond with people, of one's duties to them as to children of the same father as ourselves, the settling of accounts with oneself for all one's actions, and the consideration of one's dark past in order to guard oneself in the future from the errors of the past.

> *The Talmud.*

5

It is good to pray at a set time. But if you cannot collect your thoughts, better not pray; do not repeat words with your tongue alone.

6

*Prayer in solitude is good and needed, but most needed of all is prayer amid the noise of people, when you are agitated, carried away, irritated. To remember your soul and God at such moments—in this is the most needed and the best prayer.*

Do not think that you can please God by prayer and not by obedience to him. Prayer is only a reminder to yourself of what you are and of what the work of your life consists in.
