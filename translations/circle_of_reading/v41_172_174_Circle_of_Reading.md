17 March

Salvation from the bad arrangement of the world lies in one thing only—in the spreading of true faith among people.

1

> There has never been a single serious step in the work of improving human society without faith being the chief cause of that improvement. And therefore every teaching not founded on faith always has been and always will be powerless to improve the social order. It may perhaps succeed in creating beautiful forms, but these forms will forever lack that spark which Prometheus stole from heaven.

> *Mazzini.*

2

> “Seek first of all the kingdom of God and his justice, and all the rest will be added to you.” The first step toward a natural and healthy arrangement of society always lies in securing to all <!-- vol. 41, p. 173 --> people their natural, equal, and inalienable rights to the material world. To achieve this does not mean to do everything necessary, but it does mean to make everything else easier. And until this is achieved, nothing else will bring any benefit.

> *Henry George.*

3

> *Society cannot exist without a common faith and a common purpose; social activity must consist in applying to life the principles established by religion.*

> *Mazzini.*

4

> The apostles lived so that all had one heart and one soul. And if they had been at odds among themselves, no one would have known of the faith of Christ. Why, even now the heathen do not accept Christianity because they do not see union and love among Christians. Nothing attracts people so much as virtue, and nothing repels them so much as vice. That is why people turn away from Christianity, because when a person sees that one who is commanded to love even his enemies practices extortion, robs, makes war, stirs people up to enmity, and treats people as wild beasts, he cannot believe the teaching of love. And when he sees that a Christian is afraid of death, then he will in no way believe in immortality. We Christians are to blame that they do not believe in Christ's teaching. Perhaps we will say: “take an example from the holy people of old.” But people want to see virtuous people of today. Show us, they say, faith from your deeds, and there are no deeds. On the contrary, they see that we, worse than beasts, tear at our neighbor. That is what keeps people from the Christian teaching. We say that we profess it, but we only frighten people away from it.

> *After John Chrysostom.*

5

Christianity, if only it is sincerely accepted, acts like the most terrible dynamite, blowing apart everything old and opening up new, endless horizons.

If you see that the arrangement of society is bad and you want to correct it, then know that for this there is only one means: that all people should become better; and in order for people to become better, only one thing is in your power: to become better yourself.
