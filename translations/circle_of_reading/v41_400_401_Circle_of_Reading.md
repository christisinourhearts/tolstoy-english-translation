<!-- vol. 41, p. 400 -->

15 June

To love God means to love the highest good that we can possibly imagine.

1

People often say: I do not understand love for God. It would be truer to say: I do not understand any love without love for God.

2

> True love for God is a moral feeling founded on a clear understanding of the highest perfection. So love for God coincides completely with love for virtue, justice, kindness.

> *Channing.*

3

> A man who has made the knowledge of the law his own but is a stranger to love for God is like a treasurer who has been entrusted with the inner keys without the outer ones.

> *The Talmud.*

4

> God's commandments must be fulfilled out of love for God, and not out of fear of him.

> *The Talmud.*

5

> As a person feels himself to be in his soul, so, in essence, is his God too: either he is kind, loving, just, or vengeful, wrathful, and malicious.

> *Lucy Mallory.*

6

*If you love a person without loving God in him, that is, good, then with such love you prepare disappointment and sufferings for yourself.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 401 -->

7

He who says that he loves God but does not love his neighbor deceives people. But he who says that he loves his neighbor but does not love God deceives himself.

Only perfection is fully worthy of love.

In order to experience perfect love, we must either ascribe perfection to the imperfect object of our love, or love perfection—God.
