16 October

Consciousness of God in oneself is possible for every person. The awakening of this consciousness is what in the Gospel is called resurrection.

1

> The petals of flowers fall when the fruit begins to grow. So your weaknesses will fall away from you when the consciousness of God begins to grow in you.

> Though darkness had filled a space for thousands of years, it becomes light at once when light penetrates into it. So too your soul: however long it has been swallowed up by darkness, it will all be lit up at once as soon as God opens his eyes in it.

> *Brahmanic wisdom. Ramakrishna.*

2

> Self-respect comes from our contemplation of the deity in our own soul. The foundations of self-respect are in religion. The best example of this is greatness in humility. No aristocrat, no prince born on a throne, can compare in self-respect with a saint. The saint is humbled because, relying on the greatness of God, whom he feels in himself, he wants to be humbled.

> *Emerson.*

3

> *Skillful is he who knows people; but truly enlightened is he who knows himself.*

> *He who knows himself—he comes to know God.*

> *Eastern wisdom.*

<!-- vol. 42, p. 154 -->

4

God is near you, he is with you, he is in you. The divine spirit dwells in us, always a witness of our good and bad deeds, and as we deal with it, so it deals with us. A person cannot be good without God.

*Seneca.*

5

If it is hard for you, go deep into yourself, and at a certain depth you will find God; and as soon as you come to know him in yourself, everything hard will become easy, and you will feel love and joy.

If a person does not feel the power of God in himself, this does not prove that the power of God does not live in him, but only that the person has not yet learned to be conscious of it in himself.
