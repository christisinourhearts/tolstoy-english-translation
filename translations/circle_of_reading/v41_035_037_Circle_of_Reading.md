<!-- vol. 41, p. 35 -->

14 January

One can love in oneself only the one who is one in all. And to love the one who is one in all means to love God.

1

> Teacher! what is the greatest commandment in the law? Jesus said: love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your understanding: this is the first and greatest commandment; and the second, like it: love your neighbor as yourself; on these two commandments hang the whole law and the prophets.

> *Matt. ch. 22, vv. 36–40.*

2

All people are alive not because they provide for themselves, but because there is love in people.

It is as though God did not want people to live separately and therefore did not reveal to them what each needs for himself, but wanted them to live together, and therefore revealed to them what they all need for themselves and for everyone.

<!-- vol. 41, p. 36 -->

People think they are alive through caring for themselves, but they are alive through love alone. If there were no love in people, not one child would grow up, not one person would remain alive.

3

*People are alive through love; love of oneself* — *is the beginning of death*, *love of God and people* — *is the beginning of life*.

4

> God is love, and he who abides in love abides in God, and God in him. No one has ever seen God. If we love one another, then God abides in us, and his love is perfect in us. He who says: I love God, but hates his brother, is a liar; for he who does not love his brother whom he sees, how can he love God whom he does not see? Brothers, let us love one another, for he who loves is born of God and knows God, because God is love. He who abides in love abides in God, and God in him.

> *First Epistle of John, from ch. 4.*

5

If a person cannot forgive his brother, he does not love him. True love is infinite, and there is no number of offenses that it would not forgive, if it is true love.

6

To love someone who is pleasant to us does not mean to love. True love is only when in a person you love the same God who is in you. With this love you love not only your relatives, not only those who love you, but unpleasant, evil people who hate you. To love such people, one must remember that the person with whom you are dealing loves himself just as you love yourself, and that in him is the same God who is in you. If you remember this, then you will understand how you ought to relate to him. And if you understand, then you will love him, and if you love in this way, then such love will give you more joy than love for those who love you.

<!-- vol. 41, p. 37 -->

Love is not the fundamental principle of our life. Love is a consequence, not a cause. The cause of love is the consciousness in oneself of the divine, spiritual principle. This consciousness demands love, produces love.
