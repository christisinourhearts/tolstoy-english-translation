<!-- vol. 42, p. 316 -->

8 December

How easy, it would seem, is the fulfillment of the law of God expressed in the Christian teaching, and how far we still are from this fulfillment!

1

> You have heard that it was said to the ancients: do not kill; and whoever kills is liable to judgment (Exodus 20:13).

> But I say to you that everyone who is angry with his brother is liable to judgment.

> You have heard that it was said to the ancients: do not commit adultery (Exodus 20:14).

> But I say to you that everyone who looks at a woman with lust has already committed adultery with her in his heart.

> Again you have heard that it was said to the ancients: do not break your oath, but fulfill your oaths before the Lord (Leviticus 19:12; Deuteronomy 23:21).

> But I say to you: do not swear at all... But let your word be: “yes, yes,” “no, no”; and whatever is beyond this is from the evil one.

> You have heard that it was said: an eye for an eye, and a tooth for a tooth (Exodus 21:24).

> But I say to you: do not resist evil; but whoever strikes you on your right cheek, turn the other to him as well.

> And whoever wants to go to law with you and take your shirt, give him your outer garment too.

> And whoever compels you to go one mile with him, go two with him.

> Give to him who asks of you, and do not turn away from him who wants to borrow from you.

> You have heard that it was said: love your neighbor and hate your enemy (Leviticus 19:17–18).

> That you may be sons of your heavenly father: for he commands his sun to rise over the evil and the good, and sends rain on the righteous and the unrighteous.

> For if you love those who love you, what reward have you? Do not the tax collectors do the same?

> And if you greet only your brothers, what special thing are you doing? Do not the pagans act the same way?

> So be perfect, as your heavenly father is perfect.

> *Matthew 5:21, 22, 27, 28, 33, 34, 37–48.*

<!-- vol. 42, p. 317 --> But I say to you: love your enemies, bless those who curse you, do good to those who hate you, and pray for those who wrong you and persecute you.

2

In the five commandments of Christ the conditions for fulfilling the law are expressed; everything that hinders this fulfillment is pointed out. People need only follow these 5 commandments, and the kingdom of God will be established on earth. And following them is easy even for us, spoiled by our upbringing. What would it be like if all children were brought up in these commandments?

3

> The constant forward movement of religion lies in its drawing nearer to morality. If opinions change in theology, people's convictions about conduct are unchanging.

> *Emerson.*

4

> *The law of life is unclear to the wise, but it becomes clearer and clearer as they follow it. The law of life is clear to ordinary people, but it becomes more and more obscured as they follow it.*

> *Confucius.*

5

> There exists only one eternal, unchanging law, governing all peoples at all times. He who does not obey <!-- vol. 42, p. 318 --> this law renounces himself, despises human nature, and by this very thing such a man, though he may escape human punishment, lays upon himself a punishment that comes not from people, but is the heaviest of all.

> *Cicero.*

For the realization of the law of God, so far as it has been made clear to us, our effort is needed, and this effort is being made by people, and, however slowly, we are nevertheless drawing nearer to this realization.
