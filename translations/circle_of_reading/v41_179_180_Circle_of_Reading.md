<!-- vol. 41, p. 179 -->

20 March

He who lives to fulfill the will of God cannot be sensitive to the judgments of people.

1

> We must think as though anyone could see what is going on in our soul.

> *Seneca.*

2

> Live openly.

> *Auguste Comte.*

3

It is not good to hide bad deeds, but it is still worse to do them openly, to take pride in them.

4

Shame before people is a good feeling, but best of all is shame before oneself.

5

Nothing shows more surely the stage of moral perfection at which a person stands than what he is ashamed of and what he is not ashamed of.

6

*Do not hide anything when you are asked, but do not tell about your bad deeds without need*.

7

How good it would be if the fear of God in people were as strong as the fear of people. Before people a person hopes to hide his bad deeds; but before God one cannot hide: one must not do them.

<!-- vol. 41, p. 180 -->

8

> You can hide it from people, but from God you cannot hide it.

> *Proverb*

9

Almost always, what people try to hide is bad.

10

It is good to hide only your good deeds.

11

There is nothing secret that would not become evident, nor hidden that would not become known and come to light.

*Luke 8:17.*

Live so that you would have no need to hide, and also no wish to show before people, the deeds of your life.
