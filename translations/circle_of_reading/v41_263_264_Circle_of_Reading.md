<!-- vol. 41, p. 263 -->

17 April

Christianity is the teaching of the divine in man.

1

> Christianity is a simple thing, very simple: love for man, love for God. Be perfect, as your heavenly father; live in God, that is, do the best deeds, in the best way, and for the sake of the best ends.

> All this is very simple: a small child can understand it; and so beautiful that a great mind will not devise anything more beautiful.

> *Parker.*

2

> From Moses to Jesus a great intellectual and religious development took place among individual people and nations. From the time of Jesus to ours this movement, both in individual people and in nations, has been still more considerable. Old errors have been cast off, and new truths have entered the consciousness of humanity. One man cannot be as great as humanity. If a great man is so far ahead of his fellows that they do not understand him—a time comes when they first catch up with him, then overtake him and go so far that, in their turn, they become incomprehensible to those who stand at the place where the former great man stood, and then a new great man is needed, and he appears and opens the further path.

> *Parker.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 264 -->

3

*Without a clear understanding of the meaning of his life, without what is called faith, a person may at any minute renounce all that in the name of which he lived, and begin to live in the name of what he cursed.*

4

The goal of his life cannot be accessible to a person. A person can know only its direction.

5

The essence of all religious teachings is in love. The peculiarity of the Christian teaching about love is in this, that it clearly and precisely defined the chief condition of love—a condition the violation of which destroys the possibility of love.

This condition is non-resistance to evil by violence.

6

Christian love flows from the consciousness of the unity of the divine source in oneself and in all people, and not only in people, but in everything living.

If you want to be calm and strong—establish faith in yourselves.
