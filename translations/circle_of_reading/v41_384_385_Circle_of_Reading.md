<!-- vol. 41, p. 384 -->

7 June

Humility gives joys inaccessible to the self-satisfied and proud.

1

Peace among people is a necessary condition of a good life; but the chief obstacle to peace is our pride; only humility, the readiness to endure humiliation, to be slandered, to be misunderstood, only with such readiness can a person bring peace into his relation to other people and into the relations of people with one another.

2

> Come to me, all who toil and are burdened, and I will set you at rest. Take my yoke upon yourselves and learn from me; for I am meek and humble of heart; and you will find rest for your souls. For my yoke is good, and my burden is light.

> *Matthew 11:28–30.*

3

> When the world condemns us, slanders us, we must not be angry, but rather examine whether there is not some ground in these condemnations.

> *Hume.*

4

> If your self-love suffers at the recollection that in former times you neglected wisdom, did not live as the wise live, and will hardly earn the fame of a wise man, do not grieve over it. If you do not gain the reputation of a wise man, so much the better. Be content if you can now, at once, begin to live as your conscience requires.

> *Marcus Aurelius.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 385 -->

5

> The first thing a person must learn before he can make progress on the path to happiness is—humility. Arrogance, imperiousness, and vanity must give way to meekness and gentleness. *Arrogance can acquire nothing, since it assumes that it already has everything.*

> *From “The World's Advance Thought.”*

6

> Pride defends not only itself but all of a person's other sins as well, because it hates reproof and pushes away healing; it hides and justifies sin. The consciousness of sin, which humbles a person, is more useful than a good deed that puffs up his pride.

> *Baxter.*

7

> *Be strict with yourselves and indulgent toward others, and you will have no enemies.*

> *Chinese wisdom.*

Do not fear humiliation if you are able to accept it with humility: it will be repaid many times over by the spiritual goods that are bound up with it.
