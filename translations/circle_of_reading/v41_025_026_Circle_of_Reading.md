<!-- vol. 41, p. 25 -->

8 January

Christian teaching is so clear that infants understand it in its true meaning. Only people who wish to seem and be called Christians, but not actually to be Christians, can fail to understand it.

1

Buddha said: a person who begins to live for the soul is like a person who brings light into a dark house. The darkness is immediately dispersed. Only persist in such a life, and complete enlightenment will take place in you.

2

> The people (I speak of the good people, of those whom the corruption proceeding from the ruling classes has not touched), freed from what Christ calls the blindness of wealth, content with daily bread, asking of the heavenly Father only what he gives the little birds that neither sow nor reap—the people live the true life, the life of the heart, more than other people immersed in the desires and cares of this world. That is why heroic deeds and self-sacrifice must be sought among them, among the people. Cast aside the people—what will become of the precepts of duty, of that by which society alone is sustained, of that which constitutes the greatness and strength of nations? When nations grow weak, who renews them, revives <!-- vol. 41, p. 26 --> them, if not the common people? And if the disease is incurable, if peoples must die, from what does the young shoot come that is destined to replace the old tree, if not again from the people? And therefore Christ addresses the people, and therefore the people recognize in him the messenger of the Father, glorify his name, proclaim his authority, submitting to it. But the princes of the church, the scribes, curse him and kill him. Yet despite their violence and cunning, despite the execution, Christ triumphed among the people; the people founded his kingdom in the world, and by the people it will spread in the world; by the people the new life will be born, the divine germ of which the violent authorities, already seized with terror at their approaching end, would so like to choke.

> *Lamennais.*

3

> One must beware of two equally destructive superstitions: the superstition of theologians who teach that the essence of divinity can be expressed in words, and the superstition of science, which supposes that divine power can be explained by scientific investigations.

> *John Ruskin.*

4

The last commandment of Christ expresses all his teaching: “Love one another as I have loved you, and therefore everyone will know that you are my disciples if you have love for one another.” He does not say: “if you believe in this or that,” but “if you love.” Faith changes together with the ceaseless change of views and knowledge; it is bound to time and changes together with time. Love is not temporal; it is unchanging, eternal.

5

> My religion is love for all that lives.

> *Ibrahim of Cordoba.*

*For Christianity to be realized, only the destruction of its distortion is lacking.*
