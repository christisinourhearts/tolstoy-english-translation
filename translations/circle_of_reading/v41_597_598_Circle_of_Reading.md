27 August

The most necessary and important subject of study for every person is himself, his spiritual being.

1

A man who knows all the sciences but does not know himself is a pitiful ignoramus. But a man who knows nothing except himself, his spiritual I, is a fully enlightened man.

<!-- vol. 41, p. 598 -->

2

> Most people want to know God and do not want to know themselves. But let them recognize good within themselves and make it grow, and they will then know God, for there is no other way to the knowledge of him.

> *Lucy Mallory.*

3

> When a man asks nature what he is, he receives no answer, for only he himself is the answer to this question; he must know himself.

> *Lucy Mallory.*

4

> *When you feel the need of strength, withdraw into solitude.*

> *Thoreau.*

5

> The road to glory runs through palaces, to happiness through markets, and to virtue through deserts.

> *Chinese proverb.*

6

> For the vast majority their inner world is, as it were, an ocean, so vast that they do not dare to explore it; but sooner or later they will have to enter it and seek in it that heavenly haven which they have vainly sought in the outer world.

> *Lucy Mallory.*

7

A man always has a place of salvation from all calamities; this place is his soul.

*If only a man understood who he is, how insignificant his sorrows would seem to him.*
