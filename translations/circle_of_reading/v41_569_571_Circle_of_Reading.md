<!-- vol. 41, p. 569 -->

13 August

The wisdom of life lies in living according to reason, even if such a life is condemned by everyone.

1

> When truth presents itself to its enemies in an irrefutable form, they then bring into play the last means at their disposal: the blackening of the people who express the truth. But in pelting the people who express the truth with mud, they only cover the seed of truth with earth, and it grows up all the faster.

> *Lucy Mallory.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 570 -->

2

> Heaven is indignant with us for our sins, and the world for our virtues.

> *The Talmud.*

3

> Be concerned not with the number but with the quality of your admirers: *to displease bad people is praiseworthy for a person.*

> *Seneca.*

4

> *Human reason is a divine lamp, whose light penetrates into the very depth of things.*

> *Eastern wisdom.*

5

> If they persecuted me, they will persecute you too; if they kept my word, they will keep yours too.

> *John 15:20.*

6

> If we are sitting on a moving ship and look at some object on that same ship, our own movement is not noticeable to us; but if we look aside at an object that is not moving with us, for example at the shore, we at once notice our movement. The same happens in life. When all people live not as they should, this is not noticeable; but as soon as one comes to his senses and begins to live in God's way, it at once becomes obvious how badly the others act. And the others persecute him for this.

> *Pascal.*

7

> Woe to you if everyone speaks well of you, — said Christ.

> The meaning of these words is that we ought not to set ourselves an external aim: to please people, accommodating and adapting ourselves to the multitude of their imperfect and conflicting tastes, wishes, and whims; but that we ought to set ourselves an inner aim: <!-- vol. 41, p. 571 --> to please God, listening for and adapting ourselves to his one and perfect will.

> And just as masons could put up a building not when they hewed the stones, fitting their shapes to the irregularities and peculiarities of the shapes of other stones, but only when they fitted their shapes to the rectangle, so people will succeed in establishing the kingdom of God on earth not when they educate themselves and their children in accordance with the conflicting, changeable demands of people's talk, but only when they perfect themselves according to the laws of good and truth common to all, which are recognized with the help of conscience and reason.

> *Fyodor Strakhov.*

It is a mistake to be grieved by the persecutions, attacks, and harassment directed against wisdom. Wisdom would not be wisdom if it did not denounce the madness of a bad life. And people would not be people if, without changing their life, they could calmly bear the denunciations.
