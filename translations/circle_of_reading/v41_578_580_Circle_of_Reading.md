18 August

Christianity is true precisely because, in answering the most abstract questions, by that very answer it also resolves the most practical questions of life. It establishes the kingdom of God in the spiritual world of each person and in the social life of people.

1

> Ask these millions of people who call themselves Christians what Christianity is, and they will answer you that to be a Christian means to adhere to a certain teaching. And yet those who profess this teaching all disagree among <!-- vol. 41, p. 579 --> themselves. Some say one must believe thus, others otherwise, and from these disagreements arise accusations and hatred, even persecutions going as far as bloodshed. If true Christianity lay in this, how could Christ be the liberator, the savior of the peoples, the one for whom the human race was waiting? But he himself gives us quite another idea of his work. He was sent to bring the poor *good news,* to heal hearts broken by suffering, torments, and oppression; to announce to the blind that they will see, that they will no longer be deprived of the light that the rulers deny them so that, brutalized in ignorance, they may bear their yoke more patiently; to free those whom fetters crush; to replace universal slavery with freedom. Such was the work of Jesus, but is that what is taught in his name? Has that been fulfilled which the one desired by the people was to fulfill? Have the poor heard the *good news?* Are the broken hearts healed? Do the blind see? Have the fetters been taken off the imprisoned? Are the captives freed? No, Christ is still on the cross, waiting for his apostles. Let them come, let them come quickly, for the sorrow is great, and eyes grow tired of looking to the east, waiting for the dawn of the year of the Lord.

> *Lamennais.*

2

> *Religion is not the truth because holy men preached it, but holy men preached it because it is the truth.*

> *Lessing.*

3

All the calamities of people come from the absence of religion. Without religion one cannot live. Only religion gives a definition of good and bad, and therefore only on the basis of religion can a person make a choice among everything he may wish to do. Only religion destroys egoism, only in consequence of religious requirements can a person live not for himself. Only religion destroys the fear of death; only religion gives a person the meaning of life; only religion establishes the equality of people; only religion frees a person from all external constraints.

One cannot help believing a spiritual teaching whose application brings about the simplest practical good of all.

There is no greater test of the truth of a teaching.

And such is the Christian teaching.
