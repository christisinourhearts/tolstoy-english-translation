<!-- vol. 41, p. 120 -->

22 February

Everything that has been said about God, and everything that can be said about him, all this does not satisfy. That in God which a person can understand and cannot express—this is what every person needs, and this alone gives him life.

*After Angelus Silesius.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 121 -->

1

> Reason that can be comprehended is not the eternal reason. A being that can be named is not the eternal being.

> *Laozi.*

2

> There is a being that contains everything in itself and without which there would be neither heaven nor earth; this being is calm, bodiless; its properties are called reason, love, but the being itself has no name. It is the most distant and it is the nearest.

> *After Laozi.*

3

> God is that infinite which demands righteousness of us.

> *Matthew Arnold.*

4

*God is that all of which we are conscious of being a part*.

5

> Mad are those who ask where God is. God is in all nature and in the soul of every person. Faiths are different, but God is one. If a person does not know himself, how will he know God?

> *Indian wisdom.*

6

> I had never been, and it did not depend on me ever to be, just as it does not depend on me, now existing, to cease to be—consequently, I began and continue to be by the force of something that was before me, that will be after me, and that is mightier than I. And I am told that there is nothing of the kind that we call God.

> *La Bruyère.*

7

> Just as a person shut up from birth in a room with frosted glass in the window would begin to call the sun frosted glass, that is, by the name of the only object that lets <!-- vol. 41, p. 122 --> the light of the sun through itself into the room—so the Gospel defines the concept of God by the name of that highest feeling or that highest human faculty which serves as the only conductor of divine revelation from above. Namely, it calls God love, understanding (the word).

> And just as only release from confinement will give the prisoner the possibility of distinguishing the sun itself from the frosted glass lit by it, so exactly only one or another degree of release from the bonds of corporeality, of materiality, will give the human soul the possibility of a more immediate union with the essence of the divinity.

> And until then, people who honor their reason above all will identify God with reason and call him reason; people who honor the feeling of love above all will identify God with love and call him love.

> And, finally, people who do not yet believe either in their reason or in their love, and precisely because of this blindly and unquestioningly believe in the authority of another's personality, will identify God with a personality.

> *Fyodor Strakhov.*

If your eyes go blind from the sun, you do not say that there is no sun. Nor will you say that there is no God because your reason gets confused and lost trying to understand him.

*After Angelus Silesius.*
