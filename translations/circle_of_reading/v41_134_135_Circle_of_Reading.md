<!-- vol. 41, p. 134 -->

26 February

After a long conversation, try to remember everything that was talked about, and you will be surprised how empty and unnecessary and often bad much, and sometimes even all, of what was said was.

1

> For a foolish man it is best of all to keep silent. But if he knew this, he would not be a foolish man.

> *Saadi.*

2

> When you speak, your words should be better than silence.

> *Arab saying.*

3

If you once regret that you did not speak, you will regret a hundred times that you did not keep silent.

4

> The good are not disputers; disputers are not good.

> The wise are not learned. The learned are not wise.

> True words are not pleasant. Pleasant words are not true.

> *Laozi.*

5

*Manual labor is useful if only because it delivers one from empty talk.*

6

> If you want to be intelligent, learn to ask reasonably, to listen attentively, to answer calmly, and to stop talking when there is nothing more to say.

> *Lavater.*

7

> If people argue for a long time, this proves that what they are arguing about is unclear to themselves.

> *Voltaire*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 135 -->

8

> How many absurdities are said by people only out of the desire to say something new.

> *Voltaire.*

9

> The tongue of a mute is better than the tongue of a liar.

> *Turkish saying.*

If you have time to think before you begin to speak, then think whether it is worth it, whether it is necessary to speak, whether what you want to say may not harm someone.
