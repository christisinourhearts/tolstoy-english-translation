<!-- vol. 41, p. 180 -->

21 March

We know life only here, in this world, and therefore, if there is meaning in our life, it must be here, in this world.

1

There is no peace either for the one who lives for worldly aims among people or for the one who lives for spiritual aims alone. Peace is received only by the one who lives for the service of God among people.

2

> Do not wish for death because it is hard for you to live. The whole weight of the world on the shoulders of every moral being makes him fulfill his calling. The only means of deliverance from this weight is the fulfillment of one's calling. You will be let go only when you have done the work appointed to you.

> *Emerson.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 181 -->

3

True life is only in the present. What was is no longer, nor is there what will be—there is only what is now. And it is only for this, to live well now, that one must strive with all the strength of the soul. If people teach that in this life one must live for the future life, do not believe them. Only this life do we know and live through. And therefore all one's strength must be directed toward living this life—and not only this whole life, but every hour of this life—as well as possible.

4

> Life is neither suffering nor pleasure, but a task that we are obliged to do and honestly carry through to the end.

> *Tocqueville.*

5

> You suffer, and it seems to you that you cannot live as well as you would like, that you would more easily do what you consider you ought to do if your life were different. Know, then, this truth: that in this life, in the conditions in which you are now, you can always do what you ought to do.

> *After Carlyle.*

*Here, in this world, is the place of our service, and therefore it is to the fulfillment of service here that all our powers must be directed*.
