<!-- vol. 41, p. 416 -->

18 June

The consciousness of duty gives us the consciousness of the divinity of our soul, and, conversely, the consciousness of the divinity of our soul gives us the consciousness of duty.

1

> *There is something in our soul which, if we pay proper attention to it, we will always observe with the greatest wonder (and where wonder is legitimate, it at the same time acts upon our soul in an elevating way)—this something—the original moral inclinations laid down in us.*

> *Kant.*

2

> The dignity of man lies in that spiritual principle which is sometimes called reason, sometimes conscience. This principle, rising above the local and the temporal, contains indubitable truth and eternal right. In the midst of the imperfect it sees perfection. It is universal, impartial, and always in contradiction with everything that is partial and selfish in human nature. This principle tells each of us with authority that our neighbor is as precious as we are and that his rights are as sacred as ours. It bids us accept the truth, however repugnant it may be to our pride, and be just, however disadvantageous this may be to us. It too, this principle, calls us to what is beautiful, holy, and happy, in whomever we may meet these qualities. *This principle is a ray of divinity in man*.

> *Channing.*

3

> People attain heavenly joy, attain bliss in bodily life. Such people are pure, because they are absorbed only in the desire for a good life. And when the mind and heart are pure, divinity reveals itself to them.

> *Brahmanic wisdom (Vishnu Purana).*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 417 -->

4

> A new, secret, joyful, and supernatural beauty presents itself to a person when his heart opens to virtue. He then comes to know what is above him. He knows then that his being is boundless, comes to know that, however low he is now, he is born for good, for perfection. What he reveres already belongs to him, although he does not yet feel it. *He must*—he now knows the meaning of this great word.

> *Emerson.*

The voice of conscience is the voice of God.
