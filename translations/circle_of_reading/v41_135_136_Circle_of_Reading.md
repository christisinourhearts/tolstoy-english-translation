<!-- vol. 41, p. 135 -->

27 February

*Charity is charity only when it is a sacrifice.*

1

> Your gold and silver have rusted, and their rust will be a witness against you and will eat your flesh like fire: you have gathered treasure for yourselves for the last days.

> *James 5:3.*

2

In money, in money itself, in the possession of it, there is something immoral.

3

> If you want the mercy of God, show deeds. But perhaps even now someone, like the rich young man, will say: “I have fulfilled everything: I have not stolen, I have not killed, I have not committed adultery.” But Christ said that not this only is needed, but something else as well. What, then? “Sell,” he says, “your property and give to the beggars, and come after me” (Matthew 19:21). And to go after him means to imitate him in deeds. In what deeds? In love for one's neighbor. But if the young man, living in such abundance, could keep from giving his property away to the poor, then how could he say that he loves his neighbor? <!-- vol. 41, p. 136 --> If love is strong and not in words alone, it will show itself in deed. And for a rich man to show love in deed means to renounce riches.

> *After John Chrysostom.*

4

> A compassionate man is not rich; a rich man—surely not compassionate.

> *Manchu saying.*

5

Rich benefactors do not see that what they bestow on the poor man they have wrenched from the hands of people who are often poorer still.

6

> Even if the rich do give alms to the poor, they still do great harm to the people by being rich and living luxuriously. They do not think that their adoration of wealth, their luxury of life, their contempt for poverty and a gray life corrupt the poor and instill in them the thought that there is only one good in the world—wealth—and that one must above all strive for it.

> *After Channing.*

7

> It is hard for a rich man to enter the kingdom of heaven! It is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for a rich man to enter the kingdom of God.

> *Matthew 19:23–24.*

One cannot do good with wealth. In order for a rich man to be able to do good, he must first of all free himself from wealth.
