<!-- vol. 41, p. 314 -->

9 May

Life is unceasing change: the weakening of the fleshly life and the strengthening, the increase, of the spiritual life.

1

> Struggle with oneself and violence against oneself cannot but exist as a consequence of our former sins; but this violence against oneself is loving, lawful. A mother tears her child out of the jaws of a beast. The child is hurt, but he must of course attribute his suffering not to his mother, who is saving him, but to the beast, which wants to keep hold of him. In exactly the same way a person must regard the struggle of good with evil: good, like a mother, tears our soul away from evil, and although this struggle is tormenting for us, it is necessary and gives us the good. It would be bad for us if there were not this struggle. Without it we could not be good.

> *Pascal.*

2

> As the light increases in us, we see ourselves as worse than we thought before. We are surprised at our former blindness as we see a whole swarm of shameful feelings coming out of our heart. We never imagined that we could harbor such vile things in ourselves, and we watch their appearance with horror. But there is no need either to be surprised or to despair. We have not become worse than we were, on the contrary.

> *Fénelon.*

3

> As long as you live—learn. Do not wait for old age to bring wisdom with it.

> *Solon.*

4

> Above all, we need to free ourselves from the absurd notion that heaven will in time correct our big mistakes. If you carelessly prepare some dish, you do not count on providence making it tasty; and in exactly the same way, if over a whole series of mad years you have falsely <!-- vol. 41, p. 315 --> directed your life, you must not expect that divine intervention will direct and arrange you for the better.

> *John Ruskin.*

5

> *Virtue always moves forward and yet always begins anew.*

> *Kant.*

6

The kindness of a dove is not virtue. A dove is not more virtuous than a wolf. Virtue and its degrees begin only when effort begins.

7

> If it had pleased God, he would have made us all one people, but he tests us.

> Wherever you may be, all of you strive with all your might toward good: the day will come when God will unite you all.

> *The Quran.*

One must not stop on the path of self-perfection. *As soon as you feel a greater interest in the outer world than in your own soul, know that you have stopped; the world is moving past you, and you are standing still.*
