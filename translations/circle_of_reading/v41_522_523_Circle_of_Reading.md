<!-- vol. 41, p. 522 -->

22 July

*Faith is not faith if life does not agree with it.*

1

> So, everyone who hears these words of mine and does them I will liken to a prudent man who built his house on rock; and the rain came down, and the rivers overflowed, and the winds blew and rushed upon that house, and it did not fall, because it was founded on rock.

> And everyone who hears these words of mine and does not do them will be likened to a foolish man who built his house on sand; and the rain came down, and the rivers overflowed, and the winds blew and pressed upon that house, and it fell, and its fall was great.

> *Matthew 7:24–27.*

2

> Death is inevitable for all that is born, just as birth is inevitable for all that is mortal. Therefore one must not lament over what is inevitable. The former state of beings is unknown, the middle state is evident, the future state cannot be known; what, then, is there to care and worry about? Some people look on the soul as a wonder, and others speak and hear of it with astonishment, but no one knows anything about it.

> *Indian Bhagavata.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 523 --> The door of heaven is open to you exactly as far as you need. Free yourself from care and anxieties and direct your soul toward the spiritual. Let your actions be guided by you and not by events. Do not be one of those whose aim in acting is reward. Be attentive, do your duty, leave aside the thought of consequences, so that it is all the same to you whether the matter ends pleasantly or unpleasantly for you.

3

> *What good is it, my brothers, if someone says that he has faith but has no works?* Can this faith save him? If a brother or sister is naked and has no daily food, and one of you says to them: go in peace, be warm and be fed, but does not give them what the body needs: what good is it? So also faith, if it has no works, is dead in itself. But someone will say: you have faith, and I have works—show me your faith without your works, and I will show you my faith by my works.

> Do you see that a man is justified by works, and not by faith only? For as the body without the spirit is dead, so faith without works is dead.

> *James 2:14–18, 24, 26.*

4

> A man who knows the law but does not fulfill it is like a man who plows but does not sow.

> *Eastern wisdom.*

If a person is in no hurry to fulfill what he recognizes as the law of God, then he believes neither in God nor in his law.
