26 July

In every faith only what is spiritual is true.

1

> Jesus does not say to the Samaritans: leave your beliefs, your traditions, for the Jewish ones; he does not say to the Jews: join the Samaritans; but he says to the Samaritans and the Jews: you are equally in error. God is spirit, and faith in him is only inward, bound neither to a place nor to any outward form. What matters is not the temple and not the service in the temple. What matters is not Gerizim or Jerusalem. The time will come and has already come when they will worship the father not on Gerizim, not in Jerusalem, but when the true worshipers will worship the father in spirit and truth, for the father seeks such worshipers for himself.

> He sought them in the times of Jerusalem. He seeks them still now. When, then, will he find them? When everyone, weary of drawing from <!-- vol. 41, p. 530 --> springs that do not satisfy, says to Jesus: “Lord, give me this water, so that I may not thirst and not come here to draw!”

> *Lamennais.*

2

> Christ came in order to reveal that the *eternal* is not the *future*, but only the *invisible*—that eternity is not that ocean into which people are carried by the river of time, but that it is around them now, and that their lives are real only to the extent that they feel its presence. He came to teach them that God is not some chance abstraction, infinitely separated from them in a distant heaven, but that he is the father in whom they live, move, and exist, and that the service he loves consists not in the solemn performance of the rites of the church, but in mercy, justice, humility, and love.

> *Farrar.*

3

> *God is spirit, and one must worship him in spirit and truth.*

> *John 4:24.*

4

> Religion that consists in postures and movements of the body is lower even than the exercises of a wrestler.

> In the absence of inner knowledge, do not boast of worshiping the deity in words.

> False is the faith that says it is profitable to renounce the present life: is it not obvious that eternal life begins in this life?

> A person who has attained perfection makes no distinction between the soul and universal nature, between himself and another person.

> Only that one among the sons of men deserves the name of saint who is conscious of God in his heart. Know yourself—and you will become a deity.

> Not knowing that the source of life exists now in your spirit, why do you seek it, imagining that it can be found in another place? Whoever acts so is like a person who lights a lamp in the light of the sun.

> *The Indian Vamana Purana.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 531 -->

5

> It cannot be said that for the salvation of the soul it is absolutely necessary to acknowledge Christ in the flesh; but for the salvation of the soul it is absolutely necessary to acknowledge the son of God, that is, that eternal wisdom of God which manifests itself in all things, and chiefly in the soul of man, and which manifested itself most of all in Jesus Christ. Without this wisdom no one can attain the state of blessedness, because it alone shows what is true and what is false, what is good and what is evil.

> *Spinoza.*

Do not be afraid to cast off from your faith everything bodily, everything visible, tangible. The more you cleanse the spiritual kernel of your faith, the firmer it will be for you.
