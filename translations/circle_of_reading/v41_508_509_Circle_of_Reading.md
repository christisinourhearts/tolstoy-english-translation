<!-- vol. 41, p. 508 -->

18 July

Believing in eternal life, people believe only that the source of life is spiritual and therefore timeless.

1

> A man who has transgressed the law thinks that with death his life will be completely over; such a man is capable of inclining toward everything evil.

> *Buddhist wisdom. Dhammapada.*

2

> In our soul lies the germ of the truth of immortality. It lies in reason, which sees the incompleteness of our existence in this world, an incompleteness that requires continuation for the attainment of its aim. This germ is in our thirst for happiness, so strong that it can never be satisfied in this world. This germ is in the love of good, which, as we nurture it, awakens in us a striving for complete perfection and union with the Perfect.

> *Channing.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 509 -->

3

> People know nothing of that world and therefore wish to stay here. Their greatest desire is to live in this world forever. But a bird that has been in a flowering garden does not want to be shut up in a cage. Even if it does get into a cage, it will wish to break out of it in order to return again to the garden. So too man: when he is freed from the body, he will not want to return to it. Is there such a child as, once born, would wish to return again into his mother's womb? Is there a man who, being freed from prison, would want to go back into it again? Is there a bird that, having slipped out of its cage, will want to be shut up in it again? So too man will not fear the deliverance from the body that awaits him, if he is not attached to bodily life.

> *Tablets of the Babis.*

4

A man will come to know that he will not die only when he comes to know that he was never born, but always was, is, and will be.

A man will believe in his immortality only when he understands that his life is not a wave, but is that eternal motion which in this life shows itself only as a wave.

5

There is no need to think about death, but one must live with it in view. The whole of life, with death in view, becomes solemn, significant, truly fruitful, and joyful. It becomes so both because it may stop at any minute and because, with death in view, one cannot help doing the one thing that is needed for the undying life, that is, for God. And when you live like this, life becomes joyful, and there is none of that bugbear of death which poisons the life of people who live by the animal life alone. The fear of death is inversely proportional to a good life. In a holy life this fear is zero.

*For one who believes that life did not begin with birth and will not end with death, it is easier to live a good life than for one who does not understand this and does not believe it.*
