23 August

If people were wholly virtuous, they would never depart from the truth.

1

> Light has come into the world; but people loved darkness more than light, because their deeds were evil. For everyone who does evil hates the light and does not come to the light, so that <!-- vol. 41, p. 589 --> his deeds may not be exposed, because they are evil; but he who acts by the truth comes to the light, so that his deeds may be manifest, because they have been done in God.

> *John 3:19–21.*

2

> Do not fear any man, high or low, rich or poor, learned or unlearned. Respect all people, love all people, but fear no one. Follow the truth which your reason reveals to you, and hold to your convictions at any cost. Do not wait for an echo in the crowd. The fewer the voices on the side of truth, the more strongly raise your voice. Believe that truth is mightier than error, prejudice, or passion, and be ready for martyrdom. Know that truth is not a local and temporary phenomenon, but something unchanging, eternal, one and the same in all worlds, one with God and having his omnipotence.

> *Channing.*

3

> *Seek truth in thought, not in rotting books. If you want to see the moon, look at the sky, not into a puddle.*

> *Persian saying.*

4

> The fruit of every good deed done by you since the day of your birth will perish as soon as you depart from the truth. That higher spirit which lives in you, and which you know to be one with you, is the all-knowing observer of the good and evil that you do.

> *The Laws of Manu.*

5

> Truth is not learned by way of talk, but only by labor and observation. And when you have mastered one truth—two others will surely appear before you.

> *John Ruskin.*

Truth is harmful only to him who does evil. Those who do good love the truth.
