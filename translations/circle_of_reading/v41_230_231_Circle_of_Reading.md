<!-- vol. 41, p. 230 -->

7 April

To repay evil with good is more natural, and easier, and more reasonable than to repay evil with evil.

1

> And when they came to the place called the Skull, there they crucified him and the evildoers, one on the right and the other on the left side. But Jesus said: Father! forgive them, for they do not know what they are doing.

> *Luke 23:33–34.*

2

> No one has ever grown tired of procuring for himself every possible good. But surely the greatest good that a person can procure for himself is to act in accordance with his higher nature, and the higher, divine nature of your soul bids you tirelessly do good to others as the highest good for yourself.

> *Marcus Aurelius.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 231 -->

3

> Repay evil with good.

> *The Talmud.*

4

> How is one to take revenge on one's enemy? By trying to do him as much good as possible.

> *Epictetus.*

5

> *Overcome anger with meekness, evil with good, the miser with generosity, the liar with truth.*

> *Dhammapada.*

6

> Treating our neighbors as they deserve, we only make them worse. But treating them as though they were better than they really are, we compel them to become better.

> *Goethe.*

7

Answer evil with good—and you will destroy in the evil person all the pleasure that he gets from evil.

8

> Teach your heart, and do not learn from it.

> *Buddhist saying.*

Whoever has even once experienced the joy of repaying evil with good will never again miss a chance to obtain this joy.
