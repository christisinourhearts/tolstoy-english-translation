<!-- vol. 42, p. 159 -->

20 October

A person's life is reasonable only when it is understood as the fulfillment of a duty, as service.

1

> Only one thing we know beyond doubt: that death awaits us. “Like a swallow that has flown through a room is the life of man.” We come, no one knows from where, and go away, no one knows where. Impenetrable darkness behind, thick shadows ahead. When our time comes, what significance will it have for us that we ate tasty dishes or did not, wore or did not wear soft clothes, left a large fortune or left none, reaped laurels or were despised, were considered learned or ignorant—compared with how we used the talent entrusted to us by the master.

> *Henry George.*

2

> He who recognizes a ray of divine power in the smallest things of the world is a man of high understanding and high aspirations. Such a man respects himself and others and does not disdain insignificant matters, but looks on them as manifestations of divine power.

> *Persian Jalal al-Din Rumi.*

3

> Virtue is a service that a person is obliged to perform for himself. If there were neither heaven nor a God governing the world, virtue would still be an obligatory <!-- vol. 42, p. 160 --> law of life. To know what is just and to do it, in this is both the duty and the advantage of man.

> *Ramayana.*

4

*When you come together with a person, think not about how he can be useful to you, but about how you can serve him.*

5

We have been given an undoubted law for all our actions, and actions according to this law can be neither stopped nor constrained by any power. The fulfillment of this law is possible in prison and under the threat of torture and death.

6

> It is clear that my life in this world of God's has not an independent significance but a serving one. It is clear that in the fleshly sense we are destined to be defeated and destined to die; our eyes see this, our reason speaks of it, all nature bears witness to it; such is the law of life of God's world, so it pleases God. And the person who has reached this understanding, little by little, as this simple truth becomes clear to him, loses more and more the desire to fight and be at enmity with people for the goods of his fleshly life, for the goods of this, as it turns out, alien, temporary, and cruel ruler.

> *Buka.*

Seek for yourselves only a good life, in accord with the will of God—and you will fully fulfill the service appointed for you.
