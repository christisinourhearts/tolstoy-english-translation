2 June

The purpose of woman and of man is one and the same: serving God. But the ways in which the one sex and the other serve are different and precisely defined. And therefore each sex must serve God in its own way, the one defined for it. The chief, exclusive work of woman—granted to her alone <!-- vol. 41, p. 361 --> and necessary for the life and perfecting of humanity—is the bearing and early upbringing of children. And therefore all the powers and attention of woman must be directed to this work and to what touches on it. A woman can do everything that a man does, but a man cannot do what a woman does (childbearing and early upbringing). And therefore a woman must put all her powers into doing well that work (of childbearing and early upbringing) which she alone can do.

1

A woman, the mother of a family, who does not know how to be happy at home will not be happy anywhere.

2

Service to humanity divides of itself into two parts: one—increasing the good in existing humanity, the other—the continuation of humanity itself. Men are called predominantly to the first; women are called predominantly to the second.

3

> Man and woman are those two notes without which the strings of the human soul do not give a true and full chord.

> *Giuseppe Mazzini.*

4

There is a strange, deep-rooted error that cooking, sewing, washing, and nursing children are exclusively women's work and that for a man to do this is even shameful. And yet the reverse is shameful: it is shameful for a man, often unoccupied, to spend his time on trifles or to do nothing while a tired, often weak, pregnant woman, beyond her strength, cooks, washes, or nurses a sick child.

<!-- vol. 41, p. 362 -->

5

> *The whole world and everything in it is beautiful, but the most beautiful thing in the world is a virtuous woman.*

> *Muhammad*

6

The virtues of woman and of man are the same: abstinence, truthfulness, kindness. But in woman the same virtues take on a special charm.

7

Childbearing is for a woman a school of self-denial. Having cultivated in herself the capacity for self-denial, a woman readily shows it in other conditions of life as well.

8

A woman who tries to resemble a man is as ugly as an effeminate man.

9

The true and lasting union of man and woman is only in spiritual communion. Sexual communion without spiritual communion is a source of suffering for both spouses.

10

Woman does a great work: she gives birth to children, but she does not give birth to thoughts; man does that. Woman always only follows what has been brought in by man and what has already spread, and spreads it further. In the same way man only brings up children and does not give birth to them.

While you are unmarried, and from the time you have been freed from childbearing, do everything that a man does; but know that the work in which nothing can replace a woman is childbearing and early upbringing.
