15 May

Truthfulness is not a virtue, but a sign of the absence of vices.

1

> Mockery has never harmed truth, but the growth of truth stops in the one who mocks.

> *Lucy Mallory.*

2

The most usual and widespread cause of lying is the desire to deceive not people, but oneself. And this same lie is also the most harmful.

<!-- vol. 41, p. 327 -->

3

> *Thousands of paths lead to error; to truth—only one.*

> *Rousseau.*

4

There is no misfortune worse than when a person begins to fear the truth, lest it expose him.

5

> Only one self-evident truth should be carried out by people.

> *Confucius.*

6

> No untruth can be asserted without inventing yet another untruth for it.

> *Lessing.*

7

How easy, it seems, to speak the truth, and how much inner work is needed to achieve this.

The degree of a person's truthfulness is an indicator of the degree of his moral perfection.

8

> Truthfulness is the only coin that passes everywhere.

> *Chinese proverb.*

9

> Let us be truthful. In this is the secret of eloquence and virtue, in this is moral influence, in this is the highest rule of art and life.

> *Amiel.*

It is a very common mistake to think that there are matters in which one may depart from the truth. The consequences, both inner and outer, of every untruth, even the very smallest, are many times more harmful than that awkwardness, that unpleasantness, which may come from speaking the truth.
