5 October

Moral effort is necessary because carnal effort is made unceasingly. Only stop working on yourself spiritually, and the flesh will take possession of you.

1

> To make the truth one's own is no easy matter for people full of errors, for harmful influences of various kinds strive with extraordinary force to keep their power over them. And therefore one must seek the truth and hold to it with particular persistence.

> *Lucy Mallory.*

2

> What is unclear must be made clear. What is hard to do must be done with great persistence.

> *Confucius.*

3

> As long as you have not destroyed to the very root your lustful attachment to woman, so long will your spirit be attached to the earthly, as a sucking calf is attached to its mother.

> People driven by lust rush about like a hare caught in a trap; stuck fast in the bonds of bodily desires, they fall into suffering again and again for long ages.

> *Buddhist wisdom. Dhammapada.*

4

> The path of correcting oneself is indeed hard, but it is hard not in itself; it is hard because we have given ourselves over to vices for so long. It is these vices that make the path of correction hard for us. We suffer from this struggle to the extent that our vices have managed to take root in us. We cannot think that God is to blame for the necessity of this struggle, because, were there no vices in us, there would be no struggle either. That means the cause of the struggle is <!-- vol. 42, p. 117 --> in our own imperfection. And yet in this struggle is our salvation, and if God delivered us from this struggle, we unfortunate ones would remain forever with our vices.

> *Pascal.*

5

> With our higher feelings it is the same as with all our faculties in general. If we do not exercise them, we cease to notice that we possess them. People who never exercise their faculties become insensible to their existence.

> *Lucy Mallory.*

6

A good deed is always done with effort, but when the effort has been repeated several times, the same deed becomes a habit.

*Do not neglect any deed that can accustom you to doing good, and all the more one that can wean you from doing evil.*
