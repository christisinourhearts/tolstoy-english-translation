<!-- vol. 41, p. 202 -->

30 March

True kindness is not only a virtue and a joy, but also an instrument of struggle far more powerful than violence.

1

It is true that it is hard to be kind to a vicious, lying person, especially to one who insults us, but it is with him, with precisely this person, that one must be kind, both for his sake and for one's own.

2

> *Then Peter came to him and said: Lord, how many times shall I forgive my brother who sins against me, up to seven times?*

> *Jesus says to him: I do not say to you up to seven times, but up to seven times seventy.*

> *Matthew 18:21–22.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 203 -->

3

> If you know how people ought to live for their good, and you wish people well, then you will tell them this, and tell it in such a way that they will believe you. And in order that they may believe you and understand you, you must try to convey your thoughts calmly and with kindness.

> And yet how often we do just the opposite. We know well how to converse with a person who agrees or almost agrees with us; but when we see that the person we are talking with does not believe in the truth that we acknowledge, or even does not understand it, and, despite our efforts to explain it to him, continues to disagree with us and, as it seems to us, is being stubborn or twisting our words, how easily we lose our calm and become irritated! We either begin to get angry and say unpleasant things to the person we are talking with, or we break off the conversation, thinking that with so slow-witted or stubborn a person it is not even worth reasoning.

> When you want to show the person you are talking with some truth in conversation, the most important thing is not to become irritated and not to say a single unkind or offensive word.

> *After Epictetus.*

4

> If you have noticed a mistake in someone, correct him meekly and show him where he went wrong. But if he does not listen to you, blame yourself alone, or, better still, blame no one, but go on being meek.

> *Marcus Aurelius.*

If you have fallen out with a person, if he is displeased with you, if he did not agree with you when you were right, it is not he who is to blame but surely you, in that you were not kind when you had dealings with him.
