30 August

People know in what the good life of all humanity consists and how it can be attained; and if they know this, then sooner or later they will attain such a life.

1

> Jesus foresaw the end of the old society, already shaken in its moral foundations. He makes a prediction to his disciples that the temple, the material sign of the order of life established by men, must be destroyed in order to be replaced by a more perfect institution. And to this prediction, which was to come true shortly, he adds a prediction of similar events which will take place in later times, and presents these events in those familiar images in which the end of the world appeared to his contemporaries.

> We now live in the time he foretold. From one end of the earth to the other everything has been shaken loose. In all institutions, <!-- vol. 41, p. 603 --> of whatever kind, in all the various systems on which the social life of peoples has been founded, there is nothing firm. Everyone feels that all this must soon fall to ruin, and in this temple too no stone will be left upon a stone. But as the destruction of Jerusalem and its temple, from which the living God had withdrawn, prepared the building of a new city and a new temple, to which people of all tribes and all nations will flow together of their own will—so from the ruins of the present temples and cities a new city and temple will rise, destined to become the universal temple and the common homeland of the human race, now still divided by teachings hostile to one another, which make brothers strangers and sow among them godless hatred and loathsome wars. When that hour, known to God alone, of the union of peoples in one temple and in one city comes, then the reign of Christ will truly begin, the final fulfillment of his divine work. For he came only to teach people to unite in the law of love. Let the birth of this holy society be painful—what of that? You must not fear the last struggle of evil with good, which is already ready to be reborn: your duty is to fight. Each of you is a warrior of God. But in our time of frenzy and insane pride beware of false Christs and false prophets. Christ is not in the desert. He is not in hidden places, and certainly not in any of the gatherings of those people who, separating themselves from others, think that salvation is meant for them alone, and by this very thing deny Christ, who destroyed all barriers between people and promised peace and eternal joys to everyone who, loving God above all and his neighbor as himself, embodies this twofold love in his deeds. Where this love is, there is Christ. Do not seek him elsewhere: you will find only a deceptive shadow.

> *Lamennais.*

2

> Those who are on the top of a mountain see the sunrise sooner than the dwellers of the plains. So too with those who are on spiritual heights: they see the heavenly sunrise sooner than those living a fleshly life. But the time comes, and the sun rises so high that it becomes visible to all as well.

> *From “The World's Advance Thought.”*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 604 -->

3

> Is it really impossible to hope that the day will come when people will find that to live for others is as easy as they have often found it easy to die for them? All that is needed is an uplifting and enlightening of the spirit in people to make the life of man a noble and beautiful service to the brothers with whom God has united him.

> *Brown.*

4

> My imagination refuses to picture that blessed time when the meek inherit the earth. But this day will inevitably come, and the hopes of the poor are not vain. Not by force, not by power, but by his spirit he will call the meek to his judgment and teach them his ways.

> *John Ruskin.*

5

> Mighty forces are at work in the world. No one can stop them. The signs of this are: a new understanding of Christianity, respect for man, a new feeling of brotherhood and of the equal relation of all people to the father of all. We see this, we feel this. And before this all oppressions will fall. Society, silently permeated by this spirit, will exchange its eternal war for peace. The might of all-grasping and seemingly invincible selfishness will yield to this divine force. “On earth peace, and good will among people” will not always be only a dream.

> *Channing.*

As soon as an ideal higher than the former one is set before humanity, all the former ideals grow dim, like stars before the sun, and man cannot help recognizing the higher ideal, just as he cannot help seeing the sun.
