<!-- vol. 42, p. 186 -->

31 October

Nothing hinders the spread of truth so powerfully as obstinacy in holding on to ancient tradition sanctified by time.

<!-- vol. 42, p. 187 -->

1

> They say: God created man in his own image. This probably means that man created God in his own image.

> *Lichtenberg.*

2

> In the propensity to believe what we are told lie good and evil. It is precisely this propensity that makes possible the forward movement of society, and it is precisely this propensity that makes this forward movement so slow and painful; each generation, thanks to it, receives without effort the knowledge that comes to it by inheritance, acquired by hard labor, and each generation, thanks to it, finds itself enslaved by the mistakes and errors of its predecessors.

> *Henry George.*

3

Mankind moves forward slowly but without stopping, toward an ever greater clarification of the truth about the meaning and significance of its life and toward the establishment of a life in accord with this clarified consciousness. And therefore people's understanding of their life and human life itself are constantly changing. People who are more sensitive to the truth understand life in accordance with that higher light which is visible to them, and arrange their life in accordance with this light; people who are less sensitive hold to the former understanding of life and the former order of life and try to defend it.

So that in the world there are always, side by side with people who point out the advanced, latest expression of the truth and try to live in accordance with this expression, people who defend the former—outlived and already unneeded—understanding of it and the former orders of life.

4

Of all the deceptions of faith, the cruelest is the instilling of false faith in children. It consists in this: to a child who asks his elders, who lived before him and had the opportunity <!-- vol. 42, p. 188 --> to come to know the wisdom of people who lived earlier, what this world and its life are and what relation there is between the one and the other, they answer not what these elders themselves think and know, but what was thought by people who lived thousands of years ago and what none of the grown-ups any longer believes or can believe. Instead of the spiritual life necessary to him, which the child asks for, he is given a poison destroying his spiritual health, from which he can be healed only by the greatest efforts and sufferings.

*Disrespect for tradition has not done 1/1000 of the evil that is produced by this respect for customs, laws, and institutions that in our time no longer have any reasonable justification.*
