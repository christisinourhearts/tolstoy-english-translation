## THE REPENTANT SINNER

> And he said to Jesus: remember me, Lord, when you come into your kingdom! And Jesus said to him: truly I tell you, today you will be with me in paradise.

> *(Luke ch. 23, vv. 42, 43.)*

There lived in the world a man for seventy years, and he spent his whole life in sins. And this man fell ill and did not repent. And when death came, in the last hour he began to weep and said: “Lord! as you forgave the robber on the cross, forgive me!” He had only just said this when his soul left him. And the sinner's soul came to love God and believed in his mercy and came to the gates of paradise.

And the sinner began knocking and asking to be admitted into the kingdom of heaven.

And he heard a voice from behind the door: “What man is knocking at the gates of paradise? And what deeds did this man perform in his life?”

And the voice of the accuser answered and listed all the sinful deeds of this man. And it named no good deeds.

And the voice from behind the door answered: “Sinners cannot enter the kingdom of heaven. Go away from here.”

And the man said: “Lord! I hear your voice, but I do not see your face and do not know your name.”

And the voice answered: “I am Peter the Apostle.”

And the sinner said: “Take pity on me, Peter the Apostle, remember human weakness and God's mercy. Were you not <!-- vol. 41, p. 38 --> Christ's disciple, did you not hear his teaching from his own lips and see the example of his life? But remember—when he was in anguish and sorrow in his soul and three times asked you not to sleep, but to pray, and you slept, because your eyes were heavy, and three times he found you sleeping. It was the same with me.

“And remember too how you promised him himself that you would not deny him even unto death, and how you denied him three times when they led him to Caiaphas. It was the same with me.

“And remember too how the cock crowed, and you went out and wept bitterly. It was the same with me. You cannot refuse to let me in.”

And the voice behind the gates of paradise fell silent.

And after standing there a little while, the sinner again began knocking and asking to be admitted into the kingdom of heaven.

And another voice was heard from behind the gates and said: “Who is this man, and how did he live in the world?”

And the voice of the accuser answered, and again repeated all the sinner's bad deeds, and named no good deeds.

And the voice from behind the door answered: “Go away from here; such sinners cannot live together with us in paradise.”

And the sinner said: “Lord! I hear your voice, but I do not see your face and do not know your name.”

And the voice said to him: “I am King and Prophet David.” And the sinner did not despair, did not leave the gate of paradise, and began to say: “Take pity on me, King David, and remember human weakness and God's mercy. God loved you and exalted you before people. You had everything: kingdom, glory, wealth, wives, children, but you saw from the roof the wife of a poor man, and sin entered into you, and you took Uriah's wife and killed him himself by the sword of the Ammonites. You, a rich man, took from the poor man his last ewe lamb and destroyed him himself. The same did I. And remember afterward how you repented and said: I acknowledge my guilt and grieve over my sin. It was the same with me. You cannot refuse to let me in.”

And the voice behind the gates fell silent.

And after standing there a little while, the sinner again began knocking and asking to be admitted into the kingdom of heaven. And a third voice was heard from behind the gates and said: “Who is this man? And how did he live in the world?”

And the voice of the accuser answered and for the third time listed the man's bad deeds and named no good deeds.

And the voice from behind the door answered: “Go away from here. Sinners cannot enter the kingdom of heaven.”

<!-- vol. 41, p. 39 --> And the sinner answered: “I hear your voice, but I do not see your face and do not know your name.”

And the voice answered: “I am John the Theologian, the beloved disciple of Christ.”

And the sinner rejoiced and said: “Now you cannot refuse to let me in: Peter and David will let me in because they know human weakness and God's mercy. And you will let me in because there is much love in you. Was it not you, John the Theologian, who wrote in your book that God is love and that he who does not love does not know God? Was it not you who in old age said one word to people: ‘Brothers, love one another!’ How then will you now hate me and drive me away? Either renounce what you yourself said, or love me and let me into the kingdom of heaven.”

And the gates of paradise opened, and John embraced the repentant sinner and let him into the kingdom of heaven.

*L. Tolstoy.*
