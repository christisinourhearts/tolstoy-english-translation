<!-- vol. 42, p. 389 -->

30 December

The consciousness of the brotherhood and equality of all people is spreading more and more in humanity.

1

> He who said: “Come to me, all who labor and are burdened, and I will give you rest,” himself became through this word the center of all humanity, for all humanity lives under the weight of oppression and labor.

> Count those who do not bear this burden but lay it on others, those who profit from the labors of others and from oppressing them—are there many such? To one master a million slaves, to one fortunate man, in Satan's sense, a million beings bent down to the earth, which they water with sweat and tears. These dispossessed creatures are the sheep of the good shepherd, Christ's sheep, those for whom he gave his life. He calls them to himself, and little by little, as the promised times draw near, they, raising their heads, listen to his voice, recognize him, and make ready to follow him. From all the sheepfolds, from the midst of every people, these sheep will come running, for all belong to the good shepherd, and he will gather them. Scattered, disunited, they crowd together in vague expectation of the one who will lead them to pastures where they will not be given over into the power of hirelings, who, seeing the wolf approaching, abandon their sheep and run away, or of strangers, who, caring only for their own profit, for the satisfaction of their greed, appropriate them, clothe themselves in their wool, and feed on their flesh.

> The aim of Christ's earthly mission: to make of all people a people of brothers, to unite all people with one another by uniting them with God, to establish them in unity under the holy law of the infinite and boundless progress of love, which is the eternal life of all that exists.

> *Lamennais.*

<!-- vol. 42, p. 390 --> And having come to the good shepherd, all the sheep will gather around him, and there will be one flock and one shepherd.

2

> Do we understand our spiritual brotherhood? Do we understand our descent from one heavenly father, whose image we bear within us and to whose perfection we can constantly draw nearer? Have we recognized that in the souls of all people, just as in our own, there is one and the same divine life? And yet this alone constitutes the true, free bond of people with one another.

> To change the order of people's life, a new respect of people for one another is necessary. As long as people look at one another as they do now, almost as at cattle, they will not stop treating people like cattle, and will go on making them, by force or by cunning, into tools for attaining their own ends. There can be no brotherhood among people until they understand their kinship and relation to God and that great purpose for which life is given them. But now such thoughts are regarded as fantasies, and the teacher who hopes to find in people faith in their brotherhood and their sonship to God is regarded as a dreamer. And yet the recognition of this simplest truth of Christianity would turn the whole of society upside down and would establish among people relations such as we cannot imagine now. None of us can picture to himself that change in conduct, that tenderness, respect, gentleness, and that energy of effort for social improvement which would arise as people penetrated to the spiritual part of one another and understood the significance of the soul of every human being, even the lowest. Then those insults, griefs, and oppressions which we now do not even notice would outrage us more than the greatest crimes of today. Then every man would be sacred in the eyes of man, and an insult done to a man would appear as enmity <!-- vol. 42, p. 391 --> against God. Recognizing this truth, a man could not outrage his neighbor, because he would see the divine in him. One cannot imagine a truth as practical as this teaching. Yes, we need a new revelation—not about paradise and hell, but about that spirit which lives in us.

> *Channing.*

3

> *You cannot love either the one you fear or the one who fears you.*

> *Cicero.*

4

> People who preach morality and confine your duties within the limits of your family and your homeland are preaching egoism to you, more or less broad, but nonetheless harmful both to you and to others. The family and the homeland are two circles contained within a still wider circle—humanity. These are two steps that must be passed, but on which one should not stop.

> *Giuseppe Mazzini.*

The consciousness of one's unity with all humanity, flowing from the consciousness of the one divine source in all, gives people the highest good, both the inner—personal—and the outer—social. This consciousness is hindered most of all by superstitions of state, of nation, of class, and of religion. This consciousness is established by true religion.
