<!-- vol. 41, p. 104 -->

14 February

The spirit of God lives in man.

1

> If anyone is not born from above, he cannot see the kingdom of God.

> *John 3:3.*

2

> Reason can grow clear only in a good person. A person can be good only when reason has grown clear in him. For a good life the light of reason is needed; for the light of reason a good life is needed. One helps the other. And therefore, if reason does not help a good life, it is not real reason. And if life does not help reason, then it is not a good life.

> *Chinese wisdom.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 105 -->

3

> A merchant who had married a princess built her a palace, bought her costly clothes, set hundreds of servants to attend her, trying in every way to amuse her. But the princess pined and kept thinking of her royal stock. So it is with the soul in man. Though a man surround it with all earthly pleasures, it pines for its home, for that source—God, from whom it came.

> *The Talmud.*

4

> Although people do not know what good is, they have it in themselves.

> *Confucius.*

5

In ancient times there lived in Rome a sage, Seneca. He did not know Christ and his teaching, but he understood life in the same way as Christ. Here is what he wrote to his friend: “You do well, dear Lucilius (that was the friend's name), in trying by your own powers to keep yourself in a good and kind spirit. Every person can always attune himself so. For this there is no need to raise your hands to heaven or to ask the watchman at the temple to let us nearer to God, so that he may hear us better: God is always near you, he is within you. Yes, my dear Lucilius, I affirm that in us is a holy spirit, the witness and guardian of all that is good and bad. It deals with us as we deal with it. If we take care of it, it takes care of us.

In every good person God lives.”

6

> Just as you do not see the soul of a man, you do not see God, but you come to know him in his works. So also you cannot fail to recognize the divine power of the soul, which shows itself in its eternal striving toward perfection.

> *Seneca.*

*God lives in each of us. Nothing so holds a person back from evil and helps him to do good as the remembrance of this.*
