25 April

Man can be conscious of himself as a bodily and a spiritual being. Being conscious of himself as bodily, man cannot be free. For a spiritual being there cannot even be a question of any unfreedom.

1

> Truly, truly I say to you: he who hears my word and believes in him who sent me has eternal life and does not come to judgment, but has passed from death into life. Truly, truly I say to you: the time is coming, and has already come, when the dead will hear the voice of the son of God and, having heard, will come to life. For as the father has life in himself, so he has given to the son to have life in himself.

> *John 5:21–26.*

2

> What is “love of God” if not an intensified striving to bring into one's existence the highest creative energy. The divine creative power is contained in everything, but its greatest manifestation in this world is in man; and in order for it to act, man must recognize it.

> *Not recognizing that he can create the best, man inevitably creates the worst.*

> *From “The World's Advance Thought.”*

3

> I know that one must constantly watch over oneself, I know that heaven knows everything and that its laws are unchanging. I know that it sees everything, enters into everything, is present in everything. Heaven penetrates into the depth of all hearts just as daylight lights up a dark room. We must strive to reflect its light just as two musical instruments, tuned alike, answer each other.

> *The Chinese Shijing.*

4

> *The nature of man is upright. If this natural uprightness is lost in the course of life, then man cannot be happy.*

> *Chinese wisdom.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 283 -->

5

> When you think about the properties of the soul, it is much harder to understand what the soul is when enclosed in the body, where it lives as in a foreign country, than what it is when it has been freed from the body and united with him of whom it feels itself a part.

> *After Cicero.*

6

> Only when you say truly, from all your soul, that in all those things in which you know the will of God you have no will of your own, but do only what he wants, only then will you become fully free.

> *Epictetus.*

A person feels his freedom in the measure in which he transfers his life from fleshly existence into spiritual.
