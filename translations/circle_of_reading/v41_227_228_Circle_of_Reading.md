<!-- vol. 41, p. 227 -->

5 April

One can avoid fulfilling the law of labor only by sin: either by committing violence or taking part in it, or by flattery and servility before violence.

1

> It is better to lose one's life than to flatter the base. Poverty is better than luxury through serving the rich. Not to stand at the door of the rich man and not to speak with the voice of a petitioner—this is the best life.

> *Indian Hitopadesha.*

2

> *It is better for a person to die of starvation than to lose his innocence for the sake of getting bread.*

> *Thoreau.*

3

> There were two brothers: one served the king, and the other fed himself by the labor of his own hands. The rich brother once said to the poor one:

> — Why do you not enter the king's service? You would rid yourself of the burden of labor.

> To this the poor one said:

> *Saadi.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 228 --> — And why do you not work, so as to rid yourself of the burden of humiliation? The sages have said that it is better to eat in peace the bread of one's own labor than to wear a golden belt and be the servant of another. It is better to knead lime and clay with your hands than to fold them on your breast as a sign of your slavery. It is better to be content with a piece of bread than to bend your back like a slave.

4

> However fine the clothing bestowed by the king may be, one's own coarse clothing is better, and though the dishes of the rich are tasty, a piece of bread from one's own table is better.

> *Saadi.*

5

> It is better, much better, for every person to take a rope and go to the forest for firewood and sell a bundle of firewood for food than to ask people for it. If people do not give, it will be shameful and vexing, and if they do give, it will be still worse: you will be obligated to those who gave.

> *Muhammad.*

6

> To whoever does not work the earth, the earth says: “Because you do not work me with your right hand and your left, you will forever stand at other people's doors along with all the beggars, you will forever live on the leavings of the rich.”

> *Zoroaster.*

7

People live well if they are convinced that a life of labor is more worthy of respect than an idle one—are convinced of this, and live so, and in accordance with it value and respect people.

If you do not want to work—either do violence or abase yourself.
