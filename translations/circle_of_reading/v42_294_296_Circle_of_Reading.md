<!-- vol. 42, p. 294 -->

1 December

A woman does not differ from a man in her fundamental calling in life. This calling is the service of God. The difference is only in the object of service. Although the calling in a woman's life is the same as the calling of a man—in the service of God—and is carried out by the same means—love—for most women the object of this service is more definite than for a man. This object is the rearing and upbringing in love of more and more new workers in the work of God.

1

> Tell me, woman of luxury, if someone asked you which you would rather wish—to have a body clean, healthy, and beautiful, and to wear poor clothing, or to have a body ugly and sick, but to go about in gold and show off your finery. Would you not much sooner wish to have comeliness in the very nature of your body than in the splendor of your clothes? Will you, then, wish this in regard to the body, and the opposite in regard to the soul? Having a soul that is loathsome, deformed, and black, do you think you will gain anything by golden ornaments? Is this not the height of madness?

> *John Chrysostom.*

2

> If a woman's kindness is endless, it happens that there is no end to her malice either.

> *The Talmud.*

<!-- vol. 42, p. 295 --> A good wife is a very precious gift to her husband; an evil one is a malignant sore for him.

3

Gentle words, and few, are the best ornament of a woman.

Walk through a big city and look at what is sold in the best shops, what costs millions and is the product of the heavy, often ruinous labor of millions of workers. All these are articles of luxury used by women, such as can be done without. If only women understood the evil that their frivolous, needless luxury produces!

5

> *The more beautiful a woman is, the more honest she must be, because only by honesty can she counteract the harm that her beauty can do.*

> *Lessing.*

6

It is not the husband who chooses the wife, but the wife who chooses the husband. In order to choose the best father for her children, a woman must know what good is and what evil is. And it is precisely this that women ought to learn first of all.

7

A truly chaste girl who gives all the strength of maternal self-sacrifice given to her to the service of God, manifested in love for people, is the most beautiful and happy human being.

8

Nothing is so proper to a woman as self-sacrifice. And nothing so repels one from her as self-love.

Perfection for a man and for a woman is one and the same: the perfection of love. If a man often surpasses a woman in the reasonableness and firmness of love, a woman always surpasses a man in self-sacrifice in love.
