<!-- vol. 41, p. 424 -->

23 June

Only he can be free who places the essence of his life not in the bodily but in the spiritual life.

1

> A slave who is content with his position is doubly a slave, because not only his body is in slavery, but his soul too.

> *Burke.*

2

> If people do evil, they do evil to themselves; but to you they cannot do evil. You were born not to do evil and to sin together with people, but to help them in good deeds and to find your happiness in this.

> Of all that God grants us in this life, he has given one part into our full disposal: it constitutes, as it were, our property; the other part is outside our power, does not, so to speak, belong to us: everything that others can bind, do violence to, take away from us does not belong to us, and everything that no one and nothing can hinder or harm constitutes our property. And God in his goodness has given us as our property precisely that which is the real good. So God is not our enemy; he has dealt with us like a kind father: the only thing he has not given us is what cannot give us the good.

> And therefore the wise man cares only about doing the will of God, and reflects thus in the depth of his soul: if you wish, Lord, that I should still live, then I will live as you command, I will dispose of the freedom you have given me in all that belongs to me.

> But if you no longer need me, then let it be as you will.

> Until now I have lived on earth solely in order to serve you; and if you send me death, I will leave the world obeying you, like a servant who understands the commands and prohibitions of his master. And while I remain on earth, I want to be what you want me to be.

> *Epictetus.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 425 --> Know and remember that if a person is unhappy, he himself is to blame for it, because God created all people for their happiness, and not so that they should be unhappy.

3

> Peace is a great good, but if peace is attained by slavery, it becomes not a good but a calamity. Peace is freedom founded on the recognition of the rights of every person; slavery is the denial of the rights of man, of his human dignity. And therefore one must sacrifice everything to attain peace, and still more to be delivered from slavery.

> *After Cicero.*

4

> Remember that to change your opinion and to follow what corrects your mistake is more in keeping with freedom than persistence in your mistake.

> *Marcus Aurelius.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 426 -->

5

> I will call that soul alone free which acts from the inner motives of unchanging principles freely accepted by it. I will call free only that soul which does not submit to the slavery of custom, which is not content with old, acquired virtues, which does not shut itself up in fixed rules, which forgets what is behind, but listens to the calls of conscience and rejoices that it can strive toward new and higher tasks.

> *Channing.*

6

> Only that is a real duty which does not have our slavery as its aim. Only that is knowledge which promotes our freedom.

> Any other duty is only a new yoke. Any other knowledge is only an idle invention.

> *Vishnu Purana.*

*There is no middle ground: be a slave of God or of people.*
