<!-- vol. 41, p. 117 -->

20 February

Humanity goes forward unceasingly. There must be movement forward in faith too.

1

The order of people's life depends on their faith. Faith, with the movement of time, becomes ever simpler, more understandable, clearer, more in accord with true knowledge. And in accordance with the simplification and clarification of faith, people are more and more united with one another.

2

> If a person thinks that we must stop at that understanding of faith which has been revealed to us now, he is very far from the truth. The light that we have received was given to us not so that we should look at it unceasingly, but so that, thanks to it, new truths still hidden from us should be revealed to us.

> *After Milton.*

3

> The spirit of Jesus, which the mighty of this world strive to strangle from the heights of their thrones and pulpits, nevertheless shows itself brightly everywhere. Has not the gospel spirit penetrated the peoples? Are they not beginning to see the light? Have not the ideas of rights and of duties become clearer to everyone? Are not calls heard from all sides for more just laws, for institutions that protect the weak and are founded on just equality? <!-- vol. 41, p. 118 --> Is not the former enmity dying out between those whom sovereigns divided? Do not the peoples feel themselves brothers? Already the oppressors tremble, as if an inner voice foretold their speedy end. Alarmed by terrible visions, kings convulsively clutch in their hands the chains in which they have fettered the peoples, whom Christ came to set free and which will soon fall apart. An underground rumble troubles their sleep. In the secret depths of society a work is being done which they cannot stop with all the might of their power and whose unceasing success throws them into inexpressible alarm. It is the work of a germ ready to develop, the work of love, which will take away sin from the world, revive failing life, comfort the grieved, break the fetters of the imprisoned, and open to the peoples a new way of life, whose inner law will no longer be violence, but the love of people for one another.

> *Lamennais.*

4

Humanity moves forward only because faith moves forward. And the forward movement of faith consists not in the discovery of new religious truths, not in finding a new relation of man to the world and to its source—there is nothing new—but in casting off everything superfluous that has been attached to religious understanding. There are no new religious truths: ever since we have known reasonable man, his relation to the world and its source has been established as the same as it is now. And if there is movement in religion, it is not in discovering anything new, but only in purifying what has already been discovered and expressed.

5

Faith is the indicator of that higher understanding of life, accessible at a given time and in a given society to the best, foremost people, to which all the other people of that society inevitably and invariably draw near.

One must not confuse true progress, religious progress, with technical, scientific, artistic progress. Technical, scientific, artistic success can be very great together with religious backwardness, as is happening in our time.

<!-- vol. 41, p. 119 --> <br> *If you want to serve God—be first of all a worker for religious progress, which consists in the struggle with superstitions and in the clarification and simplification of religious consciousness*.
