<!-- vol. 41, p. 582 -->

19 August

Life is movement, and therefore the good of life is not a certain state but a certain direction of movement.

Such a direction is serving not oneself but God.

1

> Some seek the good, or happiness, in power, others in science, still others in sensuality. But those people who are really close to their good understand that it cannot lie in what only some people, and not all, can possess. They understand that the true good of man is such that all people can possess it at once, without division and without envy; it is such that no one can lose it unless he himself wants to.

> *Pascal.*

2

*Happiness is what a person desires for himself alone; the good is what a person desires for himself together with everyone. Happiness is attained by struggle, the good, on the contrary, by submission.*

3

> Real goods are few. A real good is only that which is a good and good for all.

> And therefore, if you want to be useful and not harmful to people, do only what agrees with the common good. Whoever acts so will acquire the good for himself.

> *Marcus Aurelius.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 583 -->

4

True good is the service of God. Service is always attained by spending one's animal life, just as light is attained only by spending combustible material.

5

> In doing good to our neighbors, we, without noticing it ourselves, do much more for ourselves than for others.

> *From “Pious Thoughts.”*

6

Do good without choosing to whom. The good you have done will not be lost, even if you have forgotten about it.

In doing good lies the only sure means of being happy.

The closer what a person does or experiences is to the true good, the more natural is the desire to share this good with others.
