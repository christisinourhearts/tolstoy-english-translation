<!-- vol. 42, p. 288 -->

27 November

If passion takes possession of you, remember that your passionate desire does not constitute your soul, but is only a dark film temporarily hiding its true qualities from you.

1

> Be a lamp for yourself. Be a refuge for yourself. Hold to the light of your lamp and do not seek another refuge.

> *Buddhist Sutta.*

2

> *The soul may be compared to a transparent sphere lit from within by its own light. This fire is for it not only the source of all light and truth, but also lights up for it everything external. In this state it is free and happy; only attachment to the external can agitate and darken its smooth surface, causing refraction and loss of light.*

> *Marcus Aurelius.*

3

> In all people there is a feeling of mercy, of shame, and of hatred of vice. Each can, by means of self-cultivation, make <!-- vol. 42, p. 289 --> these feelings grow or let them wither. These feelings constitute part of the man himself, just as do the members of his body. And these feelings, like the members of the body, can be cultivated. On the mountain Nikonshau beautiful trees grow. When their trunks are cut down, new shoots constantly grow up; if cattle are let in among them, the mountain will become bare. The baring of the mountain is not natural to it. So too with the corruption of the soul: if we allow base passions to devour the noble shoots of mercy, of shame, of hatred of vice in our heart, shall we say on that account that all these feelings are not in the heart of man? To know the law of heaven means to develop the higher properties of our nature.

> *Mencius.*

4

> In yourself, within you, is the source of good. It will not cease to murmur as you go on digging it out.

> *Marcus Aurelius.*

5

> The soul of man is a mirror in which one can see the image of divine reason.

> *John Ruskin.*

As soon as you feel the lust of passion, call up in yourself the consciousness of your divinity. As soon as you feel a darkening of your divinity, know that passions possess you—fight them.
