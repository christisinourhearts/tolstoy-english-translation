<!-- vol. 41, p. 277 -->

22 April

Knowledge of oneself is knowledge of God.

1

> But Jesus cried out and said: whoever believes in me believes not in me, but in him who sent me; and whoever sees me sees him who sent me. I, the light, have come into the world, so that everyone who believes in me should not remain in darkness. And if anyone hears my words and does not believe, I do not judge him, for I came not to judge the world, but to save the world. He who rejects me and does not receive my words has a judge for himself: the word that I have spoken; it will judge him on the last day. For I have not spoken from myself; but the father who sent me, he gave me a commandment, what to say and what to speak. And I know that his commandment is eternal life. So, what I speak, I speak as the father told me.

> *John 12:44–50.*

2

*The greatest knowledge is knowledge of oneself: whoever comes to know himself will also come to know God.*

3

The basic property of man—to love one thing more or less and not to love another—does not come from spatial and temporal conditions; but, on the contrary, spatial and temporal <!-- vol. 41, p. 278 --> conditions act or do not act on man only because man, on entering the world, already has a quite definite property of loving one thing and not loving another. Only from this comes the fact that people born and brought up in completely identical spatial and temporal conditions often present the sharpest contrast in their inner *I*.

4

> Without purity of soul, why worship of God? Why say: I will go to worship God? How can he who does evil worship God?

> Holiness is not in forests, not in the sky, not on the earth, not in sacred rivers. Cleanse your body, and you will see him. Turn the body into a temple, cast off bad thoughts, and contemplate God with the inner eye. When we come to know him, we come to know ourselves. Without personal experience, scripture alone will not destroy our fears—just as darkness is not dispelled by a painted fire. Whatever your faith and your prayers may be, as long as there is no truth in you, you will not reach the path of the good. He who comes to know the truth will be born again.

> The source of the true good is in the heart: mad is he who seeks it in another place. He is like a shepherd looking for a lamb that is in his bosom.

> Why do you gather stones and build great temples? Why do you torment yourselves so, when God lives constantly within you?

> A yard dog is better than a lifeless idol in the house, and better than all the demigods is the great God of the world.

> That light which, like the morning star, lives within the heart of every person, this light is our refuge.

> *Vamana Purana.*

5

> It is strange to shout to a person who does not know himself that he should pass from himself to God! It is good to say this to a person who knows himself.

> *Pascal.*

A person can transfer his *I* from a realm that is subordinate, inconstant, and calamitous into what is free, unchanging, and joyful: into the consciousness of his spiritual, divine essence.
