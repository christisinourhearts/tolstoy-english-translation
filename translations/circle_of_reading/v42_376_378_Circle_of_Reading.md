24 December

From childhood begins the growth of spiritual strength and the decrease of bodily strength. Like two cones turned apex to base, bodily strength decreases evenly and spiritual strength grows.

1

> Harmonious growth, both in nature and in man, is always accomplished in silence, in quiet; only everything destructive, vicious, and coarse is noisy.

> However, few understand the necessity of a life in quiet and silence for true spiritual growth and development. For the most part people live in bustle and turmoil and are bored when they have to remain alone.

> Only in the quiet of solitude can a person find the mighty force of life and growth. This very thing Christ said in the words: “But when you pray, go into your room”... The world is in terrible need of this silent growth for the realization of peace. It is distracted from true spiritual growth by thousands of voices promising it salvation in the form of various new teachings which supposedly can save it.

> *Lucy Mallory.*

<!-- vol. 42, p. 377 --> We need to be in quiet more, and the voice of silence will tell us that truth which will make us free.

2

> An enlightened person constantly rises in reason and insight; an unenlightened person, on the other hand, constantly sinks into ignorance and vice.

> *Chinese wisdom.*

3

> The older people who live a spiritual life become, the wider their mental horizon grows, the clearer their consciousness becomes; but people who live a worldly life grow duller and duller with the years.

> *The Talmud.*

4

> One must grow old to become kinder; I never come across a mistake that I would not have made already.

> *Goethe.*

5

> The ripening of the soul is more precious than brilliance and an abundance of strength, and the eternal in us must make use of the destruction that time produces in us.

> *Amiel.*

6

Physical growth is only the preparation of stores for spiritual work, for the service of God and people, which begins with the withering of the body.

7

> Everything in the world grows, blossoms, and returns to its root. Return to its root means repose in accordance with nature. In accordance with nature means eternal; therefore the destruction of the body holds no danger.

> *Laozi.*

<!-- vol. 42, p. 378 -->

8

*Grow spiritually and help others to grow. In this is the whole of life.*

Terrible is the condition of one who is not conscious of the spiritual life in himself and of its growth. There was bodily life alone—and that is being irresistibly destroyed and is about to vanish.

Become conscious of your spiritual essence, live by it, and instead of despair you will know a joy that nothing can disturb and that keeps on growing.
