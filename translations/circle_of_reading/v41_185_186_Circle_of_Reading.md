<!-- vol. 41, p. 185 -->

24 March

*Only he who fulfills the law of God comes to know God himself. And the more closely anyone fulfills the law of God, the more clearly and the better he comes to know God.*

1

> Jesus says to her: believe me, the time is coming when you will worship the father neither on this mountain nor in Jerusalem.

> But the time will come, and has come already, when the true worshipers will worship the father in spirit and truth, for the father seeks such worshipers for himself. God is spirit: and those who worship him must worship in spirit and truth.

> *John 4:21, 23, 24.*

2

There is not one believing person upon whom moments of doubt in the existence of God have not come. And these doubts are not harmful; on the contrary, they lead to a higher understanding of God.

The God whom you knew has become habitual, and you no longer believe in him. You believe fully in God only when he is revealed anew, and he is revealed to you from a new side when you seek him with all your soul. And the sides are countless in number.

3

> Moses says to God: “O Lord, where shall I find you?!” God said: “When you seek me, you have already found me.”

> We have no words to say what God is, but without words we know that he is.

> *Arab wisdom.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 186 --> A sage was asked why he knows that there is a God. He said: “Is a torch needed to see the sun?”

4

> Two kinds of people know God: people with a humble heart, whether they are clever or stupid, and people who are truly reasonable. Only people who are proud and of middling reason do not know God.

> *After Pascal.*

5

Just as any thing can be known only by coming close to it, so too you come to know God when you draw near to him. And one can draw near to God only by good deeds: by fulfilling his law. And the more you know God, the more willingly you fulfill his law. And the better you fulfill the law, the more closely you come to know God. One helps the other.

Among the Jews it is considered a sin to pronounce the name of God. And they are right—God is spirit. And every name is bodily, not spiritual.
