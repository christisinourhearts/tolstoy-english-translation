<!-- vol. 41, p. 567 -->

12 August

The cross that a person carries consists of a large vertical part, representing the will of God, and a small crosswise part, representing the will of man. Bring your will into one direction with the will of God, and there will be no cross.

1

> A person who seeks happiness in outward well-being builds his house on sand; lasting happiness can be given only by the agreement of the inner life with the will of God.

> *Lucy Mallory.*

2

> Whoever is not with me is against me; and whoever does not gather with me scatters.

> *Luke 11:23.*

3

In people's states there is a combination of good and evil, but in people's strivings there is no such mixture. Striving to fulfill the will of God is all good; striving to fulfill one's own will, not in agreement with the will of God, is all evil.

<!-- vol. 41, p. 568 -->

4

> Truly, truly I say to you: whatever you ask the father in my name, he will give you.

> *John 16:23.*

5

Fate crushes us in two ways: by refusing us our desires and by fulfilling them. But he who wants only what God wants escapes both misfortunes. Everything turns to his good.

*Amiel.*

6

> If you expect nothing and want to receive nothing from other people, then people cannot be frightening to you, as another bee is not frightening to a bee, as another horse is not frightening to a horse. But if your happiness is in the power of other people, then you will certainly be afraid of people.

> This is where one must begin: one must detach oneself from everything that does not belong to us, detach oneself so far that it is not our master; detach oneself from attachment to one's body and to everything needed for it; detach oneself from love of wealth, of fame, of posts, of honors; in this sense detach oneself from one's children, wife, brothers. One must say to oneself that all this is not our property.

> Then we will not need to destroy human violence with violence. Here is a prison. What harm is it to me, to my soul, that it stands there? Why should I destroy it, why attack the people who commit violence and kill them? Their prisons, chains, weapons will not enslave my spirit. My body they may take, but my spirit is free, and no one can hinder it in anything, and therefore I live as I want.

> And how did I come to this? I submitted my will to the will of God: does he want me to have a fever? And I want it. Does he want me to do this and not that? And I want it. Does he want something to happen to me? And I want it. He does not want it, and I do not want it. Does he want me to die, to be put to torture? And I want to die, I want to undergo torture.

> *Epictetus.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 569 -->

7

> That soul is great which gives itself up to God; that soul, on the contrary, is petty and depraved which rebels against God, condemns the laws that govern the world, and prefers to correct God rather than itself.

> *Seneca.*

8

> *Whoever wants to do his will will learn of this teaching, whether it is from God or whether I speak from myself.*

> *John 7:17.*

9

> Come to me, all who toil and are burdened, and I will give you rest; take my yoke upon you and learn from me, for I am meek and humble in heart, and you will find rest for your souls; for my yoke is good and my burden is light.

> *Matthew 11:28–30.*

Not only deliverance from misfortunes, not only peace, are given by merging one's own will with the will of God; but only by this path are the knowledge of God and faith in immortality obtained.
