27 March

The more a person believes in God, the less he fears people.

1

> Do not lose heart and do not despair if you do not fully succeed in carrying out in your actions all the good that you would wish.

> If you have fallen from your height, try to rise again—to bear the trial of life meekly and to come back willingly, consciously, to your foundations.

> *Marcus Aurelius.*

2

Whoever fears people does not fear God. And whoever fears God does not fear people.

3

> Honor him whose life is an unceasing victory—him who, because he strives toward the infinite and the true, finds support not in praise but in labor—who does not shine and does not wish to shine. Knowing beforehand that he will suffer for it, he has chosen that virtue which is subjected to abuse, that truth for whose destruction all its scattered enemies unite. The highest virtue is always contrary to worldly law.

> *Emerson.*

4

Every great truth, in order to enter the consciousness of humanity, must inevitably pass through three stages. The first stage: “This is so absurd that it is not even worth discussing.” The second stage: “This is immoral and contrary to religion.” The third stage: “Why, this has long been known to everyone.”

<!-- vol. 41, p. 198 -->

5

He who fears nothing and is always ready to give his life for the truth is much stronger than he whom everyone fears and who holds in his power the lives of other people.

6

*Seek the best person among those whom the world condemns.*

7

Do what you consider you must do, without expecting any glory for it. Remember that a foolish man is a poor judge of reasonable actions.

If you want to be saved from the power of people, give yourself up to the power of God. If you are conscious of being in the power of God, then people can do nothing to you.
