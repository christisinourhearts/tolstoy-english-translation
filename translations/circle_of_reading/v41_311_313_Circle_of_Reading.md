<!-- vol. 41, p. 311 -->

8 May

There is nothing more attractive than humility with kindness. But one must search for it: it does not put itself on display.

<!-- vol. 41, p. 312 -->

1

> Ahab said to a man who was walking behind him and abusing him: “If you have anything more to say against me, say it before we enter the town, or else others will hear and fall upon you.”

> *Egyptian wisdom.*

2

> And there was also a dispute among them as to which of them should be considered the greater. And he said to them: kings lord it over the nations, and those who have power over them are called benefactors; but you are not so: but whoever among you is greater, let him be as the lesser, and the one who rules as the one who serves. For who is greater: the one who reclines at table, or the one who serves? Is it not the one who reclines? But I am among you as one who serves.

> *Luke 22:24–27.*

3

Once in winter Francis was walking with Brother Leo from Perugia to the Portiuncula; it was so cold that they shivered with the cold. Francis called Brother Leo, who was walking ahead, and said to him: “O Brother Leo, God grant that our brothers may give an example of holy life throughout the whole earth; write down, however, that perfect joy is not in this.”

Going a little farther, Francis again called Brother Leo: “And write down also, Brother Leo, that if our brothers heal the sick, cast out devils, make the blind see, or raise men four days dead—write down that in this too there will be no perfect joy.”

And going still farther, Francis said to Leo: “Write down also, Brother Leo, that if our brothers knew all languages, all sciences, and all scriptures, if they prophesied not only about the future but knew all the secrets of conscience and of the soul—write down that in this too there is no perfect joy.”

Going still farther, Francis again called Leo and said: “And write down this too, Brother Leo, little lamb of God, that if we learned to speak in the tongues of angels, if we came to know the courses of the stars, and if all the treasures of the earth were revealed to us, and we came to know all the secrets of the life of birds, fishes, all animals, people, trees, <!-- vol. 41, p. 313 --> stones, and waters—write down that this too would not be perfect joy.”

And going a little farther, Francis again called Brother Leo and said to him: “Write down also that if we were such preachers that we converted all the heathen to the faith of Christ—write down that in this too there would be no perfect joy.”

Then Brother Leo said to Francis: “In what, then, Brother Francis, is perfect joy?”

And Francis answered: “It is in this: in that if, when we come to the Portiuncula dirty, wet, stiff with cold, and hungry, and ask to be let in, and the gatekeeper says to us: ‘Why do you tramps roam about the world, tempting the people, stealing the alms of poor people—get out of here!’—and does not open to us. And if we then are not offended, and think with humility and love that the gatekeeper is right, that God himself moved him to treat us so, and, wet, cold, and hungry, stay in the snow and the water until morning without murmuring against the gatekeeper, then, Brother Leo, only then will there be perfect joy.”

4

> That rivers and seas hold sway over the valleys through which they flow comes from their being lower than the valleys.

> Therefore the holy man, if he wants to be above the people, must become lower than they. If he wants to lead them, he must be behind them.

> Therefore, if the holy man does stand above the people, the people do not feel it. If he stands in front of the people as well, the people do not see it and do not suffer from it. He disputes with no one, no one in the world disputes with him. That is why the world praises him unceasingly.

> *Laozi.*

5

*A sage was told that he was considered a bad man. He answered: “It is a good thing they do not know everything about me, they would say worse than that.”*

Avoid judging yourself and, above all, comparing yourself with others. Compare yourself only with perfection.
