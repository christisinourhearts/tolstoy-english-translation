<!-- vol. 41, p. 336 -->

20 May

For a person as an animal being, there can be no talk of freedom. His whole life is conditioned by a series of causes. But if a person is conscious of himself as a spiritual being, then for him there can be no talk of unfreedom. The concept of unfreedom does not apply to the manifestation of reason, consciousness, love.

1

> Remember that your understanding, having the property of life in itself, makes you free if you do not bend it to the service of the flesh. The soul of a person, enlightened by understanding, free from the passions that darken this light, is a real fortress, and there is no refuge for a person that would be surer and more inaccessible to evil. Whoever does not know this is blind, and whoever, knowing it, does not enter the fortress of understanding is wretched.

> *Marcus Aurelius.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 337 -->

2

> *You will know the truth, and the truth will make you free.*

> *John 8:32.*

3

> Evil does not exist for material nature, but evil exists for every person to whom are given the consciousness of good and the freedom to choose between good and evil.

> *Marcus Aurelius.*

4

> Only he is a free person with whom everything happens as he wants it to. But does this mean that everything he takes a fancy to will certainly happen to him? Not at all. Literacy, for example, teaches us to write in letters and words everything we may want; but to write even my own name, I cannot write whatever letters I fancy: that way I will never write my name. Rather, I must wish to write exactly the letters that are needed, and in the order that is needed. And so in everything. We would never learn anything if we did just as we fancied. So, in order to be a free person, one should not wish for everything that merely comes into one's head. On the contrary, a free person must learn to wish for and agree with everything that happens to him, because what happens to a person happens only by the will of the one who governs the whole world.

> *Epictetus.*

5

> We are much more clearly conscious that our will is free than that everything that happens must have its cause. Could one not turn this argument around and say: our concepts of cause and effect must be very wrong, since our will could not be free if they were correct.

> *Lichtenberg.*

6

> To be highly moral means to be free in soul. People who are constantly angry at someone, incessantly <!-- vol. 41, p. 338 --> afraid of something, and wholly given over to passions cannot be free in soul.

> *Confucius.*

People who deny freedom are like the blind who deny colors: they do not know the realm in which people are free.
