<!-- vol. 41, p. 561 -->

11 August

*As a person dies alone, so a person always lives his inner spiritual life alone.*

1

> Happiest of all is the country that has little or no need of imports; so too happiest of all is the person who is content with his inner wealth and for his existence needs little or nothing from outside. Supplies from outside come dear, give rise to dependence, bring dangers in their train, cause vexation, and in the end serve as a poor substitute for the products of one's own soil. From others, from outside in general, one cannot expect much in any respect whatever. What one person can be for another is confined within very narrow limits: in the end each remains alone with himself, and then the whole question is *who* is the one with whom a person must remain when he remains alone with himself.

> *Schopenhauer.*

2

> If some unpleasantness happens to us or we fall into some difficulty, we are all inclined to blame other people or our fate for it, instead of considering that if the external, which does not depend on us, becomes an unpleasantness or difficulty for us, then something in us ourselves is not in order.

> *Epictetus.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 562 -->

3

> What a man does, that he possesses. Let no one consider anything a lasting good except what is in himself and what must grow in him as long as he lives.

> *Emerson.*

4

> You yourself commit sin, you yourself plot evil, you yourself flee from sin, you yourself purify your thoughts, by yourself you are evil or pure; another cannot save you.

> *Dhammapada.*

5

The most common and harmful error of people: to think that anything can hinder their freedom and good.

6

A person asks people or God to help him; but no one can help him except himself, because only his good life can help him. And this only he himself can do.

Every person has a depth of inner life whose essence cannot be communicated to another. Sometimes one wants to convey it to people. But at once one feels that it is impossible to convey it fully to another person.

This need is the need for communion with God. Establish this communion and do not seek another.
