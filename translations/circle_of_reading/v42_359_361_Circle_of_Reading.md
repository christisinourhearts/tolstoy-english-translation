<!-- vol. 42, p. 359 -->

20 December

The church's perversion of Christianity has put the realization of the kingdom of God further from us, but the truth of Christianity is like the fire in a bonfire smothered for a time by damp brushwood heaped on it: the fire has already dried the damp twigs and is beginning to catch them and break out. The true meaning of Christianity is now already visible to all, and its influence is already stronger than the deception that hides it.

1

> We must free the religion that Jesus professed from the religion whose object is Jesus. And when we come to know the state of consciousness that makes up the basic cell and the principles of the eternal Gospel, we will have to hold to it.

> As the wretched lamps of a village illumination or the little candles of processions go out before the great miracle of the light of the sun, so the petty, local, accidental, and doubtful miracles will go out before the law of the life of the spirit, before the great spectacle of human history guided by God.

> *Amiel.*

2

> I see a new religion, founded on trust in man; calling to those untouched depths that live in us; preaching that man can love the good without thought of reward, that the divine principle lives in man.

> *Salter.*

3

> What we need, what the people need, what our age demands in order to find a way out of the mire of egoism, doubt, and denial in which it is sunk, is a faith in which our souls could stop wandering in search of personal aims and could all go together, recognizing one origin, one law, one aim. Every strong faith that arises on the ruins of old, outlived beliefs changes the existing social order, since <!-- vol. 42, p. 360 --> every strong faith is inevitably applied to every branch of human activity.

> Mankind repeats, in different expressions and in different degrees, the words of the Lord's prayer: “thy kingdom come on earth, as in heaven.”

> *Mazzini.*

4

> There are people who love only themselves; these are people of hatred, because to love only oneself means to hate others.

> There are people of pride, who cannot bear their equals and always want to command and rule.

> There are people of greed, who demand gold, honors, pleasures, and are never sated.

> There are people who are robbers, who watch for the weak man in order to rob him by force or cunning, and who prowl around the house of the widow and the orphan.

> There are people of murder, who are full of thoughts of violence. They say: you are our brothers, and they kill those whom they call their brothers as soon as they suspect them of opposing their designs, and with their blood they write their laws.

> There are people of fear, who tremble before the wicked man and kiss his hand, hoping by this to escape his oppression.

> All these people destroy peace, security, and freedom on earth.

> But what could these oppressors of peoples do if they were left to themselves, without the support of the people?

> If, to keep the people in slavery, they used only the help of those to whom slavery is profitable, what would this small number mean against whole peoples?

> The wisdom of God has so arranged the world that people could always resist despotism, and despotism would be impossible if people understood the wisdom of God.

> But the rulers of the world set against the wisdom of God the wisdom of the prince of this world—the devil, and the devil, the king of the oppressors of peoples, taught them a hellish cunning in order to establish their despotism.

> He said to them: “This is what must be done. Take the strongest young men in every family, give them weapons and teach them to use them, and they will fight against their <!-- vol. 42, p. 361 --> fathers and brothers, because I will instill in them that this is their glory. I will make them two idols, which will be called honor and loyalty and whose law will be called unquestioning obedience. And they will worship these idols and blindly submit to this law, because I will pervert their minds, and you will have nothing to fear.”

> And the oppressors of peoples did what the devil told them, and the devil did what he had promised the oppressors of peoples.

> And so men of the people raised their hands against their own, to beat their brothers and imprison their fathers and even to forget those who had carried them under their hearts. And when they were told: “In the name of all that is holy, think of the injustice and cruelty of what you are ordered to do,” they answered: “We do not think, we obey.”

> And when they were told: “Have you really no love for your fathers, mothers, brothers?” They answered: “We do not love, we obey.”

> And when they were told of God and Christ, they said: “Our gods are loyalty and honor.”

> Truly I say to you: there has been no temptation more terrible than this.

> But this temptation is coming to an end.

> A little longer, and the devil will vanish together with the oppressors of peoples.

> *Lamennais.*

5

*One cannot hope to see the coming of the kingdom of God, but there is no need to doubt that it will come. It is drawing nearer without ceasing.*

Do not think that church Christianity is an incomplete, one-sided, formal Christianity, but still Christianity. Do not think so: church Christianity is not only not Christianity, but the most evil enemy of true Christianity. This church Christianity now stands in relation to true Christianity like a criminal caught at the scene of the crime. It has only two ways out: either to destroy itself, or to commit more and more new crimes. And however hopeless its position, it still goes on with its terrible, criminal activity.
