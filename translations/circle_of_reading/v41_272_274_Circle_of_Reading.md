21 April

The impending change in the order of life of the people of our Christian world consists in replacing violence with love, in recognizing the possibility, the ease, the blessedness of a life founded not on violence and the fear of it, but on love.

1

*It is difficult to accustom oneself not to pity people for what they themselves are sorry about in themselves: the loss of property, family, beauty, health, human glory, but to pity them for that in which they are truly pitiable: the loss of their morality, purity of reason, good habits. And yet such an attitude toward people is necessary in order to fulfill one's duties toward them.*

2

> This I command you, that you love one another. If the world hates you, know that it hated me before you. If you were of the world, then the world would love you; but because you are not of the world, but I have chosen you out of the world, therefore the world hates you.

> *John 15:17–19.*

3

People think that there are situations in which one can deal with a person without love, but there are no such situations. With things one can deal without love: one can chop trees, make bricks, forge iron without love, but with people one cannot <!-- vol. 41, p. 273 --> deal without love, just as one cannot deal with bees without caution. The nature of bees is such that if you start dealing with them without caution, you will harm them and yourself. It is the same with people.

And this cannot be otherwise, because mutual love between people is the basic law of human life. It is true that a person cannot force himself to love as he can force himself to work; but it does not follow from this that one may deal with people without love, especially if you demand something of them. If you do not feel love for people—sit quietly, occupy yourself with yourself, with things, with whatever you like, only not with people. Just as one can eat without harm and with benefit only when one wants to eat, so one can deal with people with benefit and without harm only when one loves. Only allow yourself to deal with people without love—and there are no limits to cruelty and brutality toward other people, and there are no limits to suffering for yourself.

4

> Until I see that the most important rule of Christ—love of enemies—is kept, until then I will not cease to doubt that those who give themselves out as Christians are Christians.

> *Lessing.*

5

As soon as conditions are admitted under which a person may do to his neighbor what he would not want for himself: all kinds of violence, such as all punishments and even killing, then the whole teaching about love becomes empty words.

6

> One must not do the slightest evil in order to bring success to the greatest good.

> *Pascal.*

7

> The most ruinous error that was ever made in the world was the separation of political science from moral science.

> *Shelley.*

Do not live so that, being in agreement with the world, you are ready to continue such a life. If you live so, you put off the approach of the kingdom of love. Live so that the coming of this kingdom may be possible. And in order to live so, found your life not on violence, but on love.
