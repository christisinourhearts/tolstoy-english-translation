17 November

We suffer from the past and spoil our future only because we neglect the present. The past and the future are dreams; the present alone really exists.

<!-- vol. 42, p. 259 -->

1

Be attentive to the present. Only in the present do we come to know eternity.

*After Goethe.*

2

> One of the most common delusions is that the present hour is not the critical, decisive hour. Write in your heart that every day is the best day of the whole year.

> *Emerson.*

3

> In every generation honor your outstanding people, and do not say: “their predecessors were worthier.”

> *The Talmud.*

4

Nothing is important except what we are doing at the present minute.

5

> *Use your vessel (your body) now; tomorrow it may break.*

> *The Talmud.*

6

> Whether you have been doing what you need to do takes on enormous importance, because the only meaning of your life lies in this: whether you are doing it in this short term of life given to you, whether you are doing what he who sent you into life wants of you?

> *The Talmud.*

7

> There is neither past nor future, for who has ever penetrated into those phantom realms. There is only the present. Do not worry about tomorrow, because there is no tomorrow. Live in today and for today, and if your today is good, then it is good forever.

> *The magazine “Whim.”*

When it is hard for you, and you suffer from memories of the past or are anxious about the future, remember that life is only in the present, direct all your strength upon it, and all your sufferings over the past and your anxieties about the future will vanish, and you will feel freedom and joy.
