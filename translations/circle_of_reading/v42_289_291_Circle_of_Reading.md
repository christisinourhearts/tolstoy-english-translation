<!-- vol. 42, p. 289 -->

28 November

Life is not destroyed, but only modified, by death.

1

> Sufficient unto the day is its evil. Do not waste your life in doubts and fears. Give yourself to the work of life, being sure that the good fulfillment of the duties of the present hour is the best preparation for the hours or ages that will follow it.

> Jesus explained nothing about eternal life, but his influence carried people out of time, and they felt themselves eternal.

> *Emerson.*

<!-- vol. 42, p. 290 --> Our future state will always seem an illusion to our present state. What matters is not the length of life, but its depth. The point is not the prolongation of life, but to take the soul out of time, as every lofty act of the soul does; when we live a full life, we do not ask ourselves the question of time.

2

> The house in which a person lives may be destroyed and annihilated; but the dwelling that the spirit builds for itself from pure thoughts and good deeds does not fear even eternity, and nothing can do harm to the one who lives in it.

> *Lucy Mallory.*

3

One cannot believe in a *future* life; one can not only believe, but know, that the life of the present is indestructible.

4

*Faith in immortality is given not by reasoning, but by life.*

5

What convinces one of the necessity of a future life is not arguments, but this: when you walk through life hand in hand with a person, and suddenly this person vanishes *there in nowhere*, and you yourself stop before this abyss and look in there.

6

The degree of fear that we feel before death is an indicator of the degree of truth of our understanding of life.

The less fear of death, the more freedom, calm, consciousness of the power of the spirit, and joy of life. With complete liberation <!-- vol. 42, p. 291 --> from this fear, with full consciousness of the unity of this life with infinite truth, there must be complete calm, undisturbed by anything.

The consciousness of immortality is natural to the soul of man. Only the evil that we do, and in the measure in which we do it, deprives us of the consciousness of our immortality.
