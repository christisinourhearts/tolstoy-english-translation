<!-- vol. 42, p. 60 -->

21 September

The easiest and smallest manifestation of a person's freedom consists in choosing among several indifferent actions: to go right or left or to stay where one is. A more difficult and higher manifestation is the choice between following the pull of a feeling and abstaining from it. The most important and necessary manifestation of freedom is to give one's thought one direction or another.

1

> *Work at purifying your thoughts. If you have no bad thoughts, there will be no bad actions either.*

> *Confucius.*

2

> Try not to think about what you consider bad.

> *Epictetus.*

3

> Everything is in the power of Heaven except our desire to serve God or ourselves.

> *Luther.*

<!-- vol. 42, p. 61 --> We cannot prevent birds from flying over our head, but we have the power not to let them nest on it. In just the same way we cannot prevent bad thoughts from flashing through our head, but it is in our power not to let them build themselves a nest there in order to brood and hatch evil actions.

4

> There is hardly anything more necessary for knowledge, for a calm life, and for the success of any undertaking than a person's ability to master his thoughts.

> *Locke.*

5

Thoughts are like guests; we are not responsible for their first visit. But they will visit us again, and often, only according to how we receive them. What you think today, you will do tomorrow.

6

> “He wronged me, he triumphed over me, he enslaved me, he insulted me”—in a heart troubled by such thoughts hatred will never be extinguished.

> “He wronged me, he triumphed over me, he enslaved me”—whoever gives no refuge in himself to such thoughts will stifle hatred in himself forever.

> For what comes from hatred is not overcome by hatred: it is extinguished by love—such is the eternal law.

> *Buddhist wisdom. Dhammapada.*

7

> When the view of things has been settled, knowledge will be acquired; when knowledge has been acquired, the will will strive toward truth; when the striving of the will is satisfied, the heart will become good.

> *Confucius.*

<!-- vol. 42, p. 62 -->

8

> Watch over yourself in thought, watch over yourself in word, guard your actions from everything bad. Keeping these three paths pure, you will enter the path of truth.

> *Buddhist wisdom. Dhammapada.*

9

> It is a sin not only to do what is bad but also to think it.

> *Zoroaster.*

A feeling arises independently of a person's will; but it is in a person's power, by thought, to approve or not approve the feeling, and therefore to encourage or restrain it.
