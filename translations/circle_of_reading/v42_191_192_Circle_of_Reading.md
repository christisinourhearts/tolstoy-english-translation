<!-- vol. 42, p. 191 -->

2 November

A deed done only for human glory is always bad, whatever its consequences may be. A deed in which the desire for good and the desire for human glory have taken part in equal share is an indifferent deed. A good deed is only one in which the chief motive is the fulfillment of the will of God.

1

> A free being that gives itself over to itself thereby gives itself over to the devil; in the moral world there is no land without a master, and unclaimed lands belong to the evil one.

> *Amiel.*

2

> If you knew from what source people's judgments and interests flow, you would stop seeking the approval and praise of people.

> *Marcus Aurelius.*

3

If you care about the approval of people, you will never resolve on anything. People's judgments are infinitely varied. You will say, “I seek the approval of good people,”—but after all, you call good people those who, you know, will approve the deed you have in mind.

<!-- vol. 42, p. 192 -->

4

> We are not content with our true inner life; we want to live yet another, imaginary life in the thoughts of people, and for this we force ourselves to seem other than what we are in reality. We labor unceasingly at adorning this imaginary being and neglect the real one. If we possess calm, fidelity, magnanimity, we try as quickly as possible to make it known, so as to attribute these virtues to the imaginary being.

> To give these qualities to the imaginary being, we are capable of depriving ourselves of them. We are ready to be cowards, if only to be reputed brave.

> *Pascal.*

5

In every good deed there is a share of the desire for people's approval. It is a misfortune if the deed is done only for human glory; but if a share of the desire for approval also takes part in the desire for good, the good deed remains good. But how much better it would be if it were done only for God.

*Let the approval of people be a consequence of your deed and not its aim.*

In order to accustom yourself to live only for God, it is good to do deeds of which no one will ever know. Do such deeds, and you will know a special joy.
