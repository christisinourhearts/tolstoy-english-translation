3 August

We seek requital in time for the good and evil we have done, and often do not find it. But good and evil are done in the spiritual realm, outside time, and in this realm, although we do not see clear signs of this requital, we undoubtedly are conscious of it in our conscience.

1

> It seems that life is easy for the shameless man, the braggart, the trickster, the reviler, the insolent man, and the idler; that the life of that mortal is hard who unceasingly strives for what is blameless, is always meek, reasonable, unselfish; but this only seems so. The first is always uneasy, the second always calm.

> *Buddhist wisdom. Dhammapada.*

2

> Hasten to a good deed, even an insignificant one, and flee from every sin; for one good deed draws another after it, and one sin gives birth to another: the reward of virtue is virtue, the punishment of vice is vice.

> *The Talmud.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 547 -->

3

Punishment lies only in the consciousness that you have not made use of the great good that you could have received. Do not expect greater punishments: there can be none heavier than this.

4

> *You seek the cause of evil. It is only in you.*

> *Rousseau.*

5

> What you do to others, you do to yourself. That is why every deed of mercy makes you feel kindness in yourself, and a deed of cruelty, malice.

> *Lucy Mallory.*

6

Do good without picking and choosing to whom. The good you have done will not be lost, even if you forget about it.

7

To do good is the only act that surely gives us the good.

8

> What you sow, you will reap. Strike, and you will suffer. Serve, and you will be served. If in your life you serve people, then by no cunning can you escape reward.

> *Emerson.*

9

> When you have done someone good and this good has borne fruit, why do you still seek praise and reward for your good deed?

> *Marcus Aurelius.*

Do not seek visible requital for good: it is given to you simultaneously with the act. And do not think that if you do not see requital for the evil you have done, there will be none. It is already in your soul. You are mistaken in ascribing the pain of your soul to other causes.
