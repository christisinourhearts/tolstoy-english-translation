16 September

Doubts do not destroy faith but strengthen it.

1

> I do not undertake to draw an undrawable line between God and us. The decisions of the will are undoubtedly ours, but in the higher realm, in the realm of free thought and feeling, one cannot fail to recognize his <!-- vol. 42, p. 51 --> presence. All that is deepest in us is only his reflection.

> He constantly inspires us, never ceases to act through us, if only we consent to desire and to do what he wants. He assists our moral efforts, upholds us in truth, accepts our cooperation in the struggle with evil, and reveals to us a multitude of things too beautiful to be expressed in words.

> But the slightest unfaithfulness to him—and he leaves us.

> *Martineau.*

2

> *Unbelief is not in whether a person believes or does not believe, but in a person's professing what he does not believe.*

> *Martineau.*

3

There are moments when you stop believing in the life of the spirit.

This is not unbelief; these are periods of faith in the life of the flesh.

A person who understands that his life is spiritual suddenly begins to fear death. This always happens when he is befogged by something and again begins to believe that the fleshly life is life, just as in the theater you can forget yourself and believe that what you see on the stage is really happening, and be frightened by what you see there, on the stage.

The same thing happens in life too.

But even in these moments of illusion a religious person knows that what happens in his fleshly life cannot deprive him of the good of his true life.

In periods of low spirit, one must treat oneself as a sick person—not stir.

4

> A wise man may doubt in his very best moments. That his doubt is unhindered is the basis of his certainty. True faith is always accompanied by doubts. If I could not doubt, I would not believe.

> *Thoreau.*

It is not the one who doubts his existence and is tormented by this doubt who is far from God, but the one who has believed on someone's word in the existence or nonexistence of God and does not doubt what he has been told.
