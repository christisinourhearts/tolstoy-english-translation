<!-- vol. 41, p. 168 -->

15 March

Only he knows true love who loves people who are unpleasant and hostile. The test of true love is love for enemies.

1

A person who loves us, who is pleasing to us, can be loved with human love; but only an enemy can be loved with divine love. Loving with human love, one can pass from love to hatred; but divine love cannot change. Nothing, not even death, can destroy it. It is the essence of the soul.

2

> If you do good to those who do good to you, what thanks is there to you for that? For sinners too do the same. And if you lend to those from whom you hope to receive back, what thanks is there to you for this? For sinners too lend to sinners, to receive back as much. But you, love your enemies, and do good, and lend, expecting nothing; and your reward will be great, and you will be sons of the most high, for he is good even to the ungrateful and the evil. And so be merciful, as your father also is merciful.

> *Luke 6:33–36.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 169 -->

3

> *Love your enemies—and you will have no enemy.*

> *The Teaching of the Twelve Apostles.*

4

“You have heard that it was said: love your neighbor and hate your enemy (Leviticus 19:17–18). But I say to you: love your enemies, bless those who curse you, do good to those who hate you, and pray for those who offend you and persecute you; that you may be sons of your heavenly father; for he commands his sun to rise over the evil and the good and sends rain on the righteous and the unrighteous.

*Matthew 5:43–45.*

If God makes all people equal, makes no difference between the evil and the good—and he knows people's hearts—how can we, not knowing what goes on in people's hearts, divide them by appearance alone and love some and not love others.

5

The passionate preference of some people over others, which is wrongly called love, is only a wild stock on which true love can be grafted and bear its fruit. But just as a wild stock is not an apple tree and bears no fruit, or bears bitter fruit instead of sweet, so partiality is not love and does not give good to people, or produces still greater evil.

6

The shoot of love, tender when it first appears, not tolerating a touch, is mighty only when it has grown out. Whatever people will do to it is only the worse for it. It needs one thing—that nothing should hide from it the sun of reason, which alone makes it grow.

7

> The most perfect of people is he who loves all his neighbors and does them good without distinction, whether they are good or bad.

> *Muhammad.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 170 -->

8

Set meekness against depravity: a sharp sword does not cut soft silk.

With gentle words and kindness one can lead an elephant on a hair.

*Saadi.*

Every time someone offends you and you feel malice toward a person, try to remember that all people are equally sons of God and that, however unpleasant a person may be to you, you must not stop loving him as a brother, a son of God the same as you.
