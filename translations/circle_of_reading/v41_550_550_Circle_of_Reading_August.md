## THE GREAT BEAR (THE DIPPER)

Long, long ago there was a great drought on the earth: all the rivers, streams, and wells dried up, and the trees, bushes, and grasses withered, and people and animals were dying of thirst.

One night a little girl went out of the house with a dipper to look for water for her sick mother. Nowhere did the girl find water, and from weariness she lay down in a field on the grass and fell asleep. When she woke up and took hold of the dipper, she nearly spilled the water out of it. It was full of clean, fresh water. The girl was glad and was about to drink, but then she thought that there would not be enough for her mother, and she ran home with the dipper. She was in such a hurry that she did not notice a little dog under her feet, stumbled over it, and dropped the dipper. The little dog whined pitifully. The girl reached for the dipper.

She thought she had spilled it, but no, it stood upright on its bottom, and all the water was still in it. The girl poured some water into her palm, and the little dog lapped it all up and cheered up. Then the girl took hold of the dipper again; from wooden it had become silver. The girl brought the dipper home and gave it to her mother. The mother said: “I am going to die anyway; better drink it yourself,” and gave the dipper to the girl. And at that same moment the dipper turned from silver to gold. Then the girl could no longer hold back and was just about to put her lips to the dipper, when suddenly a wanderer came in at the door and asked for a drink. The girl swallowed her saliva and held out the dipper to the wanderer. And suddenly seven huge diamonds sprang out on the dipper, and a great stream of clean, fresh water poured from it.

And the seven diamonds began to rise higher and higher, and rose up into the sky, and became the Great Bear.

*From the English.*
