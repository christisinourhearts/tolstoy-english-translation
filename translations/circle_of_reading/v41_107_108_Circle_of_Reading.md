<!-- vol. 41, p. 107 -->

16 February

The younger and the less thoughtful a person is, the more he believes that the essence of his life is in the body. The older and the more reasonable he is, the more and more he comes to know the foundation of life, both his own and that of the whole world, in the spiritual.

1

It is good to recall more often that our true life is not that outward, bodily one alone which we live through here, on earth, but that together with this life there is in us another, inner life—the spiritual.

Our visible bodily life is the same as scaffolding for the construction of a building. The scaffolding by itself is needed only as long as the building is being built. When the building is finished, the scaffolding is not needed and is taken down. The same with our bodily life. It is needed only for the construction of the building of spiritual life, and when this building has been built, the body is destroyed.

When we see huge, tall scaffolding fastened with iron, while the building itself barely rises above the foundation, it seems to us that the whole matter is in the scaffolding and not in the building. The same seems so to us when we see our whole life in our body.

It is good to remind ourselves and one another that, just as scaffolding is there only so that a building can be built, so our body is there only so that spiritual life may grow.

<!-- vol. 41, p. 108 -->

2

> Look at the sky and at the earth and think: all this is passing, all these, both mountains, and rivers, and various forms of life, and works of nature. All this passes away. As soon as you clearly understand this, enlightenment will at once appear, and you will know that which is and does not pass away.

> *Buddhist saying.*

3

*We marvel at the size of buildings, mountains, heavenly bodies, we calculate in them millions of cubic feet, of poods, and all these things that seem so great are nothing in comparison with that which knows about all this. The most powerful thing in the world is that which is not seen, not heard, and cannot be touched.*

4

> Remember that it is not you who are mortal but your body, that it is not your body that is alive but the spirit in the body. It is not your body that makes your spirit understand your life and the life of the world, but the spirit living in you moves, feels, remembers, foresees, governs, and directs your body and your actions. And just as an invisible power governs your body, so there must also be that invisible power which governs the whole world.

> *After Cicero.*

Only by freeing himself from the deception of the senses, which recognize the *bodily* world as truly existing and important, can a person understand his true purpose and fulfill it.
