1 May

For a person who places his life in spiritual perfecting there can be no fear of external events.

1

> Abu Hanifa died in prison in Baghdad, where he had been thrown by the caliph al-Mansur for refusing to recognize the teaching of Kadda. This famous teacher, having once received a heavy blow, said to the man who had struck him: “I can repay wrong with wrong, but I will not do it. I can complain of you to the caliph, but I will not complain. I can tell God in my prayers of the insult you have done me, but I will refrain from it. On the day of judgment I will be able to call down divine vengeance upon you, but if that day were to come now and my prayers were heard, I would enter paradise only together with you.”

> *Persian wisdom (from d'Herbelot).*

2

> Do not think that a man's courage consists only in bravery and strength: the highest courage is to rise above anger and to love the one who has wronged you.

> *Persian wisdom (from d'Herbelot).*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 296 -->

3

> *Condemn your own deeds. And when you have condemned them, do not despair.*

> *Epictetus.*

4

> What I say to you in the dark, speak in the light; and what you hear in the ear, preach on the housetops.

> And do not be afraid of those who kill the body but cannot kill the soul; but be afraid rather of him who can destroy both soul and body.

> *Matthew 10:27–28.*

5

> Cowardice lies in knowing what ought to be done and not doing it.

> *Confucius*

6

> No grief is so great as the fear of it.

> *Zschokke.*

7

> If someone has insulted me, that is his affair; such is his inclination, such is his disposition; I have my own disposition—such as, in my opinion, is proper to man, and in my actions I will remain true to my own disposition.

> *Marcus Aurelius.*

8

> “Do not eat away your heart, — say the sages, — do not mourn the past, the buried past.” Do what ought to be done, and, like a star, do not rest and do not hurry.

> *Hadji Abdul Gezdi.*

If you are afraid of anything, then know that the cause of your fear is not outside you but in you.
