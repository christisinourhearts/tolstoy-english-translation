8 June

Without truthfulness there can be no good; without kindness truth cannot be communicated.

1

*Good and truth condition each other.*

2

> Those who know the truth are equal to those who love it; and those who love it are not equal to those who do it with love.

> *Confucius.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 386 -->

3

> Why do you call me: Lord! Lord!—and do not do what I say?

> Everyone who comes to me and hears my words and does them, I will tell you whom he is like.

> He is like a man building a house, who dug and went deep and laid the foundation on rock; and so, when a flood came and the water pressed against that house, it could not shake it, because it was founded on rock.

> But he who hears and does not do is like a man who built a house on the ground without a foundation, which, when the water pressed against it, at once collapsed; and the ruin of this house was great.

> *Luke 6:46–49.*

4

> Answer hatred with kindness. Consider a difficulty while it is still easy. Deal with a great thing while it is still small. The most difficult undertakings in the world arise when they are easy. The greatest undertakings arise when they are still small.

> *Laozi.*

5

> There are two paths leading to virtue: to be just and not to do evil to living beings.

> *The Book of Manu.*

6

> Truth never furiously takes up arms against evil: its clarity, obviousness, and inner power strike evil more powerfully than anything.

> *Thoreau.*

7

> All malice comes from powerlessness.

> *Rousseau.*

There is nothing worse than a pretense of kindness. A pretense of kindness repels more than open malice.
