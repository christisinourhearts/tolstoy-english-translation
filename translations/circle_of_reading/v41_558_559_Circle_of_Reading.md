<!-- vol. 41, p. 558 -->

9 August

The greater part of the evil done by people is done not from evil will but from a widespread false thought accepted on faith.

1

> Material results are only the late manifestation of invisible forces. The cannonball has long since flown when the sound of the shot reaches us. Decisive events take place in thought.

> *Amiel.*

2

> Out of the heart come evil thoughts, murders, adulteries, fornications, thefts, false testimonies, blasphemies.

> *Matthew 15:19.*

3

> Actions are not so good or so vicious as our desires.

> *Vauvenargues.*

4

Far worse than a bad deed are the thoughts from which all bad deeds come. A bad deed one may not repeat and may repent of; but bad thoughts give birth to bad <!-- vol. 41, p. 559 --> deeds. A bad deed wears smooth the road to bad deeds; but bad thoughts irresistibly draw one along this road.

5

> Far away rush the bodiless thoughts; quietly they steal along, deeply hidden; whoever subdues them to himself, whoever bridles them, will be freed from their temptation.

> *Buddhist wisdom. Dhammapada.*

6

*Think well, and thoughts will ripen into good deeds.*

7

Everything lies in thoughts. Thought is the beginning of everything. And thoughts can be governed. And therefore the chief work of perfecting is this: to work on one's thoughts.

When misfortunes befall you, look for their cause not so much in your deeds as in the thoughts that moved you to do them. In just the same way, when outward events taking place grieve or outrage you, look for their causes not in the preceding deeds of people but in the preceding thoughts that called forth the deeds.
