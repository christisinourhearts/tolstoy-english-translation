<!-- vol. 42, p. 269 -->

20 November

If the good life of people calls forth not love but persecution among people living an evil life, this not only does not shake confidence in the rightness of such a life, but, on the contrary, confirms it by the most unmistakable sign.

1

> Beware of people, then: for they will hand you over to courts, and in their synagogues they will beat you, and they will lead you before rulers and kings for my sake, for a testimony before them and the Gentiles. And when they hand you over, do not worry how and what to say: for in that hour you will be given what to say; for it is not you who will speak, but the spirit of your father will speak in you.

> *Matthew 10:17–20.*

2

> Even death has no power to destroy the triumph of one who fights with all his strength for a right cause. Fight, then, unbending, faithful heart, go forward, regardless of fortune and misfortune, <!-- vol. 42, p. 270 --> and be assured that the right cause for which you fight will conquer. Only all that is unjust will perish; but the right cause cannot be conquered, because it is accomplished not by your will but by the eternal laws of God.

> *Carlyle.*

3

> An obstacle on the path of good, overcome by an effort of the spirit, gives me new strength; what threatened to be a barrier to the attainment of good itself becomes good, and a bright path suddenly opens where no way out was seen.

> *Marcus Aurelius.*

4

“He who has endured to the end will be saved.”

How often a person despairs and stops, or even turns back, just when only a small effort was needed to reach the goal.

5

Persecutions, if only they are borne with Christian meekness, produce an effect opposite to that which the persecutors strive for. People want to hide a fire that has appeared in the forest and, to put it out, press it to the ground with everything they have at hand: leaves, grass, brushwood, firewood, and the fire flares up more and more, and its light spreads farther and farther.

6

Persecutions, and therefore sufferings, are a necessary condition of fulfilling the Christian law. The degree of external suffering shows the degree of our following of Christ, as friction shows the degree of tension of any work.

7

Persecutions are precious in that they break down all artificial props and bring out into the open the real faith by which a person lives.

<!-- vol. 42, p. 271 -->

8

In persecutions what is dangerous is not the sufferings, but the temptation of pity for oneself and of the unkind attitude toward the persecutors that arises from it.

*Do not seek the love of people and do not be troubled by their lack of love. Often people love for what is bad and do not love for what is good. Try to please not people but God.*
