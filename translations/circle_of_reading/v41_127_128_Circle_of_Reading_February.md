## THE ARCHANGEL GABRIEL

Once the archangel Gabriel heard from paradise the voice of God—God had blessed some man. The angel said: “Surely this is an important servant of the Most High, surely some holy <!-- vol. 41, p. 128 --> hermit, a sage.” The angel came down to earth to find this man, but could not find him either in heaven or on earth. Then he turned to God and said: “O Lord! Show me the way to this object of your love.” God answered: “Go to the village, and there in a small temple you will see a fire.” The angel came down to the temple and there saw that a man was praying before an idol. The angel returned to God and said: “O Lord, can it be that you look with love on an idolater.” God said: “I do not look at the fact that he understands me wrongly. No one among people can understand me exactly as I am. And the very greatest sage among people is just as far from a true understanding of what I am as this man. I look not at the mind but at the heart. And the heart of this man seeks me and is therefore close to me.

*Persian (Attar).*
