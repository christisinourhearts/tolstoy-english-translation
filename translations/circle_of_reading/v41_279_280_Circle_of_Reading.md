<!-- vol. 41, p. 279 -->

23 April

True good is always simple.

Simplicity is so attractive, so advantageous, that it is surprising how few people are simple.

1

> *Do not seek happiness beyond the sea. Thanks be to the Most High that he made the needful not hard, and the hard—not needful.*

> *Grigory Skovoroda.*

2

> All truly good things are cheap, all harmful ones are expensive.

> *Thoreau.*

3

> Every acquisition of so-called progress is bound up with some deprivation: society will be enriched, for example, by a new invention, but meanwhile will lose some quality innate in each of us. The educated man has carriages, but hardly has the use of his own legs. He has a fine Geneva watch, but he cannot tell the hour by the sun. He will buy an astronomical calendar and, relying on finding everything needful in it, will not be able to distinguish a single star in the sky, will not notice either the autumn or the spring equinox.

> A reasonable man, casting off all that is superfluous, in the end returns to what is necessary for him.

> *Emerson.*

4

> Almost all our expenditures are made in order to be like others.

> *Emerson.*

5

Serve the common cause—do the work of love—by word, by restraint, by effort: here you did not say a single bad little word, did not do what would have been worse, here you overcame timidity and false shame and did and said what was needed, what was good, what was loving—<!-- vol. 41, p. 280 -->all tiny, unnoticed acts and words, and from these very mustard seeds grows the tree of love, covering the whole world with its branches.

6

There is no need to seek heroic deeds. If only you do what is required of you now, in the position in which you find yourself, in the best way, in a Christian way, with all your might, then life will be full, and there will be no need to seek heroic deeds.

7

All the greatest deeds are done in conditions of inconspicuousness, modesty, simplicity: one cannot plow, or build, or tend cattle, or even think amid thunder and glitter. Great, true deeds are always simple, modest.

Least simple of all are people who wish to seem simple. Deliberate simplicity is the greatest and most unpleasant artificiality.
