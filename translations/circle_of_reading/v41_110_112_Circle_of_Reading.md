18 February

The personality of every person is a veil hiding the divinity that lives in him. The more a person renounces his personality, the more this divinity shows itself in him.

1

> One must love only God and hate only oneself.

> *Pascal.*

2

> The father loves me because I give up my life, in order to take it again.

> No one takes it from me, but I give it up myself. I have power to give it up, and power I have to take it again. This commandment I received from my father.

> *John 10:17–18.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 111 -->

3

The more a person cares for himself, is occupied with himself, the more he guards his life, the weaker he becomes and the more he is bound. And, on the contrary: the less a person cares for himself, the less he is occupied with himself and guards himself, the stronger he is and the freer.

4

*Everything will be easy and good if it is done with renunciation of oneself, of one's own will.*

5

> The words of the teaching of truth are firm only in him who denies the personality in himself.

> *The Talmud.*

6

> Whoever wants to save his soul (life) will lose it; and whoever loses his soul (life) for my sake and the gospel's will save it.

> *Mark 8:35.*

7

> Whoever does not see himself in what is transient in him, in his name, and in his bodily being knows the truth of life.

> *Buddhist wisdom. Dhammapada.*

8

> A person has no data for an appraisal, and still less a right to judge the results of a life of complete, unconditional selflessness, until he has the courage to try such a life himself, at least for a time; but I think that no reasonable person will wish, and no honest person will dare, to deny the beneficial influence that even those chance moments when he forgot himself and renounced his personality have had on his soul and body.

> *John Ruskin.*

You have only to remember yourself in the middle of speaking—and you lose the thread of your thought. Only when we completely forget ourselves, go out of ourselves, only then can we fruitfully commune with others, serve them, and influence them.
