<!-- vol. 42, p. 183 -->

29 October

For every truth that enters people's consciousness and replaces a former error, there is a period when the error is plain and the truth that is to replace it is evident, but the error continues by inertia to hold people in its power. In such periods what is needed is not so much explanations of the truth as the example of a life in accord with it.

1

> I am always amazed at how kings can so easily believe that everything is in them, and the people—believe of themselves that they are nothing.

> *Montaigne.*

2

There is nothing more contagious than example. It makes us commit acts that we would never commit without the influence of example. And therefore association with depraved, sensual, and cruel people ruins the soul. And the reverse.

<!-- vol. 42, p. 184 -->

3

*He who does not think for himself is under the suggestion of another, who thinks for him. To hand over one's thought as someone's property is a more degrading slavery than to hand over one's body as someone's property.*

4

If you feel a desire to imitate what the people around you are doing, be sure to stop and think whether it is reasonable or not to follow the common example. Great crimes and calamities, personal and social, come only from thoughtlessly following suggestion.

5

> He who fears what is not fearful and does not tremble before what is truly fearful, following false opinion, enters on the evil path of perdition.

> *Buddhist wisdom. Dhammapada.*

6

> Our chief and most difficult duty as members of society consists in being able to use the goods of society but not submit to its yoke; to be ready to receive the thoughts and convictions of others, but hold firmly to the sacred right of our own judgment; to receive impulses from others, but act according to the demands of our own soul; to act with others, but follow our own conscience; to be able to unite respect for the opinion of others and self-determination.

> *Channing.*

Evil suggestion is destroyed only by good suggestion. And the most powerful means of good suggestion is a good life.
