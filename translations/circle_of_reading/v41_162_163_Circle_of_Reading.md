<!-- vol. 41, p. 162 -->

11 March

Just as food is a necessary condition of the life of the individual person, so marriage is a necessary condition of the life of humanity; and just as the abuse of food gives rise to evil for the individual person, so the abuses of marriage give rise to the greatest harm both for the individual person and for humanity.

1

*Cohabitation, the consequence of which may be childbearing, is true, real marriage; all rites, declarations, conditions, however, do not constitute marriage and are used for the most part in order to recognize all preceding cohabitations as not marriage.*

2

> You can neglect your duty to your husband or wife, you can rid yourself of the sorrow that these duties give you, you can go away. But what will you find?

> The same sorrow, but without the consciousness of a duty fulfilled.

> *George Eliot.*

3

Marriage, as an agreement, is the obligation of two people of different sexes to have children only by each other. Violation of this agreement is deceit, betrayal, and crime.

4

> It is a great thing when two souls feel that they are united forever in order to support each other in every labor, in every grief, to help each other in every suffering, and to be united with each other in those silent, inexpressible moments of the last farewell.

> *George Eliot.*

<!-- vol. 41, p. 163 -->

5

What great good can two loving spouses attain if they set perfecting as their goal and help each other in it: by reminder, by advice, by example?

6

> And the Pharisees came to Christ and, tempting him, said to him: is it permitted for a man to divorce his wife for any reason?

> He said to them in answer: have you not read that he who created in the beginning created them man and woman? And he said: therefore a man will leave father and mother and cleave to his wife, and the two will be one flesh (Genesis 1:27; 2:24). So that they are no longer two, but one flesh. And so, what God has joined, let man not separate.

> *Matthew 19:3, 4, 5, 6.*

7

> Everyone who divorces his wife and marries another commits adultery, and everyone who marries a woman divorced from her husband commits adultery.

> *Luke 16:18.*

The union of a man and a woman for the continuation of the human race is a matter so great and important both for each individual person and for all humanity that it cannot be done anyhow, and as anyone takes it into his head, and as anyone finds pleasant, but it must be done as the wise and holy people who lived before us decided about it and thought it through.
