<!-- vol. 42, p. 271 -->

21 November

There is no special heroic deed that we could accomplish in this life. Our whole life must be this heroic deed.

1

> On each awakening ask yourself: what good might I do today? and think: for the sun, as it sets, will carry away with it a particle of the life allotted to me.

> *Indian saying.*

2

> *A person's virtue is measured not by his extraordinary efforts but by his everyday conduct.*

> *Pascal.*

3

The advantage of serving God over serving people is that before people you involuntarily want to show yourself in the best light and are grieved when you are shown in a bad one; before God there is none of this. He knows you as you are, and before him no one can slander you, so that you need not try to seem, but need only really be better.

4

> Let every dawn be for you as it were the beginning of life, and every sunset as it were its end, and let each <!-- vol. 42, p. 272 --> of these short lives leave behind it the trace of a loving deed done for others and of a good effort over oneself.

> *John Ruskin.*

5

I am an instrument with which God works. My true good is in taking part in his work. And I can take part in his work only by keeping in order, clean, sharp, and true the instrument that has been given me: my soul.

6

Every matter, even the most complex and tangled, becomes simple and clear if one manages to place it, independently of people, before the judgment of God alone.

7

The meaning of life is to do as well as possible that work which is required of us by the power that sent us into the world. And you can always know whether you are doing this work or not: conscience is the indicator of this. One need only listen to it and try to make it more and more sensitive.

With respect to the service of God, all our acts—those that are considered important and those that are considered trifling—are equally significant or equally insignificant. We do not know what use will be made of them, but we know what we must do.
