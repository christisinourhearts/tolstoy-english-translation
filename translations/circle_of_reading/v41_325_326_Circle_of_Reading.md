<!-- vol. 41, p. 325 -->

14 May

The consciousness of the divinity of the soul gives fearlessness before all the calamities of life.

1

> We know that the soul is divine. I cannot say whether those wonderful qualities which live in me now will gather again in a bodily shell; nor can I say whether, before their appearance here, they had the same natural history as that body which I see before me; but one thing I know surely, that these qualities did not begin to exist, cannot be sick with the sickness of my body, cannot be buried in any coffin, but that they were before the world was. And this gives me faith, courage, and hope.

> *The soul knows everything. No message can surprise it. Nothing can be greater than it.* Let whoever wishes be afraid. The soul lives in its native kingdom; it is more spacious than space and older than time.

> *Emerson*

2

> *God lives in all people, but not all people live in God. This is the cause of people's sufferings.*

> *As a lamp cannot burn without fire, so a person cannot live without God.*

> *Brahmanic wisdom. Ramakrishna.*

3

> You are afraid that you will be despised for your meekness, but just people cannot despise you for it, and other people are no concern of yours—pay no attention to their <!-- vol. 41, p. 326 --> judgments. Surely a skilled carpenter will not be grieved that a man who understands nothing of carpentry does not approve of his good work.

> Do not think that evil people can harm you. Can anyone harm your soul? So why, then, are you troubled?

> I laugh to myself at those who think they can harm me: they know neither who I am nor wherein I place good and evil; they do not know that they cannot even touch what is truly mine and by which alone I live.

> *Epictetus.*

4

> *All the things of the world belong to me; creation and destruction take place by my will; the world is only a shell, I am its kernel, how then am I to fear that dust will return to dust? I am not dust. Submit to God and live in peace.*

> *Persian wisdom.*

5

Reason asks how and why? Love says: I am love. And, without answering the question, fully satisfies the one who asks.

*Fear no one and nothing. What is dearest in you cannot suffer from anyone or anything.*
