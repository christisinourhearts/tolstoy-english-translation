## THE TEACHING OF THE LORD, IMPARTED TO THE NATIONS BY THE 12 APOSTLES

There are two paths: the path of life and the path of death. And great is the difference between these two paths. The path of life is this:

First, love God, who created you.

Second, your neighbor as yourself, and therefore do not do to another anything that you do not want done to you.

The teaching of these two words is this:

1

The first commandment of the teaching: love God, who created you.

Bless those who curse you; pray for your enemies, for those who attack you, and fast for those who wrong you, because—it is no good thing to love only those who love you. The pagans do the same. They love their own and hate their enemies, and therefore they have enemies. But you, love those who hate you, and then you will have no enemies.

Beware of bodily and worldly impulses.

If anyone strikes you on the right cheek, turn the other to him as well, and you will be perfect. If anyone compels you to go one verst with him, go two with him. If anyone takes your caftan, give him your shirt too. If anyone has taken what is yours from you, do not reclaim it, because this must not be done. And to everyone who asks of you, give, and do not demand it back, because the father wants each one to have as his own that which he has given to all people. Blessed is he who gives according to the commandment: he is in the right; but woe to him who takes, because only he who takes out of need is in the right; but he who takes without need must give an account of why and for what he took. Whoever is caught in the nets of Mammon will be tormented for what he has done, and will not get free of them until he gives up the last. It is of this that it is said: let your mercy come out as sweat from your hands while you do not yet even know to whom you will give.

<!-- vol. 42, p. 322 -->

2

The second commandment of the teaching: love your neighbor as yourself, that is, do not do to another what you do not want done to you.

Do not kill, do not commit adultery, do not defile children, do not be debauched, do not steal, do not practice witchcraft, do not poison, do not kill an infant in its mother's womb, and do not kill one that is born, do not desire to have what your neighbor has, do not swear, do not bear false witness, do not use foul language, do not think foul thoughts, do not be double-minded or double-tongued—double-tonguedness is a net of death. Let your word be neither false nor empty, but always full of deeds; be neither greedy, nor rapacious, nor a hypocrite, nor sullen, nor proud. Do not bear malice toward your neighbor, do not hate any man, but rebuke some, pray for others, and love others more than your own soul.

3

My child! Avoid all evil and everything that resembles it. Do not fall into anger, anger leads to murder; do not fall into provocation, into disputes, into heat—from all this come murders. My child! Avoid lust, lust leads to debauchery; do not use foul language and do not look around at what you have no need to see; this leads to adulteries. My child! Do not tell fortunes, because this leads to idolatry; do not practice magic, do not practice the black arts, do not cast spells, and do not be present at such doings, because this is idolatry. My child! do not be a deceiver, because deceit leads to theft; do not be covetous and vain—this too brings one to theft. My child! do not be discontented, discontent leads to abuse; do not be self-satisfied and judging, because this too leads to abuse; be meek, because the meek will inherit the earth; be patient, merciful, and without malice, and humble, and kind, and with fear on every occasion remember these, which you have heard. Do not exalt yourself and do not admit self-assurance into your heart. Let your heart not incline to the high and the strong, but let it cling to the righteous and the humble. And whatever happens to you, accept as good, knowing that without God nothing happens.

<!-- vol. 42, p. 323 -->

4

My child! Both day and night remember him who teaches you the word of God, and honor him as the Lord, because the Lord is where you learned of him from. Always seek out holy people and keep company with them, so as to find rest for your soul in their words. Do not desire division among people, but reconcile those who are at enmity. Judge between them justly and, without regard to persons, convict them of their sins. Do not be two-faced and do not say: it can be this way, and it can be that way. Do not stretch out your hand when it comes to taking, and do not clench it when it comes to giving. What you have earned with your hands, give as a ransom for your sins. Do not hesitate to give, and when you have given, do not regret it, because you will learn what the best reward for your good is. Do not turn away from the needy, but let all that you have be in common with your brother, and call nothing your own property, because if what is immortal is all in common among you, then all the more must everything perishable be common among you. Do not cease to guide your son or your daughter, but from youth teach them the fear of God. Do not give orders to a slave or a maidservant: they believe in the same God as you. Or else from distress they may cease to fear the God who is over you both; because commanding falls not according to persons, but to him whom the spirit has appointed.

Hate all hypocrisy and everything that is not pleasing to God. Do not forsake the commandments of the Lord, but keep those you have received, adding nothing and casting nothing away. Among the believers repent of your sins, and do not think of praying while you have evil in your heart.

Such is the path of life.

5

But the path of death is this: first of all it is calamitous and full of abominations. Murders, adultery, lusts, debauchery, theft, idolatry, witchcraft, poisoning, robberies, deceptions, hypocrisy, duplicity, cunning, pride, malice, self-assurance, greed, foul language, envy, insolence, conceit, vanity, persecutors of the good, haters of the truth, lovers of lies, those who do not recognize the reward for righteousness, who do not cling to the good and do not know right judgment, those who care and busy themselves not about the good but about the evil, who do not know meekness and patience, lovers of trifles, seekers <!-- vol. 42, p. 324 --> of worldly rewards, who do not pity the beggar, who do not labor for the one who is burdened, who do not know him who created them, murderers and seducers of children, destroyers of the image of God, who turn away from the needy and wear out with labor the one already worn out, comforters of the rich and lawless judges of the poor—sinners all around and in everything. Beware, my children, of such people!

See that no one turns you aside from this path of the teaching.
