<!-- vol. 41, p. 507 -->

17 July

The foundation of the structure of ancient society is violence; the foundation of the structure of the society proper to our time is reasonable agreement and the rejection of violence.

1

> You have heard that it was said: an eye for an eye and a tooth for a tooth. But I say to you: do not resist evil. But whoever strikes you on your right cheek, turn the other to him as well.

> *Matthew 5:38–39.*

2

> He who is skilled in making use of people is humble. This is called the virtue of non-resistance. This is called accord with Heaven.

> *Laozi.*

3

Those who think that people cannot be guided otherwise than by violence, disregarding their reason, do with people the same thing that is done with horses, blinding them so that they will walk round the circle more meekly.

4

So-called educated people—those who ought to give an example of the attitude toward violence that is proper to a reasonable being—learned, liberal, even revolutionary people reason, condemn, preach freedom, the dignity of man. But all this only until someone whistles to him to go into the collar. And there is an end to all the reasonings, and all the liberalism, and the talk of freedom; and they dress him in a motley livery, put a rifle or a saber in his hands, and order him to run, and jump, and stand, and turn about, and put on his cap, and bow, and shout “hurrah,” and, above all, be ready at command to kill his own father; and he—a liberal, a learned man, a revolutionary, a preacher of freedom—jumps, and bows to whomever he is told, and shouts “hurrah,” and is ready to kill with a rifle whomever he is ordered to.

<!-- vol. 41, p. 508 --> So that those very people—the educated, for whom it would be most natural of all to strive to bring life into agreement with consciousness—these people do not believe in what they preach, in what they teach.

5

*What then is people's reason for, if they can be acted upon only by violence?*

In all cases where violence is used, apply reasonable persuasion, and you will seldom lose in the worldly sense, while in spiritual consciousness you will experience complete satisfaction.
