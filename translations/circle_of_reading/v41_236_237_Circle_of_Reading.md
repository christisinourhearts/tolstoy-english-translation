<!-- vol. 41, p. 236 -->

9 April

Love of good and faith in immortality are inseparable.

1

> No one can say that he *knows* that there is a future life. Our conviction of this is founded not on logical but on moral certainty, and therefore I cannot say that it is beyond doubt that God and my immortality exist, but I must say that I am morally convinced that God exists and that my “I” is immortal. This means that faith in God and in another world is so bound up with my nature that this faith cannot be separated from me.

> *Kant.*

2

> The more spiritual our life becomes, the more we believe in immortality. As our nature moves away from animal coarseness, its doubts too are destroyed. The veil is lifted from the future, the darkness is dispersed, and here already we feel our immortality.

> *After Martineau.*

3

> Everything that I have seen and know teaches me to trust what I have not seen and do not know. What providence is preparing for us in the future, whatever it may be, must be something majestic, good, of the same kind as those works of its that we know here. Our future must correspond to the very highest that we can only imagine while living in this life.

> *Emerson.*

4

There is nothing terrible in death; death seems terrible only to the extent that in this life we depart from the eternal law.

5

> We in the world are like a child who has come into a room where a learned man is speaking about his science. The child did not hear the beginning and goes away before the end. He has heard something, but has not <!-- vol. 41, p. 237 --> understood what he heard. The great speech of God was begun many centuries before we began our learning, and it will go on even when we are dust. We have heard only part of it and have not understood the greater part of what we heard. But all the same, though little and dimly, we have understood something great and solemn.

> *David Thomas.*

6

> Whoever truly loves God will not strive to have God love him. It is enough for him that he himself loves God.

> *Spinoza.*

*Whoever loves good (God) with his whole being cannot doubt his immortality.*
