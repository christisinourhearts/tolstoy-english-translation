*11. To T. A. Yergolskaya and Countess E. A. Tolstaya. December? 1848. Moscow.*

Thank you for your letters and for the package, dear aunts. [^1] I see that you have a very false idea of the state of my health; it is no doubt the word consultation that frightened you so much. But it means nothing more than this: since it would have cost me too much to be treated by Over, [^2] I engaged him only for a consultation, in which he gave advice to Deutsch, [^3] who is my doctor.

Even Over said that what I have is not a hernia but a varicocele; [^4] the name naturally makes no difference, but the latter is less dangerous and easier to cure. In general I feel much better than at Yasnoye.

<!-- vol. 59, p. 27 --> Goodbye, I kiss your hands and in the P.S. do the same thing again.

This seal is charming; thank you for it. Send me the large one too. Do not be surprised, my aunts, that I need so much money. I do not gamble, but I paid 250 silver rubles to Basile 5 and had a fur coat and indispensable clothes made for 400 silver rubles; after that I must buy myself a watch. All these are temporary expenses.

Thank you for the letters and the package, dear aunties. You are mistaken about my health; the word consultation probably frightened you. The point is that treatment by Over [^5] would have cost me too much, so I invited him together with Deutsch, [^6] who is actually treating me and to whom he passed on his instructions.

Over too found that it is not a hernia but *varicosella*. [^7] Of course the word is not the point, but this illness is less dangerous and easier to cure. In general I feel better than at *Yasnoye*.—

Goodbye, I kiss your hands and in the postscript repeat the same thing again.

Your seal is charming; thank you for it. Send me the large one too. Do not be surprised, aunties, that I need so much money. I do not gamble, but I paid Vasenka 250 silver rubles and ordered a fur coat and necessary clothes for 400 silver rubles. Then I need to buy myself a watch. But all these expenses are temporary.
