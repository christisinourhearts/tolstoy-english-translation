\* 30. To T. A. Yergolskaya. 1850. December 4. Tula?

Dear aunt!

I was obliged to spend the night in Tula because of my leave, which is going to be brought to me,[^1]—then nothing will prevent me from leaving;[^2] I think the *starosta* will be able to tell you at what hour he saw me leave. — I have just received a letter from the Perfilievs[^3] addressed to you, which I opened, hoping to find something there for me, and indeed Basile writes to me that he is very sorry that I owe him and asks me to wait for better circumstances before paying him. — I did not read what Pauline writes to you.[^4] — I spent yesterday evening at the Arsenievs', where I saw all the Misses Chulkov,[^5] who are not leaving for Kazan until January 8. — I took from Kapylov a paper payable in Moscow by which I shall receive another 600 rubles in assignats.

Goodbye, I kiss your hands.

December 4, 1850.

<!-- vol. 59, p. 63 --> Dear auntie!

I was obliged to spend the night in Tula because of the leave that I am about to receive[^6]—then nothing will delay my departure;[^7] I think the *starosta* will tell you at what hour I left. — I have just received a letter addressed to you from the Perfilievs;[^8] I opened it, hoping to find something there for me; and indeed Vasenka writes to me that he regrets that I owe him and asks me to postpone payment until circumstances are more favorable for me. — I did not read what Polina writes to you.[^9] — Yesterday I spent the evening at the Arsenievs', where I saw all the young ladies Chulkov,[^10] who are not going to Kazan until January 8. — I took from *Kopylov* a paper by which I shall receive another 600 rubles in assignats in Moscow. Goodbye, I kiss your hands.

December 4, 1850.

## Notes

Some of them were class ladies at the Kazan Rodionov Institute for Women. See Tolstoy's letter to Countess Maria Nikolaevna Tolstaya, no. 42.
