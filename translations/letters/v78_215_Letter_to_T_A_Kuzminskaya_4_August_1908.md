*215. To T. A. Kuzminskaya. 1908. August 4. Yasnaya Polyana.*

4 Aug. 1908.

Dear Tanya,

I cannot say exactly which regiment Vronsky was in. Only, probably, not a Life Guards Hussar. I wish your dear Vasya[^1] were interested in learning from me things more necessary for life.[^2]

I kiss you, Sasha,[^3] and Vasya as a brother.

L. Tolstoy.

### Editorial notes

1. Printed from copying book no. 8, fol. 273. The date was entered in the hand of N. N. Gusev.

2. Reply to a letter from T. A. Kuzminskaya of 1 August 1908. At the request of her son, V. A. Kuzminsky, Kuzminskaya asked which regiment Vronsky belonged to (*Anna Karenina*).

## Notes

[^1]: Vasily Alexandrovich Kuzminsky (b. 1882), at that time a lieutenant in the Guards Equipage.
[^2]: That same day, 4 August, Tolstoy said at dinner in connection with this letter: “What questions interest the Petersburg youth!” (YaZ, entry for 4 August 1908).
[^3]: Alexander Mikhailovich Kuzminsky.
