\* 38. To T. A. Yergolskaya. 1851. End of February. Moscow.

Dear aunt!

You have more or less guessed that I was dissatisfied with myself and that this was the cause of my silence. — I amused myself too much and went about in society all this time, with the result that I neglected my correspondence; as for gambling, <!-- vol. 59, p. 89 --> it is true that I lost, but very little. I am writing to you only to reassure you about me and ask you to forgive my silence. Nikolai[^1] is waiting for me to go out with him on errands and purchases. Goodbye, or rather, until we meet; tomorrow I shall write you at greater length. You asked me what came of my meeting with Kireevsky and Yazykov;[^2] nothing came of it. We talked about their health and mine, the theater, and flowers; but, as you can well imagine, not a word about your letter. —

I kiss your hands and embrace Maria[^3] very tenderly. —

On the envelope:

To Her High Nobility Tatyana Alexandrovna Yergolskaya. Tula Province, town of Chern.

Dear auntie!

You almost guessed that the reason for my silence is that I am dissatisfied with myself. I amused myself too much, went about too much in society, and neglected my correspondence; as for gambling, it is true that I lost, but very little. I am writing you only to reassure you about me and to ask you to forgive me for my long silence. Nikolenka[^4] is waiting for me so that I can go with him on errands and purchases. Goodbye, or rather, until we meet; tomorrow I shall write you a longer letter. You ask what consequences came from my meeting with Kireevsky and *Yazykov*[^5]—none. The conversation was about their health, mine, the theater, and flowers. Of course, not a word about your letter. —

I kiss your hands and kiss Mashenka[^6] tenderly.

### Editorial notes

1. Printed from the autograph held in the ATB. Published here for the first time. The letter is dated on the basis of T. A. Yergolskaya's notation on the envelope: “1 March 1851,” and is a reply to her letter of January 27, 1851, quoted in our note to letter no. 37.
