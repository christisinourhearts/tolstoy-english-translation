*240. To G. A. Novichkov. 1907. September 26. Yasnaya Polyana.*

Gavrilo Alexandrovich,

I wrote to my good acquaintance Count Olsufyev, asking him to intercede in your case with the governor. He is an influential man, and I hope that he will do what is necessary and possible.

<!-- vol. 77, p. 208 --> With all my heart I sympathize with you and advise you not to lose heart. I hope that even without my advice you are bearing your misfortune courageously and without complaint, in a Christian way.

Leo Tolstoy.

26 Sept. 1907.

### Editorial notes

1. Printed from copying book no. 7, fol. 450; the end of the letter was poorly reproduced and was restored in the hand of Yu. I. Igumnova.

2. Reply to a letter from G. A. Novichkov of 19 September 1907, reporting his imprisonment for three months, supposedly “for an illegal gathering on May 1.”

3. It is known from D. A. Olsufyev's reply letter to Tolstoy of 7 October that Governor S. S. Tatishchev granted Tolstoy's request and released G. A. Novichkov.
