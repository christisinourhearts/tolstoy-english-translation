*164. To I. I. Petrov. 1887. September—November.*

Ivan Ivanovich!

Please pass this note to Ivan Dmitrievich, together with my request that he fulfill the wish of these gentlemen. I know them as people who are seriously engaged in this work and have already done much for it. —

I shake your hand in friendship

Leo Tolstoy.

### Editorial notes

1. Dated on the basis of a notation on the letter in an unknown hand: “Autumn 1887.”

2. The autograph of the letter is a postscript to a letter (undated) to Tolstoy from a group of unknown persons, reporting that in Moscow there existed <!-- vol. 64, p. 127 --> several circles that had set themselves the aim of “supplying the people with inexpensive and good reading material.” For this purpose, by common agreement, the circles decided to organize their own library. But because of limited means, the initiators of this undertaking were forced to appeal to book publishers with a request that they donate to their collection one copy of every publication. The correspondents asked Tolstoy to “support their request with his authority.”

3. No information is available about the fate of these circles and their activities.
