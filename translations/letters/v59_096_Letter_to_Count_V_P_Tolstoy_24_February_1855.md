*96. To Count V. P. Tolstoy. 24 February 1855. Position on the Belbek River.*

Dear friend,

As I think you know from my letter to Nikolenka, I lost the 1,500 rubles that were sent to me; but what <!-- vol. 59, p. 301 --> is worse, I lost another 575 silver rubles on credit. [^1] I must have this money sent to me as quickly as possible, so be so kind as to arrange at Yasnaya Polyana for enough grain to be sold to make up what is lacking, and have the money sent to me in Sevastopol.—If the money has not yet been sent to the Guardianship Council, then I ask that these 575 rubles be sent to me before anything is sent to the Council.—

It is shameful and painful to write this. Please do not show the letter to everyone. I have stopped gambling. Thank God, I am alive and well in camp on the Belbek near Sevastopol.—

Kiss your wife and children.

Your true friend and brother,

Count L. Tolstoy.

1855, 24 February.

Belbek.

### Editorial notes

1. Printed from the autograph held in the ATB. Published here for the first time.

2. Count Valerian Petrovich Tolstoy (born 19 October 1813, died 6 January 1865), son of Count Pyotr Ivanovich (1785–1834) and Elizaveta Alexandrovna Yergolskaya (1790–1851). He served in the Prince of Orange Hussar Regiment and was adjutant of the Separate Siberian Corps. Before his marriage, on 3 November 1847, he retired with the rank of major. He was married to his third cousin Countess Maria Nikolayevna Tolstaya. For more on Count V. P. Tolstoy, see also the note on Countess M. N. Tolstaya, letter no. 42.

## Notes

[^1]: The diary contains the following entries about gambling losses. 2 February: “It was not enough for me to lose everything I had. I lost another 150 rubles to Meshchersky on my word, which I do not have.” 6–8 February: “Played cards again and lost another 200 silver rubles. I cannot give myself my word that I will stop; I want to win it back, and at the same time I may get terribly entangled.” 12 February: “Lost again, 75 rubles. God is still merciful to me in that there have been no unpleasant consequences, but what will happen next? My only hope is in Him!” 15–16 February: “Yesterday I lost another 20 silver rubles and I will play no more.”
