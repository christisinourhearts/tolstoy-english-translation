1. To the Rector of Kazan University, N. I. Lobachevsky. 29 May 1844. Kazan.

To His Excellency Mr Rector of the Imperial Kazan University, Actual State Councillor and Knight Nikolai Ivanovich Lobachevsky.

Petition.

Wishing to enter the number of Students in the Oriental Division 1 of the Philosophical Faculty of the Imperial Kazan University, 2 I ask Your Excellency to admit me to the examination. My documents: a Certificate of Birth from the Tula Ecclesiastical Consistory under No. 252, 3 and a Certificate of my Noble Origin 4 from the Tula Noble Deputies' Assembly under No. 267, I have the honor to submit herewith.

Count Lev Tolstoy.

A Certificate of Health 5 I will deliver in the near future 6.

L. Tolstoy. 30 May 1844.

### Editorial notes

1. Printed from the autograph held in the State Tolstoy Museum. First published by N. P. Zagoskin in the article “Count L. N. Tolstoy and His Student Years” — *Istorichesky Vestnik*, 1894, no. 1, pp. 94–95.

2. The petition bears the annotations: “Subm[itted] 29 May 1844” and “There is no medical certificate of health; however, the documents are sufficient.”

3. After the text of the petition, N. I. Lobachevsky wrote: “Admit Lev Tolstoy to examination before the 2nd committee, informing the petitioner, <!-- vol. 59, p. 4 --> so that he should provide a certificate of health. 29 May 1844. Rector Lobachevsky.”

4. After the death on 30 August 1841 of the guardian of Nikolai, Sergei, Dmitry, Lev, and Maria Count Tolstoy, Countess Alexandra Ilyinichna von der Osten-Sacken, the guardianship passed to another of their aunts, their father's sister Pelageya Ilyinichna Yushkova, wife of the Kazan landowner V. I. Yushkov, who moved the Tolstoy children to Kazan in the autumn of 1841. In that same year, 1841, Count Nikolai Nikolaevich Tolstoy transferred from Moscow University to the third year of Kazan University, which Sergei and Dmitry Tolstoy entered in the autumn of 1843. Lev Tolstoy spent 1842–1844 preparing for the entrance examinations for the Turko-Arabic section of the Philosophical Faculty. The entrance examinations took place from 29 May to 5 June 1844. Tolstoy received the following marks: 29 May — religious instruction — 4; 30 May — general and Russian history — 1; 30 May — statistics and geography — 1; 31 May — mathematics — 4; 1 June — Russian literature — 4; 1 June — logic — 4; 2 June — Latin — 5; 5 June — Arabic — 5; 5 June — Turkish-Tatar — 5. See N. P. Zagoskin, “Count L. N. Tolstoy and His Student Years” — *Istorichesky Vestnik*, 1894, no. 1, pp. 78–124.

5. Nikolai Ivanovich Lobachevsky (1793–1856), the famous mathematician and brilliant creator of the non-Euclidean system of geometry, was professor at Kazan University from 1816 to 1846, rector of Kazan University from 3 May 1827 to 14 August 1846, and administrator of the Kazan educational district from 18 April 1845 to August 1847. Kazan University owed much to Lobachevsky in the construction of university buildings, the equipment of its physics cabinets, the organization of its library, and the transformation of the university's printed periodical.
