*298. To F. A. Strakhov. 1905. January 28? Yasnaya Polyana.*

I am very grateful to Maria Vasilievna [^1] for reminding me about your notebook. [^2] I have now read it carefully and found much that is good. What I think is best I marked off in pencil. [^3] I am also putting scores in the table of contents.

L. Tolstoy.

### Editorial notes

1. Dated on the basis of its contents and a note on the autograph, in pencil by an unknown hand: “Feb. 1905.”

## Notes

[^1]: Maria Vasilievna Syaskova, an employee of the Posrednik publishing house. She arrived at Yasnaya Polyana on 24 January and left on 28 January 1905.
[^2]: It is possible that Tolstoy has in mind the notebook about which Strakhov wrote to him on 26 October 1903: “If you find the time, you will read... a short little article entitled ‘Self-Knowledge Is the Path to Union with God,’ placed by me in my bound notebook, which I left with you” [in May 1903, when Strakhov visited Yasnaya Polyana].
[^3]: On the autograph of the letter there is a note in Strakhov's hand: “L[ev] N[ikolaevich] conveyed through M. V. Syaskova that the passages he marked should be included in the *Circle of Reading*.”
