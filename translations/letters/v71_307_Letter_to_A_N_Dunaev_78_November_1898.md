*307. To A. N. Dunaev. 1898. November 7–8? Yasnaya Polyana.*

Dear Alexander Nikiforovich,

Please pass these two letters [^1] to Archer, and as soon as possible. I want him to take the letter to Konshin himself and get the money and tell Konshin about the matter. I keep hearing that your physical health is not good. How I would like to see you and say many things. I kiss you.

L. Tolstoy.

Give my love to Rusanov and ask him to forgive me for not writing. Tell him that I often, often think of him, of Antonina Alexeyevna, and of the whole dear, beloved family.

### Editorial notes

1. Dated on the basis of the mention of Tolstoy's letters to Archer and Konshin (see note 1).

## Notes

[^1]: The letters Tolstoy was forwarding—the letter to A. N. Konshin, in reply to his letter of 5 November, and the covering letter to Archer—are unknown. Konshin's letter of 5 November was received at Yasnaya Polyana, judging by the postmark, on 7 November.
