2. To the Rector of Kazan University, N. I. Lobachevsky. 3 August 1844. Kazan.

To His Excellency Mr Rector of the Imperial Kazan University, Distinguished Professor, Actual State Councillor Nikolai Ivanovich Lobachevsky. 1

From Lev Nikolayevich Count

Tolstoy

Petition.

In May of this year, together with the pupils of the First and Second Kazan Gymnasiums, I underwent examination with the aim of entering the number of students of Kazan University in the division of Arabic-Turkish literature. But since at that examination I did not demonstrate the requisite knowledge in history and statistics, I most humbly request Your Excellency to permit me now to be examined again in these subjects. 2

With this I have the honor to present the following documents:

1) a certificate of birth and baptism from the Tula Consistory, 3

2) copies 4 of the resolution of the Tula Assembly of Deputies of the Nobility. 3 August 1844. To this petition the above-named petitioner, Count Lev Nikolayev Tolstoy, has set his hand.

### Editorial notes

1. Printed from the autograph held in the archive of Kazan University. First published by N. P. Zagoskin in the article “Count L. N. Tolstoy and His Student Years” — *Istorichesky Vestnik*, 1894, no. 1, pp. 48–99.

2. On the petition Lobachevsky wrote: “Permit him to take the supplementary examination. 4 August 1844. Rector Lobachevsky.” After the text of the petition is written: “Resolved to admit Tolstoy to the University as a student supporting himself at his own expense, in the division of Arabic-Turkish literature, in the first year, of which the Division of Sciences and the inspector of students are to be notified; the original, bearing the signatures of the gentlemen present and countersigned by the secretary, is correct; for the section head, Andronikov. Executed 20 September. To the 1st division, no. 1189; to the Inspector, no. 1138.”
