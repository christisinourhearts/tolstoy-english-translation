## **UNPUBLISHED, UNFINISHED** **AND INCOMPLETE**

## VIII. * LIST OF CHAPTERS OF *YOUTH*.

Chapter I. What I Consider the Beginning of Youth—nothing to redo.

Ch. II. Spring—good as regards style; the description of the little garden could be redone.

Ch. III. Dreams—the ending is not entirely skillful.

Ch. IV. Our Family Circle—so-so. On the whole, nothing to redo.

Ch. V. Rules—nothing to redo.

Ch. VI. Confession—

Ch. VII. The Journey—good.

Ch. VIII. The Second Confession—excellent.

Ch. IX. How I Prepare for the Examination.

Ch. X. History Examination—decent, little content.

Ch. XI. Mathematics Examination—

Ch. XII. Latin Examination—not entirely skillful and somehow uninteresting.

Ch. XIII. I Am Grown Up—not bad, but indistinct; better to rewrite.

Ch. XIV. What Dubkov and Volodya Did—not coherent.—

Ch. XV. I Am Congratulated—bad in language.

Ch. XVI. The Quarrel—good; Volodya could be added.

Ch. XVII. Ilenka Grap—empty, but all right.

Ch. XVIII. The Valakhins—good.

Ch. XIX. The Kornakovs—one passage is bad.

Ch. XX. The Ivins—good.

Ch. XXI. Prince Ivan Ivanovich—fairly awkward, the way they look at me.

Ch. XXII. Kuntsevo—fairly empty.

Ch. XXIII. The Nekhlyudovs—good, except for the description of the ladies. Sofya Ivanovna should be made thin.

Ch. XXIV. Love—add the little cushions, the pastry; the ending is nonsense.

Ch. XXV. I Become Acquainted—good.

<!-- vol. 2, p. 340 --> Ch. XXVI. I Show Myself to the Best Advantage—~~all right~~ ~~very weak~~.

Ch. XXVII. Dmitry—good.

Ch. XXVIII. In the Country—the part with Katenka is bad.

Ch. XXIX. Relations Between Us and the Girls—reasoning, not artistic.

Ch. XXX. My Occupations—bad, all reasoning.

Ch. XXXI. *Comme il faut*—reasoning, but good.

Ch. XXXII. Youth—the beginning is weak, drawn out; the ending excellent.

Ch. XXXIII. The Neighbors—sluggish and weak in language.

Ch. XXXIV. Father's Marriage—decent.

Ch. XXXV. How We Receive This News—mannered.

Ch. XXXVI. University—weak.

Ch. XXXVII. Affairs of the Heart—not bad, except for the irrelevant beginning.

Ch. XXXVIII. Society—to my surprise, excellent.

Ch. XXXIX. The Revel—good.

Ch. L. Friendship with the Nekhlyudovs—good.

Ch. LI. Friendship with Nekhlyudov—good, but crudely frank.

Ch. LII. Stepmother—decent, but general.

Ch. LIII. New Comrades—awkwardly put together, but not bad.

Ch. LIV. Zukhin and Semyonov—decent, but better to redo, because the content is delightful.

Ch. LV. I Fail—not bad, but better to redo.
