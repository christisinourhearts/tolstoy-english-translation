## I. \* PROJECT FOR A JOURNAL. SOLDIER'S HERALD.

## AIM OF THE JOURNAL.

1. — The dissemination among soldiers of the rules of military virtues: devotion to the Throne and Fatherland and the sacred fulfillment of military duties. —

2. — The dissemination among officers and lower ranks of information about contemporary military events, ignorance of which gives rise among the troops to false and even harmful rumors; about feats of bravery and valiant actions by detachments and individuals in all theaters of the present war. —

3. — The dissemination among military men of all ranks and branches of service of knowledge concerning special subjects of the military art. —

4. — The dissemination of critical information concerning the merit of military writings, new inventions, and projects. —

5. — The provision of interesting, accessible, and useful reading to all ranks of the Army. —

6. — The improvement of the soldier's poetry, which constitutes his only literature, by placing in the Journal songs written in a pure and sonorous language, instilling in the soldier correct ideas about things and, more than others, filled with feelings of love for the Monarch and Fatherland.

## PROGRAM OF THE JOURNAL.

The Journal is divided into two parts: official and unofficial. —

The official part contains:

1. — Reports from the various theaters of war.

2. — Orders concerning rewards for merits that fall outside the ordinary class of distinctions. —

<!-- vol. 4, p. 282 --> 3. — Sentences of military courts for unlawful and shameful actions.[^1]

4. — And orders to which the Lord Commander of the Troops may wish to give special publicity. —

The unofficial part contains:

1. — Accounts from military ~~officer and soldier life and letters from eyewitnesses~~ from the various theaters of war, containing: feats of individual detachments and persons, biographies, obituaries, descriptions of the region, inhabitants, character and mode of action of the enemy, and so forth.[^2]

2. — Popular articles on artillery and engineering and instructions to soldiers on the proper handling of horses and weapons and their preservation.[^3]

3. — Critical review. A list of useful works and descriptions of projects and innovations in the military art.[^4]

4. — Soldiers' songs.[^5]

5. — Religious instruction for soldiers.

6. — Official and private announcements concerning the needs of the troops.[^6]

In general, the editors will endeavor to have contributors and permanent correspondents in all places where the Russian army is stationed and even in the fleet. —

It is proposed that the Journal be published at the Headquarters of the troops of the 3rd, 4th and 5th Infantry Corps, weekly, one printed sheet at a time in quarto format.

(As resources increase, the publishers hope and will endeavor to bring the Journal from a weekly publication to a daily one):

The editors will maintain permanent offices in both capitals and in certain provincial cities. —

The editors will ask the Lord Commander of the Troops for permission to act, before publication begins, to obtain contributors and materials for the Journal and, if the project for the Journal is approved, for free distribution of its issues among the various units of the troops.[^7]
