## **WORKS** **OF 1852—1856**

## NOTES OF A BILLIARD MARKER.

## VARIANTS OF THE TEXT IN *SOVREMENNIK*, 1855, No. 1.

\*Page 101, lines 15—16. *Instead of the passage from:* he's not made of stone *through:* hit him in the mug. — *in “S.”:* he's not a man of stone? — just listen:

\* Page 103, line 23. *Instead of the passage from:* And the devil knows *through:* this Pan. — *in “S.”:* And I don't even know who this Pan was.

\* Page 103, line 25. *The words:* and they beat him and cursed him, — *are absent from “S.”*

\* Page 103, line 27. *The word:* scoundrel! — *is absent from “S.”*

\* Page 194, lines 28—29. *The words from:* that is, he wanted a duel *through:* one word, gentlemen! — *are absent from “S.”*

\* Page 104, line 32. *Instead of:* don't want to fight, — *in “S.”:* don't want to give me satisfaction,

\* Pages 104—105, lines 39—6. *Instead of the passage from:* persuade him so that he, that is, *through:* I don't want to, and that's that. — *in “S.”:* The prince went. The big man says: — I, he says, am not afraid of anything. I will not, he says, explain myself to a boy. I don't want to, and that's that.

\* Page 105, lines 28—31. *Instead of the passage from:* Have a drink for courage, *through:* off they drove the young fellow. — *in “S.”:* — Let's go. They went.

\* Page 106, lines 2—5. *The words from:* Nekhlyudov doesn't look like himself: *through:* and that knocked him down. — *are absent from “S.”*

Page 106, line 10. *The word:* drank, — *is absent from “S.”*

\* Page 106, lines 17—19. *The words from:* One of the officials *through:* God knows who he was, only — *are absent from “S.”*

\* Page 106, line 21. *Instead of:* God knows. — *in “S.”:* I really don't know.

\*Page 106, line 22. *Instead of:* rogue — *in “S.”:* sly one

\* Page 106, lines 23—25. *Instead of the passage from:* They had cursed him, <!-- vol. 3, p. 212 --> *through:* challenged him to a duel... — *in “S.”:* There was no end to what he got...,

\* Page 107, lines 28—30. *The words:* First a little preference, and then, before you know it, *love-me, love-me-not* starts. — *are absent from “S.”*

Page 107, lines 37—38. *Instead of the passage from:* tousled, *through:* his hands dirty. — *in “S.”:* doesn't look like himself.

\* Page 108, lines 4—5. *Instead of:* *he* won't beat my cards. — *in “S.”:* *he* won't play with me.

\* Page 109, line 17. *The words:* I, he says, will thrash you. — *are absent from “S.”*

\* Page 111, line 23. *Instead of:* ruined the peasants, — *in “S.”:* ruined the estate,

\* Page 111, line 28. *Instead of:* Ruined the peasants completely, they say. — *in “S.”:* Ruined the estate completely, they say.

\* Page 115, lines 10—11. *Instead of:* What won't gentlemen do! Gentlemen, as they say... one word: gentlemen! — *in “S.”:* Truly, what doesn't happen in the world!

\* Page 115, lines 21—28. *The words from:* It would be easier for me *through:* I know this—and cannot get up. — *are absent from “S.”*

\* Page 115, line 34. *The word:* baseness, — *is absent from “S.”*
