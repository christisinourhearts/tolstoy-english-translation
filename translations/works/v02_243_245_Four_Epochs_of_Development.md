## **UNPUBLISHED, UNFINISHED** **AND INCOMPLETE**

## II. * SECOND PLAN OF THE NOVEL *FOUR EPOCHS OF DEVELOPMENT*.

*1st page.*

## Fundamental ideas of the work.

1) To show the interesting side of the relations between brothers.—

2) Sharply mark the characteristic features of each epoch of life: in childhood warmth and fidelity of feeling; in boyhood skepticism, sensuality, self-confidence, inexperience, and (the beginning of vanity) pride; in youth beauty of feelings, development of vanity, and lack of confidence in oneself; in young manhood—eclecticism in feelings, self-love takes the place of pride and vanity, recognition of one’s own worth and purpose, many-sidedness, frankness.

3) To show the bad influence of the vanity of educators and the clash of interests in the family.—

4) Throughout the whole work to carry through the distinction between the brothers: one inclined to analysis and observation, the other to the pleasures of life.

5) To show the influence of innate inclinations on the development of characters.

6) Throughout the whole work to carry the heroes through 4 spheres—feelings, sciences, social relations, and monetary circumstances—

*2nd page.*

7) To show the impossibility of love for one woman.—

## Form of the work.

Division into 4 parts: childhood, boyhood, youth, and young manhood, in the form of the autobiography of the younger brother.

## Contents.

Persons in the 1st part: father, mother, two sons, daughter. Tutor, governess and her daughter.—In the 2nd—father, 2 sons, another <!-- vol. 2, p. 244 --> tutor, teachers, governess, her daughter, maid, comrades, attendant, persons at the evening party.—

*3rd page.*

Childhood, *(already written).* Boyhood. Mother’s death, move to Moscow, new tutor, new studies, allowance, gingerbread, loans, courting the maid, love for comrades.

Sister’s marriage and brother-in-law’s philandering. My plans on this score. K. A. patronizes us, brother enters the service. I abandon enthusiastic plans and live dissolutely, but vainly. *After the journey, return.*

## Young Manhood.

Trip to the country to Dmitry, love for his wife. Writing a discourse.

*4th page.*

## Childhood.

~~Scene~~ Description of the day spent in the country on the eve of the departure of the two brothers to the city to their grandmother. Departure with father and tutor, separation from mother, sister, governess and her daughter. Arrival in Moscow, grandmother, acquaintance with a young girl and her brother, observations of visitors.—Successes in society, reflections and conversation with brother. Mother’s death.—

## Boyhood.

Return to Moscow, dismissal of Fyodor Ivanovich. New tutor ~~the governess’s daughter becomes a bad girl, impulses of sensuality, vacations, love for stepmother, grandmother’s death~~.

## Youth.

I enter U[niversity] badly. We live in Petrovskoye alone with father,—I begin to misbehave, fall in love <!-- vol. 2, p. 245 --> brother wins her away; he does not give money, madly ambitious plans, bad occupations, loss of innocence, I do not pass to the next course, go to the country, in Moscow find an old love, live there without a kopeck, they leave, I go to the country, enthusiastic plans, brother arrives with Dmitry conversations with them. I begin not to love father and brother, in Moscow I meet the old tutor and governess, Katenka abroad refused suitors. I persuade stepmother to take Fyodor Ivanovich. He dies.—
