## TO THE TRAP.

From beneath an old building  
Amid abandoned ground  
A little mouse crawled. From agitation  
He trembled and from love.  
He burned with secret love,  
Never having loved before,  
And sought a chance love,  
As on Nevsky, gentlemen.  
Suddenly, hearing above the board  
A tender lady mouse's little voice,  
Crushed by her sorrow,  
He could not master himself.  
He dashed off and, poor fellow,  
Ran toward her voice.  
A terrible fate befell him:  
He fell into a mousetrap.  
The bolt slid shut,  
And the little mouse was locked up.  
But even there the little thief contrived  
To find consolation.  
To the lady mouse, delicately,  
He sprang and spoke thus:  
“If you should wish  
To love me in return, then I  
Will not curse fate forever,  
I’ll eat the whole candle stub with you.  
I will love you with all my heart  
In the life to come and this day.” —
