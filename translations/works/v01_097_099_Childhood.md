### VARIANTS IN THE TEXT OF *SOVREMENNIK*, 1852, No. 9

Page 3, line 8 from top. <br> *Instead of:* an image of my patron saint, — a portrait of my mama. *Changed to comply with censorship requirements.*

Page 8, line 6 from top. <br> *In the 1856 ed.:* did not notice that we had come in. — *instead of the reading in Sovremennik:* did not even notice that we had come in. *The third-redaction manuscript has the same reading as Sovremennik, which is why we have adopted the Sovremennik text.*

Page 26. <br> *In Sovremennik, Chapter VIII has the epigraph:*

Oh, rendez moi mon ignorance!

Follets, dansez, dansez, dansez.

Beranger. [^1]

Page 27, line 14 from bottom. <br> *Instead of:* If one judges by what's real, — If one judges as though by what's real.

Page 29, line 4 from top. <br> *Instead of the end of the first paragraph of Chapter X, beginning with the words:* Two chief passions — *in Sovremennik:* He was a man of ardent passions.

Page 30, line 12 from top. <br> *Instead of the end of the paragraph beginning with the words:* His nature was one — *in Sovremennik:* His view of things was always fairly flexible. His nature was one of those that need an audience. His life was so full of enthusiasms of every kind.

Page 35, line 18 from top. <br> *Instead of:* collapsed to the ground in tears!... — fell to the ground in tears.

<!-- vol. 1, p. 98 -->

Page 35. <br> *Instead of the beginning of the last paragraph of Chapter XII, ending with the words:* in the closet. — *in Sovremennik:* Trying to settle myself more comfortably, I caught my foot on a broken chair standing in the closet and made a noise.

Page 35, line 1 from bottom. <br> *Instead of:* the servant girl *Natashka.* — the servant girl Natalia.

Page 36, line 2 from top. <br> *Instead of:* the maid *Natashka* — the maid Natasha.

Page 36, lines 4–5 from top. <br> *Instead of:* entrusted to *Natashka.* — entrusted to Natalia.

Page 36. <br> *Instead of the second half of the first paragraph of Chapter XIII, beginning with the words:* But the powdered head — *in Sovremennik:* She even suppressed in her heart her love for the young footman Foka.

Page 36, line 21 from bottom. <br> *Instead of:* From then on Natashka — From then on Natasha.

Page 43, line 19 from bottom. <br> *Instead of:* I settle myself down comfortably — I settle myself down willingly.

Page 57. <br> *In Sovremennik, Chapter XIX has the epigraph:*

Honny soit qui mal у pense. [^2]

Page 62, line 14 from bottom. <br> *In the 1856 ed.:* “Eh, Sergei!” I said to him: — *instead of the reading in Sovremennik:* “Eh, Seryozha!” I said to him:. *The third-redaction manuscript has the same reading as Sovremennik, which is why we have adopted the Sovremennik text.*

Page 65, line 13 from top. <br> *Instead of:* looked intently at each other; — examined each other attentively;.

Page 72, line 22 from top. <br> *In the 1856 ed.:* I remembered the meadow — *instead of the reading in Sovremennik:* I was remembering the meadow. *The third-redaction manuscript has the same reading as Sovremennik, which is why we have adopted the Sovremennik text.*

Page 80, line 11 from top. <br> *In the 1856 ed.:* embrace and bless them: — *instead of the reading in Sovremennik:* embrace you and bless them:. *The third-redaction manuscript has the same reading as Sovremennik, which is why we have adopted the Sovremennik text.*

Page 81, line 15 from top. <br> *Instead of:* in delirium, — in a fever,.

<!-- vol. 1, p. 99 -->

Page 82, lines 13–12 from bottom. <br> *In the 1856 ed.:* “you cannot go through here — the way is through the doors.” — *instead of the reading in Sovremennik:* “you cannot go through here — the way is through the maids' room.”. *The third-redaction manuscript has the same reading as Sovremennik, which is why we have adopted the Sovremennik text.*

Page 90, line 10 from top. <br> *The end of the paragraph beginning with the words:* Before the soul — *is absent from Sovremennik.*

Page 91, line 2 from bottom. <br> *In the 1856 ed.:* ought to be prepared — *instead of the reading in Sovremennik:* had to be prepared. *The third-redaction manuscript has the same reading as Sovremennik, which is why we have adopted the Sovremennik text.*

Page 94, line 16 from top. <br> *In the 1856 ed.:* given into her full ownership. — *instead of the reading in Sovremennik:* given to her into full ownership. *The third-redaction manuscript has the same reading as Sovremennik, which is why we have adopted the Sovremennik text.*
