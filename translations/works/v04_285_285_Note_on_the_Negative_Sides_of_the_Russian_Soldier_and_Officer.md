## 1. \* [INITIAL SKETCH OF THE BEGINNING OF THE “NOTE.”]

The Russian army is enormous and was glorious, was invincible; for that very reason it is self-confident and lacking in unity; and despite the enormous size of this army Russia is in danger. —[^1]

Many understand this position of the fatherland, sympathize with it, and are ready to sacrifice property, labor, and life for it. Many, carried away by passions and by the habit of abusing the laws, hinder—by example and even by authority—people devoted to the fatherland from rendering it the benefit they could. The majority are indifferent.

The words self-sacrifice, disinterestedness, industriousness have lost their sense and meaning. The rules of honor of the old army have become barriers too high, which we have grown accustomed to passing by stooping beneath them.

A new barrier and a new impulse are needed, which would give activity to devoted people, moderate the abuses of people carried away by passion, and give direction to the indifferent as well.

## Notes

[^1]: *The original text of the first phrase was:* Russia is powerful, but it is vast, therefore: *then, instead of this phrase occupying the first line, above it was written* The Russian army... etc. *through the word* invincible; *part of the original text was crossed out, while part—namely the words:* Russia but it *remained uncrossed; we think this happened accidentally and that all these words should be crossed out, and that the only possible reading is the one given here.*
