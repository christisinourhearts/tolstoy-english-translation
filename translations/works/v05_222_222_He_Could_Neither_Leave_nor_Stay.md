## \* V. [UNTITLED FRAGMENT]

He could neither leave nor stay. He could not leave because he had no money, and because without evenings at the Pushkovs' life seemed impossible to him. Yet every evening he left them with a feeling of gnawing anguish and said to himself: squeezed dry, squeezed dry, the orange. That very orange that she had eaten in his presence. And he could not stay because so much gossip had been spun and respun around him and her, and between him and her there were relations that could only be felt, not understood. Had she refused him, or he her? Who had deceived whom[^1] <ins>relations.</ins>
