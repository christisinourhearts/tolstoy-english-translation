## II.

Long ago I forgot about happiness, —  
The dream of a forgotten soul —  
But petty passions have fallen silent  
And the voice of love has awakened......  
The stars are scattered in the sky;  
All is quiet and dark, all sleeps,  
All the lights have gone out: it is late,  
Only my candle burns.  
I sit at the window and in thought  
Follow the pictures of the past,  
But of happiness in all my life,  
I find only one minute:  
A minute of love, of hope,  
A minute without an evil thought,  
A minute without a shadow of desire,  
A minute of unearthly love...  
................................................  
&lt;The child so involuntarily said  
Its whole soul in a single look,  
That words could never have sufficed  
To say what was said in it.  
And with the sweet tremor of happiness  
I grasped its meaning;  
But words of love and sympathy  
I did not wish to say at that moment.  
Words are so insignificant in comparison  
With the divine feeling of love....  
In their dark, empty expression  
You will not find even a spark of the soul.&gt;  
And vain regret over it  
Sometimes awakens in my soul  
And says: why could you not  
Prolong that moment forever?

December 30, 1852. <br> Starogladovskaya.
