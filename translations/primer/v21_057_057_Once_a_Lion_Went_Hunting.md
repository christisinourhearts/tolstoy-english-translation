<!-- vol. 21, p. 57 -->

Once a lion went hunting and took a donkey with him. He said to him: “Donkey, go into the forest and shout as loud as you can; you have a big throat. Whatever animals start running from the noise, I'll catch.” So they did. The donkey shouted, the animals ran every which way, and the lion caught them. After the hunt the lion said to the donkey: “Well, I praise you, you shouted well.” And from then on the donkey keeps braying, always waiting to be praised.
