## THE DEER

*(A Fable)*

A deer came to a river to drink, saw himself in the water, and began admiring his antlers because they were large and branching; but he looked at his legs and says: “Only my legs are bad and thin.” Suddenly a lion jumped out and sprang at the deer. The deer set off bounding across the open field. He was getting away, but when he came into the forest, his antlers got tangled in the branches, and the lion caught him. When the deer was about to perish, he says: “What a fool I am! The ones I thought were bad and thin were saving me, and the ones I admired are what ruined me.”
