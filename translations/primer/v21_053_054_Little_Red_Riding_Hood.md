**LITTLE RED RIDING HOOD.**

A girl lived in a village. Her mother made her a red cap, and the girl always wore it. People began calling the girl Little Red Riding Hood. One day her mother said to Little Red Riding Hood: go and visit Grandmother and take her some flat cakes and a pot of butter from me. Little Red Riding Hood

<!-- vol. 21, p. 54 -->

took the treats and set out. She was walking through the forest. Suddenly a wolf came toward her in the forest. “Hello, Little Red Riding Hood?” “Hello, wolf.” “Where are you going?” “I'm going to Grandmother's. She is old and cannot walk, so I'm taking her some butter and flat cakes.” — “And where does your grandmother live?” “There, beyond the forest, in the village, in the house at the edge.” — “Well then, Little Red Riding Hood, let's see who gets there first?” And the wolf ran off; but Little Red Riding Hood began picking mushrooms and berries and forgot about the wolf. The wolf arrived before Little Red Riding Hood, found Grandmother's house, and pushed the door. Grandmother asked: “Who's there?” The wolf said: “It's me,

Grandmother, Little Red Riding Hood.” Grandmother said: “Lift the latch, granddaughter.” The wolf went in, jumped onto Grandmother's bed, devoured Grandmother, put her headscarf on his head, and lay down in bed. When Little Red Riding Hood came, she too pushed the door, and the wolf said to her: “Lift the latch, granddaughter.” Little Red Riding Hood went in, came up to the bed, and said: “Hello, Grandmother! Why are your eyes so big today?” And the wolf said: “So I can see you better, granddaughter.” — “And why are your teeth so big, Grandmother?” — “So I can bite you to death, granddaughter!..” And the wolf grabbed Little Red Riding Hood and devoured her. Then he undressed and went back into the forest.
