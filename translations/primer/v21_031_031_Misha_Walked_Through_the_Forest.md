<!-- vol. 21, p. 31 -->

Misha walked through the forest. A mushroom grew by an oak. The mushroom was old; no one picked it. But Misha was glad and carried the mushroom home. Here is a mushroom, better than all the others! But the mushroom was rotten; no one ate it.
