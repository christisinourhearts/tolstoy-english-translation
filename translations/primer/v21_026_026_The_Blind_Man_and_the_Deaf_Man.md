## THE BLIND MAN AND THE DEAF MAN

*(A True Story)*

A blind man and a deaf man went into someone else's field for peas. The deaf man said to the blind man: “You listen and tell me; I'll look and tell you.”

They went into the peas and sat down. The blind man felt the peas and said: “Full of pods.” The deaf man said: “Where's the knocking?” The blind man tripped over a boundary ridge and fell. The deaf man asked: “What happened?” The blind man said: “The ridge!” The deaf man said: “Run?” — and ran. The blind man followed him.
