## FIRE DOGS

*(A True Story)*

It often happens that when there is a fire in a city, children remain inside houses and cannot be brought out, because they hide from fear and keep quiet, and the smoke makes it impossible to see them. For this reason, dogs are trained in <!-- vol. 21, p. 110 --> London. [^1] These dogs live with the firefighters, and when a house catches fire, the firefighters send the dogs in to bring out the children. One such dog in London saved twelve children. His name was Bob.

Once a house caught fire. When the firefighters arrived at the house, a woman ran out to them. She was crying and said that a two-year-old girl was still inside. The firefighters sent Bob in. Bob ran up the stairs and disappeared into the smoke. Five minutes later he ran out of the house carrying the girl by her little shirt in his teeth. The mother rushed to her daughter and cried for joy because her daughter was alive. The firefighters petted the dog and looked him over to see whether he had been burned, but Bob struggled to go back into the house. The firefighters thought there must be something else alive inside and let him go. The dog ran into the house and soon came out with something in his teeth. When the people saw what he was carrying, they all burst out laughing: he was carrying a large doll.

## Notes

[^1]: London is the main city of the English.
