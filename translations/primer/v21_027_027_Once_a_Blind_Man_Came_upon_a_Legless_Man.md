<!-- vol. 21, p. 27 -->

\*\* Once a blind man came upon a legless man, tripped over him, and fell. The legless man says: “Didn't you see?” And the blind man says: “Are you legless?” And they began to quarrel. A peasant was passing by and says: “Instead of quarrelling, you had better join together. The blind man will carry, and the legless man will see.” So they did and began to live together.
