\*\* ~~A boy saw a beggar. He felt sorry for him. He brought him his little horse and gave it to him. — I don't need a horse. I need bread. Another peasant boy grew fond of a young gentleman and brought him some eggs. But the young gentleman says: I don't need eggs; I don't eat them. A boy lost all his knucklebones in play and cried. But a butcher gave all the knucklebones to the dogs. He did not need them. One poor man said: If someone gave me a gold coin, I would be happy. But another man was dying of hunger and found a purse with money and threw it away. A thing is good only for the person who needs it.~~
