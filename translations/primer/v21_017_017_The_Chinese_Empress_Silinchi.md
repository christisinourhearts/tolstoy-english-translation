## THE CHINESE EMPRESS SILINCHI

*(A True Story)*

The Chinese emperor Goangchi had a beloved wife, Silinchi. The emperor wanted all the people to remember his beloved empress. He showed his wife a silkworm and said: “Learn what to do with this worm and how to raise it, and the people will never forget you.”

Silinchi began watching the worms and saw that when they became still, a web formed around them. She unwound this web, spun it into thread, and wove a silk kerchief. Then she noticed that the worms lived on mulberry trees. She began gathering mulberry leaves and feeding them to the worms. She raised many worms and taught her people how to raise them.

Five thousand years have passed since then, and the Chinese still remember Empress Silinchi and celebrate in her honor.
