## 2 [March–April 1892]

The Lord’s work, however, is to manifest You in oneself and in the world.

We cannot understand life after death because it lies not only beyond our senses but also beyond our mind. The mind can define and understand only what concerns this life.

Solovyov, Urusov see nothing except their own.

1. Replenish the Filosofov storehouse.

2. Give a note to the Filosofovs’ manager for the receipt of as much rye as possible.

3. The manager offers up to 1,000 poods of beets at 18 kopecks. Set up a storehouse near Tushin in Rozhne. [^1]

[^1]: Entry in the hand of M. L. Tolstaya.
