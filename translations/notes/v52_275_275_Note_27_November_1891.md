## 1 [27 November 1891]

In memory

Zakhar Ryab.

St. Ranen

School

Danila Lavnikov.

Family love did not interfere with public love. Slavophilism without stupidities.

They say peasant women bake better.

Priest's wife, boy.

Filiation of dining rooms.

One must know that there are such people. Others live more cheerfully.

Is it not disadvantageous to look at them as at everyone? And I looked that way.

His people—servants.

Early rose. [^1]

In the heat he said: How the affair got settled. It had to, after all.
