## [NOTE, 1884]

6 June 1884.

Anna Ivanovna Esipova. Smallholder from the village of Trosna. Widow, 7 children. One hand does not work. Ran around the yard and around her son, a cabman. They took her to the madhouse; ordered that a wolf be buried.

Akulina from Kolpenka. For taxes on the pledged crop, 3—6 rubles.

Peasants about the bank.

Andrei Vasilyev Antonov. Clerk — put in police custody — by the Zotovs. They do not give him the money he earned.

Ivan Samsonov from Gorodnya killed through negligence — ask the prosecutor.
