## [NOTEBOOK No. 5, 1866–1871]

Pierre is tormented by fear of death before the duel.

N. pretends that he wants [illegible: 1 word]—His company pretend to one another.—

Andrei is already a Mason and religious. Pierre turns him toward the activity of self-sacrifice.

At the Rostovs' the rooms are packed with people. She wants to fly.

*Vestnik Evropy*. 12.

On the reform of '09.

Rastopchin in '62.

[Mid-November 1866]

Pictures, balls.

Gotthelf book.

Manuscript to Bashilov.

Money to the engravers.

Fish oil—Ferein.

Museum correspondence.

Natasha says to herself—Now everyone says about me: what grace! What a voice! What a lovely face! And indeed I know that I have a lovely face, and it is true.—

Pierre a Mason. In Moscow they give to Andrei.

N. Rostov before Friedland makes a brilliant attack. He overcame himself and is brave. He won, but no one knows about it. In the regiment he is respected, but at headquarters they have not even heard of it.

Natasha prepares for Communion and prays at the ektenia.—Well, and for the Synod.—

Money [1 illegible]

Pierre says to Natasha: You do not know what you have done to him—he lived for you, he is proud, and he will forgive neither him, Anatole, nor you, nor himself. He is ruined.—Natasha: I understood—cries like children.

At the ball Natasha sees that Andrei is insolent with another woman, and suddenly approaches her tenderly.—

—I tried whether I can live without you—no, I cannot.

“170 rubles were stolen from me,” says the thief and cries. Napoleon says: I gave away my diamonds and pearls—see *The Sovereign*.

Hearts—comb them, pomade them.—
