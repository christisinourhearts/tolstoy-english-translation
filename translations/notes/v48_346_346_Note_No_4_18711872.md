### No. 4
1871 or 1872.

First spring.

No roads — grass in the low places like needles. — Lapwings. Noise of streams. Birds. Peasant women, boys barefoot, feet white. —

Orion and Sirius are setting.

Summer, June.

Blue — sultry — thunderstorms. Shoots on the trees — dews. Flowers everywhere. Yellow fields of Turkish rocket. Blue forget-me-nots. Birds, nightingales. People, songs. — The rye is waving, only just beginning. Berries, mushrooms. — Hay is heating. Buckwheat is spreading broad leaves.

Manure smells. Roads are rolled into the fields.

Crowdedness in the forest. Hum of bees. Swarms are running. The linden is weighed down with blossom. Dog rose. Low parts of the meadow; gray uncut panicles stand.

Beginning of August.

Roads are rolled down by grain.

Late hay in rows, marsh scents.

Blue in the distance.

Nights secret, starry.

No birds — silence.

Porcini mushrooms.

Bees on burdocks.

Fruit on the linden.

Smell of apple.

Smoke thick, fragrant.

Thickness and heaviness in everything.

Stacks, unfinished in the threshing yards.

Water steely and quiet, dense.
