## [NOTE ON A SHEET, 6 DECEMBER 1881]

Set off from one shore, did not reach the other.

Like a quail under a hawk, does not raise its head.

Like two different wheels, apart.

The hens will scratch it over.

6 December 1881.

All the evil in the world comes from the fact that people do not know what God is, and worship as God what is not God. —

Revolutionaries are a specialty, a profession, like any other — like the military profession (the analogy is complete). It is a mistake to think that it is higher than the others. —
