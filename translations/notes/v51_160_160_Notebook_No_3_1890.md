## [NOTEBOOK NO. 3, 1890]

## [ENTRIES OF 5—6 AUGUST 1890]

5 Aug. 90. We shall be forgiven only when we forgive, i.e. there will be no consequences. When we love.[^1]

You feel yourself, your personality, as long as you do not let God through. Let God through—and you will receive a new consciousness.

How can one know the will of God? Everything that is spiritually painful, burdensome, is not God's will.

For *Father Sergius*. She left, and he is in despair over his own badness.

She expects something majestic, and suddenly he is simple, embarrassed, runs his fingers through his beard, but the eyes... That is what inflames her.[^2]

6. The more immoral, the higher the demands put forward. — Holy foolishness, do not care about opinion.[^3]

One must care about a bad opinion.[^4]
