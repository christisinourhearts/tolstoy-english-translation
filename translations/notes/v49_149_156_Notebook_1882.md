Tsvetkov’s horse was found. From Prishni. They will not give it to him.

In weather so fair  
All Tata’s children there in Yasnaya [^2]  
Lived, making merry.  
Suddenly it came to Tatyana’s mind  
That in Yasnaya, in Polyana,  
One cannot live forever.  
And Tatyana thought:  
Sooner or later  
The children need a certificate.  
A certificate. I’ll give them that thing,  
Advance the children into learning, [^3] <sup></sup>  
They’ll be learned.  
She laid in a curriculum,  
Hired a madame for the children,  
Began to teach.  
The children learned the tricks,  
All about the different sciences,  
Very well. [^4]  
And they studied without sorrow,  
Until they were taught [^5]  
God’s Law.  
But when they began to teach it, [^6] <sup></sup>  
The mother began to notice  
Something was not right.  
Masha cannot even grasp it,

<!-- vol. 49, p. 153 -->

And Vera keeps on wailing:  
It is very difficult.  
And it happened, while they were going over  
The expulsion from Paradise,  
Vera says: [^7]  
We are told to study the Law,  
How God drove Adam out  
Together with Eve.  
For why was Adam expelled?  
Madame herself says —  
For *curiosité*. [^8]  
That he ate the apple,  
That would be nothing.  
And it is offensive to learn this,  
Because it is plainly clear  
*que ce n’est pas vrai*. [^9]  
If they were driven from the garden,  
Then we need not study at all,  
And here is why.  
Everyone may eat apples,  
But one must not listen too much,  
Lest there be harm.  
They were driven out precisely because  
They learned too much,  
And God did not want it.  
So to go against God  
I would need a great deal of courage,  
*J’aime à obéir*. [^10]

<!-- vol. 49, p. 154 -->

In weather so fair  
All in Yasnaya lived happily,  
Lived, making merry.  
Suddenly it came to Tatyana’s mind  
That in Yasnaya, in Polyana,  
One cannot live forever.  
Tatyana says to herself:  
Sooner or later  
The children need a certificate.  
I’ll send the girls into learning,  
Advance them into every sort of thing,  
They’ll be mademoiselles. [^11]  
They bought books, notebooks,  
Whether the girls were glad or not,  
They began to teach.  
And they studied without sorrow, [^12] <sup></sup>  
But when they began God’s Law,  
Things did not go.  
Masha simply cannot grasp it,  
And Vera already wails aloud:  
I do not like the Law.  
And one day [^13] [?], while going over  
The meaning of the expulsion from Paradise,  
Vera says:  
We are told to study the Law,  
How God drove Adam out  
Together with Eve.  
And it is offensive to learn this,  
Because it is plainly clear  
That [^14] one must not know.  
For why was Adam expelled?  
Madame herself says —  
For *curiosité*.

<!-- vol. 49, p. 155 -->

[^15] They had learned too much,  
They were kicked out by the scruff for it,  
I do not want that.  
And now the mother does not know  
What to answer to this.  
Indeed, it is difficult.  
They scold Masha and me  
And will not let us go to Vasily  
To shake apples.  
But in Paradise it was not so,  
Nothing was locked,  
Eat as much as you like.  
And there was every sort, no [unclear: joke],  
There was Arkad, there was [Grushovka?],  
There was every sort of rabble.
