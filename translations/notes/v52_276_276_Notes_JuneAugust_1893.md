## 3 [June — August 1893]

Only those who do not understand art spread the fame of quasi artists-imitators: Shakespeare, Wagner, Raphael, in whom the chief thing cannot be seen: the creator’s soul.

A pleiad of Americans.

Medicine. <sup>1</sup> / <sub>1000</sub> of [illegible: 1 word] are known and act against it.

Vasilievsky Island, 3rd Line, house 36, apartment 3. Alexander Modestovich.

1) To Leontiev

2) To Grot

3) To Schroeder
