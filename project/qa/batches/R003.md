### R003.02–03 method note

The February and March blocks were drafted in parallel by nine translator passes working from one written brief (`project/qa/batches/R003_BRIEF.md`), each checked mechanically (block count, page markers, line and quote structure, italics, punctuation, spelling) and then reviewed against the Russian. Book-wide conventions settled during the review: «Иосиф Мадзини» = "Giuseppe Mazzini"; «магометане» = "Muslims" (as on the website); a period after a quoted title goes inside the quotes (“Pious Thoughts.”); one-letter transcription slips are translated in their evident sense and recorded in `project/qa/source_suspected/`.
