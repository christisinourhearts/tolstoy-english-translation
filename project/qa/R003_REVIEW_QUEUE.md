## 2. Conventions to confirm (book-wide)

- [ ] «воспитание» = "upbringing" where Tolstoy sets it against «образование» ("education"); elsewhere sometimes "education". Weekly reading 18 is titled "Upbringing" on the site (was "Education").
- [ ] «начало» = "source" (Gospel in Brief rule) vs "principle": batches differ ("divine source" / "divine principle", "spiritual principle"). Pick one book-wide rule for «божественное/духовное начало».
- [ ] «господь» = capitalized "Lord"; pronouns for God/Christ lowercase as in the Russian; «отец» lowercase "father" in Gospel quotations.
- [ ] Attribution labels: "*The Talmud.*", "*The Quran.*", "*The Laws of Manu.*" take "The"; "*Dhammapada.*", "*Indian Kural.*", "*Chinese wisdom.*" do not. A few mixed cases ("*The Chinese Shijing.*", "*The Indian Agni Purana.*" vs "*Indian Bhagavata.*").
- [ ] Attributions keep the printed form (surname only where Tolstoy prints only a surname; no added final period where the print has none). The website keeps its own display form for attribution lines (full names).
- [ ] «магометане» = "Muslims" (period term "Mohammedans" not used); «Магомет» = "Muhammad".
- [ ] Place names: historical "Kiev", "Vilna"; Polish names in Polish spelling in *What For?* (Józef Migurski, Rosołowski with ł).
- [ ] Dialogue: Russian dash convention kept ("— Well, — said Korney, — come on.") rather than English quotation marks.
- [ ] Weekly titles changed on the site to match the translation: "Perfecting" (was "Self-Perfection"), "The Buddha", "The Archangel Gabriel", "The Surat Coffeehouse", "The Street Vendor", "A Grain as Big as a Hen's Egg", "Upbringing", "Must It Be So?", "First Sorrow", "Voluntary Servitude", "The Order of the World", "The Unbeliever", "Why Do Men Stupefy Themselves?", "Petr Chelčický", "What For?", "Living Relics", "The Divine and the Human", "The Demands of Love".
