# Names and Forms of Address Ledger

Record recurring transliteration, conventional English names, titles, diminutives, and forms of address here when consistency across files matters.

General rule (D0003): Russian ё is rendered *yo* even when the source prints е (Федор → Fyodor, Петр → Pyotr, Семен → Semyon); word-initial Е → *Ye*.
