| хозяин | master | husband (colloquial, of a peasant woman's man); host; householder | *Master and Man* (v29_003_046): kept as “master” throughout, including “the chief Master” for God in ch. VII, because the repetition carries the title and argument. The cook's хозяин in ch. I is her husband (“your man”). |
| работник | workman | hired man, laborer | *Master and Man*: “workman”; the title pairing is rendered “Master and Man” as the established English title. |
| разумение | understanding | — | *The Gospel in Brief* (v24_801_938): Tolstoy's rendering of the Logos; always “understanding” (разумение жизни = “the understanding of life”, сын разумения = “son of understanding”, свет разумения = “light of understanding”). Ordinary понимание/понять also take “understand”; where both meet (поняли разумение) понять is “comprehend”. See D0002. |
| начало | source | beginning (temporal sense only) | *Gospel in Brief*: начало жизни = “the source of life”, бесконечное начало = “infinite source”, John I, 1 «в основу и начало всего» = “the foundation and source of all”; ch. III title НАЧАЛО РАЗУМЕНИЯ = “The Source of Understanding”. |
| благо | the good | welfare (avoided) | *Gospel in Brief*: возвещение о благе = “the proclamation of the good” (Tolstoy's gloss on “gospel”); истинное благо = “the true good”. добро is also “good”; the distinction is usually carried by the article (“the good” vs “good”). |
| жизнь истинная / ложная | true life / false life | real life (for жизнь настоящая) | *Gospel in Brief*: истинная = “true”, настоящая = “real”, keeping Tolstoy's pun on настоящее (“the present”) recoverable. |
| сын человеческий | son of man | — | *Gospel in Brief*: lowercase as in the source; likewise сын Бога = “son of God”, отец = “father”, святой дух = “holy spirit” following source capitalization. |

| воля отца | the will of the father | — | Lowercase “father” where the source has отец; “Father” only where the source capitalizes. |
