## Required preflight

Before translating any P003 unit, run `tools/preflight_boundaries.py` against the authoritative Russian source repository or ZIP. Any P003 path flagged MEDIUM/HIGH must be inspected against its neighboring source/page and, when necessary, the printed/electronic 90-volume edition before translation. A flagged unit may be replaced without changing the 50-unit target.

The preflight is a suspicion detector, not a proof of corruption; intentional fragments must remain eligible when their source state is verified.
