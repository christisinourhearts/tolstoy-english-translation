## 1. Two Brothers and Gold — PASS

Source: `corpus/works/v25_028_030_Dva_brata_i_zoloto.md`

Difficulties encountered:
- The audited Markdown reads `жили в горè` in the opening sentence. A cross-check against another Russian collected-text witness gives `жили на горе`, which also agrees with the later geography of the story. The English therefore uses “lived on a hill.” This is a possible source-layer transcription/conversion defect and should be reported upstream rather than silently changing the Russian repository.
- `беремени на два` is archaic; rendered conservatively as “enough for two loads.”

Substantive fidelity corrections during audit:
- Changed “mountain” to “hill” throughout for consistency of the repeated `гора` imagery and the opening witness noted above.
- Removed an unnecessary possessive from “only with one's labor”; the Russian says simply `только трудом`.

English edit:
- Kept Tolstoy's repeated “Athanasius” and repeated sentence structures rather than varying them decoratively.
- Retained the deliberately plain moral-fable register.

Unresolved textual questions: none blocking acceptance. The opening Russian-source discrepancy is an upstream QA item.

Apparatus: not applicable.

Final source audit: PASS.
Mechanical validation: PASS.

## 2. Childhood — Sovremennik textual variants — PASS

Source: `corpus/works/v01_097_099_Detstvo_Varianty_teksta_Sovremennika_1852_g_No_9.md`

Difficulties encountered:
- This `works` file is not a continuous Tolstoy work. It is overwhelmingly scholarly/editorial variant apparatus containing small snippets of Tolstoy text. The current manifest's simple body/apparatus distinction does not adequately model this case.
- Several entries exist specifically to record very small wording differences. A smooth translation can accidentally erase the difference being documented.
- French epigraphs belong to the textual witness and therefore remain in French; the second source footnote is translated, while the first remains blank because the source note is blank.

Substantive fidelity corrections during audit:
- `образок моего ангела` revised from “icon of my guardian angel” to “image of my patron saint,” avoiding an incorrect identification of the Orthodox name-saint image with a guardian angel.
- `Ежели судить по настоящему` / `как по настоящему` was initially flattened into “properly”; revised so the contrast around reality/realism remains visible.
- `повалился` / `пал` initially collapsed to the same English verb; revised to “collapsed” / “fell” so the variant remains meaningful.
- `всматривались` / `рассматривались` revised to preserve the distinction between looking intently and examining.
- `надо бы приготовить` / `надо было приготовить` revised to “ought to be prepared” / “had to be prepared,” preserving modality and passive structure.

English edit:
- Editorial formulae were standardized in clear English while retaining references to pages, lines, editions, manuscript redactions, and `Sovremennik`.
- Intentionally awkward micro-variants were not normalized away.

Unresolved textual questions:
- The foreign epigraph `Honny soit qui mal у pense` contains nonstandard spelling/character forms in the source. It is preserved rather than silently emended.

Apparatus: translated, but this unit demonstrates that `apparatus_translation_status` needs a richer model for files whose entire content is editorial apparatus.

Final source audit: PASS.
Mechanical validation: PASS.
